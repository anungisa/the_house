## V2-28.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines product ownership, decision rights, and service accountability for the
affiliation product (REG-201 OUT-V2-023; REG-203 CAP-V2-045, FR-V2-045). Authority derives
from an accountable function, not from visibility in a workspace. Visibility in a workspace
must not imply authority (REG-203 BR-V2-032, RULE-V2-031).

## V2-28.2 Governance domains and accountable authorities

This section is normative.

Each governance domain maps to an accountable authority. The following domains are defined:

- Product definition maps to Product authority (REG-202 STK-V2-019).
- National strategy maps to Strategic authority (REG-202 STK-V2-020).
- Affiliation policy maps to Policy and compliance authority (REG-202 STK-V2-015).
- Pathway eligibility maps to Policy authority (REG-202 STK-V2-015).
- Operational execution maps to Service owner (REG-202 STK-V2-021).
- Product and technology consistency maps to Technology authority (REG-202 STK-V2-022).
- Financial policy and reconciliation maps to Financial authority (REG-202 STK-V2-013).
- Privacy maps to Privacy authority (REG-202 STK-V2-018).
- Bilingual content maps to Official-languages and content authority (REG-202 STK-V2-016).
- Accessibility maps to Accessibility authority (REG-202 STK-V2-017).
- Data authority maps to Domain data owner (REG-202 STK-V2-023).
- Support operations maps to Support owner (REG-202 STK-V2-024).
- Material organizational commitment maps to Executive authority (REG-202 STK-V2-007).

## V2-28.3 Decision-domain record structure

This section is normative.

Each decision domain is defined through a controlled record with the following fields:

- Decision type
- Accountable authority
- Consulted functions
- Evidence required
- Delegation rules
- Escalation route
- Change classification
- Approval status
- Future blocking gate

## V2-28.5 Visibility is not authority

This section is normative.

Access to, or visibility of, a workspace, register, or artifact does not confer decision
authority. Governed decisions are valid only when exercised by the accountable function with
the required evidence (REG-203 RULE-V2-031, CTRL-V2-022). No Button surface holds governed
authority (REG-203 BR-V2-032).

## V2-28.6 Staffing and named-role posture

This section is normative.

This chapter defines functional accountability, not permanent staffing. Named-role and
permanent-staffing confirmation remain pending organizational approval; the accountability
model does not assign final permanent staffing where organizational approval has not
occurred. Each accountable function carries validation_status: stakeholder_validation_pending
in REG-202.

## V2-28.7 Authorization posture

This section is normative.

This chapter defines accountability and decision rights only. It authorizes no
implementation, no procurement, no technical architecture, no delivery plan, no staffing
plan, no cost plan, and no master development plan. Executive organizational acceptance
remains pending at the material-commitment gate.
