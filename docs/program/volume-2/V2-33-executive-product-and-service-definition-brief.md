## V2-33.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter is the executive product-and-service definition brief for Volume 2 (REG-201
OUT-V2-022; REG-203 CAP-V2-050, BR-V2-035). It summarises the ratified affiliation product
definition for executive audiences. It is a summary of ratified product definition and must
not become a new source of authority (REG-203 BR-V2-035).

## V2-33.2 What Volume 2 defined

This section is normative.

Volume 2 defined the affiliation product and its service: scope and offer, recognition and
pathways, journeys and exceptions, requirements and acceptance, policy and rules, the service
experience, the operations and control model, product ownership and decision rights, service
measures, lifecycle and change governance, integrated traceability, and the validation
backlog carried into Volume 3.

## V2-33.3 What The House is

This section is normative.

The House is the governed system-of-record and platform core. It owns identity, tenancy,
permissions, lifecycle state, audit, evidence, consent, workflow, idempotency, and durable
data. The Governance Kernel is the sole authority for governed lifecycle transitions.

## V2-33.4 What The Button is

This section is normative.

The Button is a stakeholder and public experience layer. It presents governed status and
requests governed transitions. It never owns governed lifecycle rules and never holds governed
authority.

## V2-33.5 Why affiliation is the first service

This section is normative.

Affiliation is the foundational annual relationship between the national organization, member
organizations, and clubs. It is the first service because every downstream capability depends
on correct, governed club recognition and affiliation.

## V2-33.6 The three affiliation pathways

This section is normative.

The affiliation product recognizes three pathways: continuity confirmation for a continuing
club, renewal with remediation where requirements must be brought current, and new affiliation
for a club not previously affiliated. Pathway determination and seasonal applicability are
governed.

## V2-33.7 What the product includes

This section is normative.

The product includes guided affiliation, governed recognition and identity, pathway and
applicability determination, evidence and submission integrity, governed review and decision,
fee and reconciliation visibility, exactly-once activation, and a bilingual, accessible,
privacy-aware experience with support recovery, all under append-only audit and immutable
evidence.

## V2-33.8 What it excludes

This section is normative.

The product excludes technical architecture, delivery sequencing, staffing plans, cost plans,
a master development plan, detailed screen layouts, and any capability not required for
governed affiliation. It excludes implementation and procurement authorization.

## V2-33.9 How governed authority is protected

This section is normative.

Governed authority is protected by the House and Button boundary, by decision rights that
derive from accountable functions rather than workspace visibility, by fail-closed handling of
unknown transitions, guards, and permissions, and by the rule that no requirement authorizes
implementation and no Button surface holds governed authority.

## V2-33.10 What the service experience provides

This section is normative.

The service experience provides a guided, unambiguous affiliation task flow with clear status
and required actions, role-based workspaces, completeness-aware forms, communication and
support recovery, and bilingual, accessible, privacy-aware task equivalence.

## V2-33.11 What remains pending validation

This section is normative.

Pending validation includes policy confirmation (pathway eligibility, evidence carry-forward,
retention), financial confirmation (fee policy, payment and reconciliation contract, accounting
boundary), bilingual, accessibility, and privacy conformance, service-level baselines and
operating-model decisions, and executive organizational acceptance at the material-commitment
gate. The validation backlog (V2-32) owns and gates each item.

## V2-33.12 What Volume 3 is authorized to define

This section is normative.

Volume 3 is authorized to define the business operating model for affiliation: the operating
model, roles, and processes that deliver the ratified product, and the resolution paths for the
validation backlog. Volume 3 is definition work; it does not authorize implementation or
procurement.

## V2-33.13 What remains unauthorized

This section is normative.

Implementation, procurement, technical architecture, delivery sequencing, staffing plans, cost
plans, and the master development plan remain unauthorized. This brief adds no new authority.
