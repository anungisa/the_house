## V2-24.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines product requirements for a bilingual, accessible, inclusive, and
privacy-aware affiliation service experience (REG-203 CAP-V2-043, FR-V2-041, FR-V2-042,
FR-V2-043).

## V2-24.2 Bilingual parity

This section is normative.

The service defines: English and French parity; language persistence; and equivalent
status and error meaning across both official languages, using plain-language terminology
(REG-203 NFR-V2-018, CTRL-V2-017). Owner: Bilingual Experience and Official-Languages
Authority (STK-V2-016).

## V2-24.3 Accessibility

This section is normative.

The service defines: keyboard access; focus behaviour; semantic structure; screen-reader
interpretation; accessible evidence upload; accessible error correction; and
colour-independent status communication (REG-203 NFR-V2-019, CTRL-V2-018). Owner:
Accessibility and Inclusive-Design Authority (STK-V2-017).

## V2-24.4 Recovery and resilience

This section is normative.

The service defines timeout and session-recovery behaviour and low-bandwidth and
interrupted-session recovery, preserving user progress and the governed record (REG-203
NFR-V2-021, CTRL-V2-020).

## V2-24.5 Privacy

This section is normative.

The service defines: privacy notices; restricted evidence; minimum-necessary disclosure;
and representative and reviewer visibility boundaries (REG-203 NFR-V2-020, CTRL-V2-019).
Owner: Privacy and Data-Protection Authority (STK-V2-018).

## V2-24.6 Validation-pending conformance

This section is normative.

Conformance levels that are not yet formally approved remain classified as validation
pending rather than assumed. Accessibility conformance is
ACCESSIBILITY_VALIDATION_PENDING; bilingual conformance is BILINGUAL_VALIDATION_PENDING;
privacy conformance is PRIVACY_VALIDATION_PENDING. No conformance claim is asserted as
validated. Pending validation blocks only the affected requirement.

## V2-24.7 Authorization posture

This section is normative.

This chapter defines bilingual, accessibility, inclusive, and privacy requirements only. It
authorizes no implementation, no conformance certification, and no technical architecture.
All referenced requirements in REG-203 carry `authorizes_implementation: false`.
