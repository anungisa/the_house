## V2-27.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter establishes the single integrated affiliation product-service definition
baseline for Volume 2. It consolidates every ratified package into one authoritative
description of the affiliation product and its service so that Volume 3 receives a complete,
unambiguous input (REG-201 OUT-V2-022; REG-203 CAP-V2-044, FR-V2-044). This chapter is a
consolidation. It must not introduce a new governed decision merely to make the
consolidation appear complete (REG-203 BR-V2-031, RULE-V2-030).

## V2-27.2 Inherited package baselines

This section is normative.

The integrated baseline is assembled on the frozen work of Packages 1 through 4:

- Package 1 (V2-01 through V2-05, closed V2-A) established product definition, stakeholder
  and outcome model, House and Button product boundary, service blueprint, and the
  requirement and acceptance architecture.
- Package 2 (V2-06 through V2-11, closed V2-B) established affiliation scope, recognition
  and pathways, journeys and exceptions, the capability and requirement catalogue, the
  experience and support model, and the product-measure and acceptance baseline.
- Package 3 (V2-12 through V2-18, closed V2-C) established policy, terminology and authority,
  pathway and seasonal applicability, evidence and submission rules, review, decision and
  escalation, fee, payment, reconciliation and activation rules, the operations and control
  model, and rule-coverage and acceptance decision tables.
- Package 4 (V2-19 through V2-26, closed V2-D) established the detailed service blueprint,
  role-based workspaces, the status and required-action model, forms and evidence
  interaction, communication and support experience, bilingual, accessible and privacy-aware
  definition, operational acceptance scenarios, and downstream constraints.

The integrated baseline inherits this content unchanged and does not reopen it.

## V2-27.3 Integrated capability record structure

This section is normative.

The integrated baseline expresses each material capability through a controlled
consolidation record with the following fields:

- Capability
- Product outcome
- Primary personas
- House responsibility
- Button responsibility
- External dependency
- Controlling rules
- Experience commitment
- Acceptance coverage
- Validation status
- Unresolved condition

Each field references ratified Volume 2 content. Where a field has no established content,
the record states the unresolved condition and its future blocking gate rather than
inventing a new commitment.

## V2-27.5 Governed authority preserved

This section is normative.

The integrated baseline preserves the House and Button product boundary (V2-03). The Button
presents governed status and requests governed transitions; it never becomes the authority
for lifecycle state. The Governance Kernel remains the sole authority for governed
transitions (REG-203 RULE-V2-031).

## V2-27.6 Authorization posture

This section is normative.

This chapter defines a consolidated product baseline only. It authorizes no implementation,
no procurement, no technical architecture, no delivery sequencing, no staffing plan, no cost
plan, and no master development plan. Every requirement it consolidates carries
authorizes_implementation: false. Executive organizational acceptance remains pending at the
material-commitment gate.
