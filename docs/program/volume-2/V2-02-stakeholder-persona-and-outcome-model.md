## V2-02.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter normalizes the stakeholders and personas of the affiliation service into a
single model (REG-202) and links their outcomes (REG-201) to the product boundary. It
distinguishes stakeholders that hold **governed authority** (act within The House on the
authoritative record) from those served **through the guided experience** (The Button).

## V2-02.3 Personas

This section is normative.

Personas are recorded per stakeholder in REG-202. The primary Package 1 personas are:

- **Affiliation applicant** (Club Administrator) - prepares and submits an application
  and responds to required actions through The Button.
- **Affiliation reviewer** (NSO Membership/Governance Officer) - evaluates applications
  against policy and records governed decisions with evidence in The House.
- **Regional oversight** (Member Organization) - monitors affiliation status across
  clubs in its jurisdiction.
- **Status recipient** (Participant/Member) - receives status and communication.
- **Executive acceptance authority** - accepts material organizational commitment at a
  later gate.

## V2-02.4 Outcome model

This section is normative.

Outcomes (REG-201) express the value each stakeholder must realize and are linked to the
product boundary and to stakeholders. Each outcome carries a validation status.
Outcomes asserted by the author on inherited Volume 1 evidence are marked
`author_asserted`; outcomes requiring stakeholder confirmation are marked
`stakeholder_validation_pending`. No outcome is recorded as `stakeholder_validated`
in Package 1, because independent stakeholder validation has not yet occurred.

## V2-02.5 Unresolved validations

This section is normative.

The following outcomes and stakeholder characterizations require stakeholder validation
and are recorded as pending, not fabricated: OUT-V2-001, OUT-V2-004, OUT-V2-006,
OUT-V2-007, and the member-organization, club, administrator, participant, and
external-system stakeholder records. These are surfaced to Gate V2-G1 condition 8 and
carried forward for validation in a later package. Recording pending validation is a
required control; asserting unearned validation is prohibited.
