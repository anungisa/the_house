## V2-13.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines how a club enters one of the three governed pathways established in
V2-07 - continuity confirmation, renewal with remediation, and new affiliation - and how
requirements are made applicable by season, jurisdiction, pathway, and club
classification. Pathway is determined by governed inputs, not by applicant self-selection
(REG-203 BR-V2-015, RULE-V2-010). This chapter is rule definition and authorizes no
implementation.

## V2-13.2 Pathway determination

This section is normative.

The pathway determination rule (RULE-V2-010) evaluates governed inputs - historical
recognition, organization continuity, standing, evidence condition, jurisdiction, and
material organizational change - and yields exactly one outcome: continuity, renewal with
remediation, new affiliation, or manual policy determination where inputs are ambiguous.
Manual policy determination is a governed exception, not a default. Detailed eligibility
thresholds are recorded as POLICY_VALIDATION_PENDING pending validation by the Pathway and
Evidence Policy Authority (Jen).

## V2-13.3 Continuity confirmation

This section is normative.

Continuity eligibility (RULE-V2-011) requires all of: historical recognition;
satisfactory standing; unchanged organization identity; valid jurisdiction; current
authorized representatives; and evidence eligible for carry-forward. Where any current-season
confirmation is outstanding, the club completes only the confirmation. Conditions that
force remediation include missing or expired carry-forward evidence, a change in identity
or jurisdiction, unsatisfactory standing, or an open compliance exception.

## V2-13.4 Renewal with remediation

This section is normative.

The remediation trigger rule (RULE-V2-012) routes a club to renewal with remediation when
there is missing evidence, expired evidence, contradictory records, changed
representatives, changed jurisdiction, outstanding fees, or compliance exceptions.
Temporary continuation while remediation is underway is permitted only where policy allows
and is itself a governed state. Escalation thresholds determine when unresolved
remediation is escalated; these thresholds are POLICY_VALIDATION_PENDING.

## V2-13.5 New affiliation

This section is normative.

The new-affiliation determination rule (RULE-V2-013) applies when there is an absence of
historical recognition, material organizational change, or a new legal or operating
identity. New affiliation requires jurisdiction determination, a complete requirement
application, satisfied decision prerequisites, and initial activation under V2-16.

## V2-13.6 Requirement applicability and versioning

This section is normative.

The requirement applicability rule (RULE-V2-014) resolves the applicable requirement set,
evidence rules, fee rules, and reviewer authority from governed inputs. Requirements are
versioned by:

- season;
- jurisdiction;
- pathway;
- club classification;
- requirement version;
- effective date;
- expiry date;
- policy authority.

A decision is always evaluated against the requirement version in force at the applicable
time (REG-203 NFR-V2-014). Requirement content and thresholds remain subject to policy
validation and are recorded as pending where not settled.
