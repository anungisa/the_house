## V2-26.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates the Package 4 affiliation experience definition and states the
downstream constraints that remain outside Volume 2 (REG-203 FR-V2-036).

## V2-26.2 Consolidated experience definition

This section is normative.

Package 4 defines: pathway-specific service blueprints (V2-19); role-based workspaces and a
task model (V2-20); a separated status, state, and required-action model (V2-21); forms and
evidence interaction (V2-22); communication, notification, and support behaviour (V2-23);
bilingual, accessible, inclusive, and privacy-aware requirements (V2-24); and operational
acceptance scenarios and a service-quality baseline (V2-25).

## V2-26.3 Governed authority preserved

This section is normative.

The affiliation experience presents governed status and requests governed transitions; it
never becomes the authority for lifecycle state. The Governance Kernel remains the sole
authority, and The Button remains a non-authoritative experience layer (REG-203 BR-V2-024,
BR-V2-027, RULE-V2-027).

## V2-26.4 Downstream constraints (out of scope)

This section is normative.

- database design;
- event schemas;
- infrastructure and platform selection;
