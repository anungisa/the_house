## V2-07.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines how a club is recognized and how it reaches an active affiliation.
It defines three governed affiliation pathways inherited from the Volume 1 target
operating model (V1-24). It defines the pathways as a product; it does not define the
detailed eligibility rules, which remain subject to policy and operational validation.

## V2-07.2 Recognition and affiliation distinction

This section is normative.

Recognition establishes that an organization is a known club within the NSO ecosystem.
Affiliation establishes that a recognized club is affiliated for a specific season.
Recognition is a precondition for affiliation. A club may be recognized without holding
a current affiliation, and affiliation is always seasonal.

## V2-07.3 Pathway A - Continuity confirmation

This section is normative.

Continuity confirmation applies to a recognized club in good standing that affiliated in
the prior season and seeks to continue. The pathway steps are:

1. resolve the club and its jurisdiction;
2. open seasonal affiliation for the current season;
3. confirm that representatives and core club information remain current;
4. apply the season's versioned requirements and identify any changed requirements;
5. collect only the evidence that has changed or expired;
6. submit for confirmation;
7. reviewer confirms continuity and records the decision; and
8. activate the affiliation for the season.

Continuity confirmation is the lowest-friction pathway and presumes no unresolved
compliance conditions.

## V2-07.4 Pathway B - Renewal with remediation

This section is normative.

Renewal with remediation applies to a previously affiliated club whose affiliation
lapsed or that carries outstanding conditions. The pathway steps are:

1. resolve the club and its jurisdiction;
2. open seasonal affiliation for the current season;
3. determine outstanding conditions and remediation requirements;
4. confirm representatives and update club information;
5. apply the season's versioned requirements plus remediation items;
6. collect and bind the required and remediating evidence;
7. submit for review;
8. reviewer evaluates remediation and records the decision, returning for information
   where remediation is incomplete; and
9. activate the affiliation once conditions are satisfied.

## V2-07.5 Pathway C - New affiliation

This section is normative.

New affiliation applies to an organization not previously recognized or affiliated. The
pathway steps are:

1. capture the organization's recognition request and identity;
2. resolve or assign jurisdiction;
3. establish recognition of the club as a governed entity;
4. confirm authorized representatives;
5. open seasonal affiliation for the current season;
6. apply the full season requirement set;
7. collect and bind all required evidence;
8. submit for review;
9. reviewer evaluates recognition and affiliation and records the decision, returning
   for information where required; and
10. activate the affiliation once recognition and affiliation conditions are satisfied.

## V2-07.6 Pathway determination

This section is normative.

The service determines the applicable pathway from the club's recognition and prior
affiliation status and any outstanding conditions. Pathway determination is a governed
capability (see REG-203) and is auditable. Where the determination is ambiguous, the
service routes to review rather than presuming a pathway.

## V2-07.7 Eligibility remains subject to validation

This section is normative.

The eligibility rules that govern each pathway - what qualifies a club for continuity,
what remediation is sufficient, and what recognition requires - remain subject to policy
and operational validation and are owned outside this package. This chapter defines the
product's obligation to support the three pathways and to apply versioned requirements;
it does not fabricate the eligibility rules themselves. Unresolved eligibility questions
are recorded in V2-B and carried to a future blocking gate.

## V2-07.8 Authorization posture

This section is normative.

This chapter defines pathways at a product level only. It authorizes no implementation,
no procurement, and no policy ratification of eligibility rules.
