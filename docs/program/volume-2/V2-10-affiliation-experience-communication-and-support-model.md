## V2-10.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the experience, communication, and support model for the
affiliation service at a product level. It defines what must be communicated, to whom,
when, and how, without defining email templates, copy, or user-interface layouts in
detail.

## V2-10.2 Experience principles

This section is normative.

The applicant experience is guided, pathway-aware, bilingual, and accessible. At every
point the applicant can determine current status and the next required action
(OUT-V2-001, NFR-V2-003). The reviewer experience presents a clear queue, the evidence
under review, and governed decision actions (UX-V2-006). No experience surface owns
governed lifecycle state; all governed actions resolve to the House.

## V2-10.3 Applicant and club communications

This section is normative.

The service communicates to applicants and clubs on at least the following events:

- affiliation opened for a season;
- pathway determined and requirements presented;
- outstanding required actions and reminders;
- evidence received or replaced;
- application submitted and received;
- application returned for information, with the specific information required;
- approval, refusal, and activation outcomes;
- fee-status updates at the reconciliation boundary; and
- material administrative corrections.

## V2-10.4 Reviewer and administrator communications

This section is normative.

The service communicates to reviewers and administrators on at least the following
events:

- new application routed to a review queue;
- resubmission of a returned application;
- escalation or reassignment of a review;
- pending-decision reminders and ageing items;
- unmatched fee signals requiring administrative handling; and
- non-standard club scenarios requiring resolution.

## V2-10.5 Notification model

This section is normative.

Notifications have a defined trigger, recipient, channel, and language. The language is
the recipient's chosen official language (English or French) per the bilingual
requirement (NFR-V2-005, CTRL-V2-004). Channels are a product concern to be confirmed
with stakeholders; specific channels are not fixed in this package. Reminders and
escalation follow defined triggers rather than ad hoc timing.

## V2-10.6 Accessibility and inclusion

This section is normative.

Client-facing experiences must meet an accessibility standard to be confirmed with
stakeholders (NFR-V2-006, CTRL-V2-005). The specific standard and conformance level are
recorded as pending validation rather than asserted. Bilingual delivery is a first-class
requirement, not an afterthought.

## V2-10.7 Support and service recovery

This section is normative.

The support model provides assisted resolution for applicants and reviewers
(CAP-V2-029, NFR-V2-010). Support agents (STK-V2-010) triage and hand off to the
appropriate authority but do not own governed lifecycle state. Service recovery covers
notification-delivery failure, evidence-upload failure, and reconciliation exceptions,
each with a defined handling path. Support handoff to a governed authority is explicit
and audited.

## V2-10.8 Boundaries

This section is normative.

This chapter does not define message copy, templates, visual design, or interface
layouts. Those are downstream concerns. It defines the communication and support
obligations of the product and the conditions those obligations must satisfy.

## V2-10.9 Authorization posture

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the experience, communication, and support model at a product level
only. It authorizes no implementation, no procurement, and no master development plan.
