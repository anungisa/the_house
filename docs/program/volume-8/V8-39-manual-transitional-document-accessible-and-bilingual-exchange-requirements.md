## V8-39.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the manual, transitional, document-based, accessible, and bilingual exchange requirements of the governed exchange plane. It records that exchanges conducted by people, on paper, or through documents are governed to the same standard as automated exchanges. It authorizes no process, form, template, or tool.

## V8-39.2 Manual and transitional exchanges are governed

This section is normative.

Manual and transitional exchanges — those conducted by staff, by email, on paper, or through documents during a transition period — are governed exchanges. Each manual exchange context declares its records dependency or evidence requirement and its accessibility requirement. A manual exchange that produces no governed record and no evidence is not an accepted exchange; the absence of automation does not remove the obligation to record and evidence.

## V8-39.3 Manual conduct is not reduced governance

This section is normative.

The model preserves the following distinctions:

```
Manual exchange
≠ ungoverned exchange

Document produced
≠ document delivered
≠ document received

Available in one language
≠ bilingual obligation met

Presented
≠ accessible
```

An exchange being manual does not reduce its governance. A document being produced does not establish that it was delivered or received. Content available in only one language does not meet a bilingual obligation, and content that is merely presented is not necessarily accessible.

## V8-39.4 Bilingual obligations apply to exchanges

This section is normative.

Where an exchange carries an obligation to serve stakeholders in both official languages, the governed record declares an English semantic and a French semantic for the governed content of that exchange. The French-language obligation is recorded explicitly and is not treated as optional or deferred where it applies. A bilingual obligation is met only when the governed meaning is available in both English and French, not when a translation is merely intended.

## V8-39.5 Accessibility obligations apply to documents and manual exchanges

This section is normative.

Document-based and manual exchanges declare their accessibility requirement so that stakeholders who rely on accessible formats are not excluded. Accessibility is a property of how content is made available, not a property of its mere production. An exchange that is presented in an inaccessible form has not met its accessibility obligation, and the requirement is recorded against the exchange rather than assumed to be satisfied elsewhere.

## V8-39.6 No claim of manual-process implementation

This section is normative.

Nothing in this chapter asserts that any manual, transitional, document, accessible, or bilingual exchange is implemented, delivered, translated, or made accessible. These exchange requirements are documentary. Every controlled record is in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.

## V8-39.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It defines no manual process, form, template, translation workflow, or accessibility tool; it produces no document; and it changes no governed state. Every controlled Package 4 record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture and authorizes no construction.
