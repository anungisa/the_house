## V8-51.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates the assumptions, risks, exceptions, completed-gate references, and open items carried across Volume 8 into a single readiness register, and assigns each open item a readiness disposition. It authorizes no implementation and closes no material commitment by assertion; it records honestly what remains open and where each open item is carried forward.

## V8-51.2 Assumptions and risks

This section is normative.

Every assumption on which the Volume 8 contract definition depends is carried in REG-804 with an owner and a forward blocking gate, and every risk is recorded with its exposure and its forward owner. Assumptions and risks are not resolved by this chapter; they are made explicit so that no downstream volume inherits a hidden dependency. An assumption or risk without a named owner and a forward blocking gate fails the backlog contract.

## V8-51.3 Exceptions and completed-gate references

This section is normative.

Exceptions taken during Volume 8 are recorded with their rationale and their forward disposition. Where a chapter references a completed gate — the frozen Package gates V8-G1 through V8-G4 — that reference is historical and read-only: a completed gate is never named as a forward blocking gate, and no open item points to a completed gate or to the closing Gate V8-G5. Every open item's blocking gate is a forward gate in a downstream volume.

## V8-51.4 Readiness disposition

This section is normative.

Each open item in the readiness register carries an explicit readiness disposition drawn from the controlled vocabulary: closed with evidence; accepted as a downstream-volume input; a material commitment pending; a policy, legal, contract, privacy, or records validation pending; implementation, migration, or operational evidence required; independent assurance required; or a defect requiring amendment. Every disposition carries a rationale. No open item is dispositioned as closed unless closure evidence exists; readiness is disposed honestly, never asserted.

## V8-51.5 No manufactured completeness

This section is normative.

This chapter makes no operational-readiness, security, privacy, or assurance claim, and it does not represent the contract definition as implementation. Items requiring implementation, migration, operational proof, or independent assurance are dispositioned as such and carried forward; they are not closed by documentary assertion. The readiness register is complete in the sense that every open item is named and dispositioned, not in the sense that every item is resolved.

## V8-51.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation, resolves no material commitment by assertion, and changes no governed state. Every controlled Package 5 record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture and authorizes no construction.
