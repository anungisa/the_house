## V8-41.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter opens Volume 8 Package 5 by consolidating Packages 1 through 4 into a single integrated contract-definition baseline. It records that the affiliation command and query plane, the resource and response plane, the event, outbox, webhook, notification, and delivery plane, the identity and authorization plane, the error and reconciliation plane, the data-privacy plane, the external-provider, file, batch, migration, and exchange plane, and the versioning and compatibility plane are one coherent body of contract definitions under a single institutional authority. It authorizes no implementation. Packages 1 through 4 remain frozen and unchanged; this baseline consolidates them without reopening or amending any frozen record.

## V8-41.2 The integrated baseline

This section is normative.

The integrated baseline is a synthesis, not a new authority. Every capability in the baseline traces to a contract already defined in a frozen Package 1 through Package 4 chapter and register record; no capability in this baseline invents a contract that did not already exist, and no capability manufactures apparent completeness. The integrated capability records in REG-802 name, for each capability, its institutional authority, its interaction family, its producer, its consumer, its authoritative source, its organization and jurisdiction scope, its authorization context, its information classification, its lifecycle dependency, its idempotency and delivery posture, its reconciliation, privacy, records, accessibility, bilingual, and validation dependencies, and its not-implemented-or-not-proven status. A capability that cannot be traced to a frozen definition and an institutional authority fails closed and is not part of the baseline.

## V8-41.3 Capability families

This section is normative.

The baseline organizes contracts into distinct interaction families: synchronous commands, synchronous queries, logical resources, structured responses, domain and integration events, transactional-outbox obligations, webhooks and provider callbacks, notifications, event delivery, external-provider surfaces, file and batch exchanges, migration exchanges, and manual and transitional exchanges. Each family carries its own governing distinctions defined in the frozen packages; the baseline does not collapse the families into one another. The family of an interaction determines the obligations it must satisfy, and no interaction is defined outside a named family.

## V8-41.4 Authority and non-authorization

This section is normative.

The House holds institutional authority over the entire integrated baseline. Other participants — including experience layers such as the Button, staff tools, external providers, and downstream volumes — may produce or consume contracted interactions, but they do not hold governed lifecycle authority. The baseline confers no authority that was not already recorded in a frozen package, and it authorizes no implementation, executable interface, endpoint, transport, provider integration, or infrastructure.

## V8-41.5 No claim of implementation or conformance

This section is normative.

Nothing in this baseline asserts that any contract is implemented, delivered, integrated, provider-assured, migrated, reconciled, privacy-compliant, or compatibility-validated. Every controlled record is in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture. The generated projections that accompany this baseline are non-authoritative views of the source-controlled corpus and assert no capability. The authoritative record is the source-controlled chapters, registers, schemas, and controls.

## V8-41.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It defines no executable interface, event, integration, or exchange specification, no endpoint, route, client, adapter, worker, topic, queue, storage, transfer, or migration script, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, launch, or master development plan. Every controlled Package 5 record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture and authorizes no construction.
