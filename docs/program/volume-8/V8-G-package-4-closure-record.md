## V8-G.4 Gate V8-G4 disposition

This section is normative.

Gate V8-G4 is dispositioned as EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_FILE_BATCH_MIGRATION_AND_EXCHANGE_CONTRACT_DEFINITION_READY. The disposition is recorded in register REG-805 as a ratified gate approval. The gate confirms that Package 4 inherits the frozen Package 1, Package 2, and Package 3 corpora and the Package 3 provenance; that institutional authority over governed facts is retained across every exchange and that provider custody is not institutional authority; that provider, import, export, file, batch, migration, and manual-exchange contexts are catalogued with a named producer, consumer, owner, purpose, authority, classification, and trust boundary; that files and batches declare identity, provenance, version, manifest, counts, integrity, and reconciliation, and that structural validity is distinct from semantic and institutional validity; that imports define acceptance, rejection, quarantine, duplicate, correction, and partial-success semantics, that rejected and quarantined records remain non-authoritative, and that imports cannot silently create governed authority; that exports require explicit disclosure and recipient authority, that read access is distinct from export authority, and that export generation, delivery, receipt, processing, and reconciliation remain distinct; that migration contracts preserve source provenance and uncertainty, that mapping is distinct from identity resolution and authority confirmation, and that migration completion is distinct from business acceptance and source retirement; that provider incidents, continuity, substitution, return, deletion, residual copies, and exit are governed, and that provider certification is distinct from Curling Canada confirmation; that batch idempotency, replay, ordering, concurrency, partial failure, compensation, and reconciliation are controlled and that replay creates no new authority; that manual and transitional exchanges remain governed and auditable and that accessible and bilingual requirements apply; that a deterministic analysis completes without blocking defects and creates no executable artifacts; that the backlog carries owners, evidence requirements, and forward gates; and that no record authorizes implementation.
