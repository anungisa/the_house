## V7-38.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the measurement model for the club-affiliation experience: what may be measured, under what consent, and how measurements may and may not be interpreted. It defines the model only. It collects no telemetry and produces no measurement.

## V7-38.2 Measurement definitions and their bounds

This section is normative.

Each experience measure is defined with a precise meaning, a bounded purpose, and an explicit interpretation limit. A measure describes observed interaction; it is never the institutional truth of an affiliation. The model records measurement definitions in register REG-702 with a consent basis, an interpretation limit, and an aggregation rule, so that no measure can be silently repurposed or over-read.

## V7-38.3 Consent and privacy

This section is normative.

Measurement is governed by explicit consent and by the inherited privacy postures. No measurement expands access to personal, financial, or restricted information beyond what the governed experience already permits, and no sensitive content is copied into telemetry. Consent for measurement is explicit, purpose-bound, and revocable, and the model fails closed where consent is absent. Measurement of governed tasks never becomes a channel for disclosure.

## V7-38.4 Aggregation is not anonymization

This section is normative.

The model states that aggregation is not the same as anonymization: combining records into totals does not by itself remove the possibility of identifying an individual, particularly in small populations. Aggregation rules therefore name the thresholds and protections required before any measure may be shared, and the model treats an aggregate as potentially identifying until the protection is demonstrated. No measure is asserted to be anonymous by virtue of being aggregated.

## V7-38.5 Interpretation discipline

This section is normative.

The model names the interpretation limit of each measure and forbids treating a metric as proof. A favourable measure does not establish usability, accessibility, bilingual equivalence, or acceptance; an unfavourable measure identifies a question, not a verdict. Measures inform governed decisions; they do not make them, and they never override the authoritative source of truth for an affiliation's status or standing.

## V7-38.6 Acceptance authority and future gate

This section is normative.

The model names the acceptance authority empowered to accept measurement definitions and the forward gate at which any collection may be authorized, distinct from the authors of the experience. Package 4 defines what may be measured and how it may be interpreted; it authorizes no collection.

## V7-38.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It collects no telemetry, computes no metric, and makes no measurement-based claim. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
