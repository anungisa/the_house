## V7-14.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter specifies the interaction model for presenting requirements, capturing responses and attestations, and attaching, replacing, or withdrawing evidence. It records the relevant command, query, and validation records in register REG-702 and the requirement and evidence views and form sections in register REG-701.

## V7-14.2 Requirements and responses

This section is normative.

Applicable requirements and their versions are read through a query intent against authoritative House data. Responses are captured through form sections that validate required and conditional inputs inline, reflect derived applicability rather than a fixed field set, and preserve entered work on validation failure. Saving draft responses expresses a command intent to the House affiliation-case authority.

## V7-14.3 Attestations

This section is normative.

Attestations and acknowledgements are presented with their institutional consequence stated before confirmation. A consequential attestation requires explicit acknowledgement and is never implied by an unrelated action.

## V7-14.4 Evidence interactions

This section is normative.

Attaching evidence expresses a command intent bound to a requirement version with recorded provenance. Validation checks format and accessibility, not evidentiary sufficiency, and the interaction never implies that an uploaded document is automatically valid or sufficient evidence. Withdrawing or replacing evidence expresses a distinct command intent that preserves prior evidence history and never implies that replacement erases the prior record. Evidence is restricted and is displayed with version and provenance under minimum-necessary disclosure.

## V7-14.5 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter does not authorize production user interface, executable workflows, interface or integration contracts, final visual design, branded mockups, design tokens, production content, translations, procurement, staffing, cost commitments, pilots, rollout, or a master plan. It defines documentary interaction behaviour only, pending Gate V7-G2.
