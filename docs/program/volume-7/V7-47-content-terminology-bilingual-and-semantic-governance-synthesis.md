## V7-47.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter synthesises the content, terminology, bilingual, and semantic-governance definitions of Packages 1 through 4 into one settled account of governed meaning. It ensures that every governed concept carries an equivalent English and French meaning and a consistent expression across the experience. It synthesises definition; it approves no production copy and validates no translation.

## V7-47.2 Canonical concepts and expressions

This section is normative.

The synthesis consolidates the canonical concepts of the affiliation experience and their governed English and French expressions: statuses; actions; confirmations; errors; denials; privacy notices; evidence notices; financial distinctions; decisions; support content; and notifications. Each canonical concept is defined once and expressed consistently wherever it appears, so that the same institutional meaning is never rendered by conflicting language.

## V7-47.3 Bilingual equivalence

This section is normative.

For every canonical concept, the synthesis records the requirement that its English and French expressions carry equivalent governed institutional meaning. Machine translation and literal word-for-word matching are insufficient to establish that equivalence; qualified human judgement of institutional meaning is required. The synthesis records the equivalence requirement and the pending validation obligation; it does not assert that any translation has been validated.

## V7-47.4 Supersession and historical terminology

This section is normative.

The synthesis preserves the governed treatment of superseded and historical terminology. When a concept's expression changes, the prior expression is superseded rather than erased, and historical records retain the terminology in force when they were created. The synthesis records this supersession discipline so that terminology change never rewrites the historical record.

## V7-47.5 Semantic governance

This section is normative.

The synthesis records the governance of meaning: which authority owns each canonical concept, how a concept's expression is approved, and how its lifecycle and localization are governed. Every governed status, action, confirmation, error, denial, and notice traces to the canonical concept it expresses and to the authority that owns that concept. Where a governed expression would lack a canonical concept, the closure assessment records the gap.

## V7-47.6 Pending production content

This section is normative.

Production copy and validated translations remain pending. The synthesis defines the concepts, the expressions, and the equivalence and governance requirements; it does not produce final approved content, does not validate any translation, and asserts no bilingual conformance. Every content and terminology record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture until approved and validated at a valid downstream gate.

## V7-47.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It performs no validation and accepts no result. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
