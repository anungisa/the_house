## V7-28.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the content architecture, terminology, microcopy, and bilingual design for the club-affiliation experience. It records content terms in register REG-702. Each term in English and in French traces to one canonical governed concept. Production copy and validated translations remain pending.

## V7-28.2 Content architecture

This section is normative.

Content is organized so that each governed concept has one authoritative meaning expressed consistently across surfaces. Terminology is stable, and the same governed concept is never named by two conflicting terms. Content architecture separates institutional obligation, status meaning, and guidance so that actors can distinguish what is required from what is advisory.

## V7-28.3 Terminology and canonical concepts

This section is normative.

Each content term names its canonical governed concept and its user-facing meaning. Terminology never introduces a meaning that the governed system does not hold, and it never presents receipt as approval, review as decision, or approval as activation. Terms that carry institutional consequence disclose that consequence plainly.

## V7-28.4 Microcopy

This section is normative.

Microcopy is designed to guide action at consequential moments without overstating authority or outcome. It explains what an action will do, what evidence is required, and what recovery is available on failure. Microcopy is concise, plain, and free of internal implementation detail.

## V7-28.5 Bilingual design

This section is normative.

Bilingual design preserves equivalent institutional meaning rather than literal translation. The English and the French expression of a content term each trace to the same canonical governed concept, and status terminology is consistent across both official languages. This chapter records bilingual semantic requirements only; production copy and validated translations are recorded as pending and are not produced here.

## V7-28.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. It defines documentary content architecture, terminology, microcopy, and bilingual design only, pending Gate V7-G3.
