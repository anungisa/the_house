## V7-24.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the design of forms, validation, evidence, upload, and document interactions for the club-affiliation experience. It records design specifications in register REG-702 and preserves the governed requirement, response, attestation, and evidence model established in Package 2.

## V7-24.2 Form design

This section is normative.

Forms are designed to make the governed requirement, the expected response, and the institutional consequence clear before submission. Fields are grouped by governed section, labelled by intent, and accompanied by instructions rather than by decorative hints. Required and optional responses are distinguished, and no form implies that completion confers approval, decision, or activation.

## V7-24.3 Validation design

This section is normative.

Validation is designed to prevent avoidable error, to explain a problem in plain institutional language, and to route to correction without loss of entered work. Validation distinguishes a formatting problem from a governed completeness or eligibility problem, and it never presents a validation pass as a governed decision. Every validation message names the governed source of the rule it enforces.

## V7-24.4 Evidence and upload design

This section is normative.

Evidence and upload interactions preserve provenance and history. A replaced document never erases the record of the prior document. Upload states cover selection, in-progress, success, rejection, and recovery, and each names its governed semantic source. Evidence interactions are accessible, disclose sensitivity, and never present received evidence as reviewed or accepted evidence.

## V7-24.5 Document design

This section is normative.

Documents generated or displayed within the experience are designed to be legible, bilingual-equivalent, and unambiguous about their institutional status. A document that records receipt is visually distinct from a document that records a governed decision. Document design never asserts an outcome the governed system has not recorded.

## V7-24.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, upload pipeline, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. It defines documentary form, validation, evidence, upload, and document design only, pending Gate V7-G3.
