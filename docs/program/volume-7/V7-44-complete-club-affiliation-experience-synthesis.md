## V7-44.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter synthesises the first complete vertical of the platform: the club-affiliation experience from representative authority through standing and expiry. It draws the interaction, design, content, accessibility, bilingual, privacy, and validation definitions of Packages 1 through 4 into one continuous account of a single governed journey. It synthesises settled definition; it performs no validation and authorizes no implementation.

## V7-44.2 The affiliation vertical

This section is normative.

The synthesised vertical proceeds through its governed stages in order: representative authority; club identity; jurisdiction and season; pathway and applicability; requirements and evidence; completeness and submission; review and return; correction and resubmission; decision; finance and reconciliation; activation; and standing and expiry. Each stage is a governed step whose meaning is owned by the frozen corpus, and the synthesis preserves the order, the transitions, and the authority boundaries between stages.

## V7-44.3 Per-stage definition

This section is normative.

For every stage in the vertical, the synthesis records a governed set of attributes: the actor objective; the governed authority; the experience surface; the task flow; the command or query intent; the required data; the required evidence; the content; the required states; the errors; the recovery; the accessibility dependency; the bilingual meaning; the privacy constraint; and the future verification obligation. These per-stage attribute sets are projected non-authoritatively under the generated final-closure directory and trace to the frozen Package 1 through 4 records that define them.

## V7-44.4 Authority continuity

This section is normative.

The synthesis preserves the authority distinctions that govern the vertical. Representative authority is distinct from club identity, from delegated authority, from reviewer assignment, and from finance authority. Every governed mutation in the vertical is a House action requested through a Button responsibility; no stage permits an experience surface to change institutional state on its own. Return, correction, and resubmission preserve prior submitted work and its evidence rather than discarding it.

## V7-44.5 Evidence, privacy, and content continuity

This section is normative.

Across the vertical, evidence is bound to the requirement it satisfies and to the governed record it supports; sensitive information carries its privacy posture at every stage where it is presented; and content carries governed English and French meaning that remains equivalent across the journey. The synthesis records these continuities so that no stage silently drops an evidence, privacy, or bilingual obligation established earlier in the vertical.

## V7-44.6 Validation neutrality

This section is normative.

The synthesised vertical is a definition, not a proven experience. It states, for each stage, the future verification obligation that a downstream volume must execute and accept; it does not assert that any stage has been usability-tested, accessibility-verified, bilingually validated, or accepted by any stakeholder. Every stage remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture until executed and accepted at a valid downstream gate.

## V7-44.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It performs no validation and accepts no result. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
