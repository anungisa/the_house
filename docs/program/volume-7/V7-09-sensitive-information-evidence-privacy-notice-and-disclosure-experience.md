## V7-09.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the interaction requirements for sensitive information, evidence, privacy notices, and disclosure in the club-affiliation experience. The privacy interactions are recorded in register REG-702 as privacy records, each with a privacy posture and a prohibited inference.

## V7-09.2 Interaction areas

This section is normative.

The experience defines interaction requirements for collection notices, restricted evidence, evidence preview and download, evidence replacement, sharing and disclosure, consent or authority dependencies, access and correction requests, support visibility, export, and retention and legal-hold dependencies.

## V7-09.3 Privacy posture and minimum disclosure

This section is normative.

Each privacy interaction names its privacy posture. Sensitive information is presented at the minimum necessary level. Collection is accompanied by a collection notice that states purpose.

## V7-09.4 Access does not confer disclosure or disposition authority

This section is normative.

The experience must never imply that operational access to information or evidence confers authority to disclose it, share it, or decide its retention or disposition. Each privacy interaction records this prohibited inference explicitly.

## V7-09.5 Requests, retention, and legal hold

This section is normative.

Access and correction requests are handled through governed processes. Retention and legal-hold dependencies constrain disposition. The experience presents these constraints without implying that any operator may override them through operational access.

## V7-09.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
