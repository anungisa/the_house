## V7-53.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter reviews every active assumption, risk, exception, and validation obligation of Packages 1 through 4 and records a readiness disposition, a downstream volume, and a valid future gate for each. It ensures that no unresolved concern is left pointing at a completed gate. It records dispositions; it resolves no concern by assertion and authorizes no implementation.

## V7-53.2 Review scope

This section is normative.

The review covers every active assumption, risk, exception, and validation obligation recorded in the Package 1 through 4 backlog. Each active item is examined for its current state, the evidence it still requires, and the point in the downstream programme at which it may be resolved.

## V7-53.3 Readiness disposition

This section is normative.

For every active item, the review records a readiness disposition: whether the item is an accepted assumption carried forward, a monitored risk, a deferred exception with an expiry, or a pending validation obligation. The disposition states what the item is and what must happen to resolve it; it does not assert that the item is resolved.

## V7-53.4 Downstream destination and forward gate

This section is normative.

For every active item, the review records a downstream volume that owns its resolution and a valid future gate at which it may be resolved. No active item points to Gate V7-G1 through Gate V7-G5, because those gates are completed once Gate V7-G5 passes. The validation-execution obligations that Package 4 had provisionally associated with the Volume 7 experience programme are advanced to the downstream volumes that execute them: interface, accessibility, bilingual, and conformance validation to the acceptance-testing volume, and content-operations, measurement, and service-readiness obligations to the operations volume. Each item names its downstream volume and its forward gate.

## V7-53.5 Owner and evidence

This section is normative.

For every active item, the review records the owner accountable for its resolution and the evidence its resolution requires. The owner is the accountable authority for the downstream volume that owns the item; the evidence is the concrete artifact that a future gate will require before the item may be closed. The review records these so that no item can be closed without an accountable owner and required evidence.

## V7-53.6 No resolution by assertion

This section is normative.

The review dispositions items; it does not resolve them by assertion. An assumption remains an assumption, a risk remains a risk, and a validation obligation remains pending until the named downstream gate accepts the required evidence. Every active item remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture, and no item is treated as closed by virtue of this review.

## V7-53.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It performs no validation and accepts no result. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
