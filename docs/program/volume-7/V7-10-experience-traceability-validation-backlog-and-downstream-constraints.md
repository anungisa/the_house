## V7-10.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter assesses experience traceability, records the validation backlog, and states downstream constraints for the club-affiliation experience. The validation backlog is recorded in register REG-704, and non-authoritative coverage projections are generated under the volume's generated foundation directory.

## V7-10.2 Traceability assessment

This section is normative.

The assessment considers actors without governed needs, journey stages without authority, user actions without House commands, statuses without authoritative sources, errors without recovery paths, support actions without restrictions, sensitive content without privacy controls, workflows without accessibility obligations, concepts without bilingual semantics, degraded states without visible communication, and requirements implying implementation. Deterministic controls report these as coverage signals.

## V7-10.3 Validation backlog

This section is normative.

Unresolved items are recorded in register REG-704 as assumptions, risks, exceptions, and future tests. Each item names an owner, a required evidence expectation, and a future blocking gate. Exceptions carry an expiry or an approval reference. No unresolved item names an already-dispositioned gate as its future blocker.

## V7-10.4 Downstream constraints

This section is normative.

The experience foundation constrains downstream volumes. Interface and integration design in Volume 8 must preserve House authority and the separation invariants. Quality and master test definition in Volume 9 must validate coverage, comprehension, accessibility conformance, and bilingual equivalence. Operational proof belongs to Volume 11 and independent release assurance to Volume 12. These downstream routes are constraints, not authorizations.

## V7-10.5 Non-authoritative projections

This section is normative.

Generated coverage projections and the package report are non-authoritative. The source-controlled chapters, registers, schemas, and controls remain the authoritative record.

## V7-10.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
