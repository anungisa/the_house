## V7-40.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the service-readiness handoff for the club-affiliation experience: what the support, staff, and content functions must be prepared to sustain before the experience can be considered ready for implementation planning. It defines the readiness criteria only. It writes no operating procedures, sets no staffing, and defines no service levels.

## V7-40.2 Readiness dimensions

This section is normative.

The handoff names the readiness dimensions the service must satisfy: that support can assist a representative through the governed tasks and their recovery paths; that reviewers, finance, and privacy functions have the governed information their workbench design assumes; that content is governed, approved, and localized under chapter V7-37; and that the exception and remediation paths of chapter V7-41 are understood by the functions that will operate them. Each dimension is a readiness criterion, not an operating instruction.

## V7-40.3 Authority separation in readiness

This section is normative.

Service readiness preserves the authority separation of the frozen corpus. The handoff names, for each function, what it is prepared to do and what it is not permitted to do, so that readiness never dissolves the separation between representative, reviewer, finance, support, privacy, and administrative-correction authority. A function is not ready if readiness would require it to assume authority it does not hold.

## V7-40.4 What this handoff deliberately excludes

This section is normative.

The handoff deliberately excludes operating procedures, staffing models, rosters, service-level commitments, and cost. Those are downstream operational decisions outside Volume 7. The handoff names the readiness that must be demonstrated and the future authority and forward gate at which readiness may be accepted; it does not staff, schedule, or price the service, and it makes no commitment on behalf of any operating function.

## V7-40.5 Evidence and acceptance

This section is normative.

Readiness evidence is the demonstrated capability of each function against its readiness dimension, recorded as governed handoff artifacts with the evidence required and the deviation treatment for gaps. Readiness is accepted only by the named future authority at the forward gate, distinct from the authors of the experience. Package 4 defines what readiness must be shown and who may accept it; it accepts none and asserts no operational capability.

## V7-40.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It writes no operating procedure, sets no staffing, defines no service level, and asserts no readiness. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
