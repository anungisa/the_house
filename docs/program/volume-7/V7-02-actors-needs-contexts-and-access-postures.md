## V7-02.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the experience contexts for the club-affiliation experience. Each context names an actor, its authority posture, its primary goal, its permitted and prohibited actions, the information it needs, its sensitivity, its accessibility needs, its language needs, and its failure and recovery needs. The controlled records for these contexts are held in register REG-701.

## V7-02.2 Actor contexts

This section is normative.

The following actor contexts are defined: authorized club representative, delegated representative, new club representative, returning club representative, jurisdiction reviewer, national reviewer, finance operator, support operator, privacy and records function, service administrator, user requiring accommodation, and user working in English or French. Each is recorded in REG-701 with the required fields.

## V7-02.3 Authority postures

This section is normative.

Each actor carries an explicit authority posture. External representatives act only within confirmed or delegated authority. Reviewers act only within their jurisdiction or national scope. Finance operators confirm financial dependencies without deciding affiliation. Support operators assist without authority. The privacy and records function upholds privacy and records obligations without deciding affiliation. Service administrators maintain configuration without case-level authority.

## V7-02.4 Prohibited actions

This section is normative.

Each actor names explicit prohibited actions. In particular, no external actor may decide, reconcile, or activate its own affiliation, and the support operator may not perform decision, reconciliation, activation, disclosure, or records-disposition actions. These prohibitions are enforced as obligations of the experience.

## V7-02.5 Information, sensitivity, accessibility, and language needs

This section is normative.

Each actor records the information it needs, the sensitivity of that information, its accessibility needs, and its language needs. Every actor's language need is English and French governed-meaning equivalence. Users requiring accommodation must be able to complete every governed task through accessible primary, exception, and recovery paths.

## V7-02.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
