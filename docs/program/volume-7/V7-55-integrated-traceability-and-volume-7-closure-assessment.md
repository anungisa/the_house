## V7-55.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter assesses the integrated traceability of the Volume 7 experience corpus and records the Volume 7 closure assessment. It confirms, by deterministic analysis, that the consolidated definition is coherent and complete, and it names any gap rather than concealing it. It assesses closure; it authorizes no implementation and proves no validation.

## V7-55.2 Closure assessment scope

This section is normative.

The closure assessment examines the consolidated corpus for the coherence gaps that would prevent it from governing downstream work. It examines whether there are actors without needs; journey stages without surfaces; actions without House intents; statuses without authoritative sources; surfaces without complete states; components without accessibility or bilingual posture; sensitive interactions without privacy controls; errors without recovery; prototypes without validation protocols; measurements without interpretation limits; handoffs without evidence requirements; assumptions without destinations; House P0 findings without experience coverage; records claiming validation or implementation; and records authorizing implementation.

## V7-55.3 Deterministic analysis

This section is normative.

The closure assessment is computed deterministically from the source-controlled corpus by the Volume 7 final-closure control and the Gate V7-G5 readiness control. The controls read the frozen registers and chapters, aggregate the coverage analyses of the foundation, interaction, design, and validation controls, and report any gap as a finding. The results are projected non-authoritatively under the generated final-closure directory; the chapters, registers, schemas, and controls remain authoritative.

## V7-55.4 No blocking defect

This section is normative.

Volume 7 closure requires that the deterministic closure analysis report no blocking defect: every actor has a need; every stage has a surface; every action maps to a House intent; every status has an authoritative source; every material surface has complete state coverage; every component has accessibility and bilingual posture; every sensitive interaction has privacy controls; every error has recovery; every prototype has a validation protocol; every measurement has interpretation limits; every handoff has evidence requirements; every assumption has a downstream destination; and every House P0 finding has experience coverage.

## V7-55.5 No validation or implementation claim

This section is normative.

The closure assessment confirms that no record in the corpus claims usability, accessibility, bilingual, stakeholder, operational, or production validation, and that no record authorizes implementation. Every controlled record carries a non-implementation status, and every validation obligation carries a pending posture with a downstream destination. Where any record would claim validation or authorize implementation, the assessment reports it as a blocking defect.

## V7-55.6 Closure disposition

This section is normative.

When the deterministic closure analysis reports no blocking defect, the Volume 7 experience and service-design definition is coherent, complete, traceable, accessible, bilingual, privacy-aware, validation-ready, and implementation-neutral, and it is ready to govern the downstream volumes. This closure disposition is a readiness assessment of the definition; it is not a validation result and not an implementation authorization.

## V7-55.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It performs no validation and accepts no result. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
