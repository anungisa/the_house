## V7-43.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter establishes the integrated experience and service-design baseline for Volume 7. It consolidates the frozen definitions of Packages 1 through 4 into a single coherent reference so that downstream volumes can govern interfaces, integrations, testing, delivery, operations, and release assurance against one settled experience definition. It consolidates; it does not revalidate, does not approve a production design, and authorizes no implementation.

## V7-43.2 Scope of consolidation

This section is normative.

The baseline consolidates the complete experience corpus: the experience doctrine and service principles; the actors and their governed authority; the end-to-end affiliation journey; the service blueprint and channel responsibility model; the information architecture; the interaction architecture; the task and wire flows; the screen states; the visual system; the component taxonomy; the content architecture; the accessibility obligations; the bilingual semantics; the privacy and evidence constraints; the support and recovery experience; the validation protocols; the measurement definitions; and the implementation-handoff obligations. Each consolidated element remains owned by its frozen source chapter; this baseline references and integrates those sources without altering them.

## V7-43.3 Capability record structure

This section is normative.

For every material experience capability, the baseline records a governed set of attributes drawn from the frozen corpus: the capability itself; the actor who exercises it; the user or institutional need it serves; the House authority that governs it; the Button responsibility that surfaces it; the authoritative source of its governed meaning; the interaction source that specifies its behaviour; the visual or component source that expresses it; the content source that gives it language; its accessibility dependency; its bilingual dependency; its privacy constraint; its required states; its validation dependency; and its implementation status. These attribute sets are projected non-authoritatively under the generated final-closure directory and trace to the frozen Package 1 through 4 records that define them.

## V7-43.4 Authority integration

This section is normative.

The baseline preserves the settled separation of authority. The House is the governed system of record and the sole authority over institutional state; the Button is an experience and stakeholder surface that requests governed actions and never mutates institutional state independently. Every capability in the baseline names its House authority and its Button responsibility distinctly, and no capability grants an experience surface authority reserved to the governed platform.

## V7-43.5 Coherence and traceability

This section is normative.

The baseline is coherent when every capability traces backward to an authoritative governed source and forward to the interaction, visual, content, accessibility, bilingual, privacy, state, and validation records that complete it. Where a capability would lack any required trace, the closure assessment records the gap rather than silently accepting it. The integrated traceability is projected non-authoritatively and is not a source of truth; the frozen chapters and registers remain authoritative.

## V7-43.6 Implementation neutrality

This section is normative.

The baseline is implementation-neutral. It defines what the experience must mean and require; it does not define how any interface, service, or integration is built, and it does not assert that any capability has been implemented, rendered, validated, or accepted. Every consolidated capability remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture until a future volume executes and accepts it at a valid downstream gate.

## V7-43.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It performs no validation and accepts no result. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
