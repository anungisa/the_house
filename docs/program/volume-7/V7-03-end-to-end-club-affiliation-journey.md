## V7-03.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the complete club-affiliation journey as the first complete experience vertical. The journey and its stages are recorded in register REG-701 as a journey and its ordered stages. Each stage identifies the user objective, the House authority, the Button responsibility, the required information, the decisions, the evidence, the states, the errors, the recovery, the accessibility needs, the bilingual semantics, the privacy constraints, and the future verification.

## V7-03.2 Journey stages

This section is normative.

The journey proceeds through the following ordered stages: establish or confirm representative authority; identify or establish the club; confirm jurisdiction and season; select or derive pathway; review applicable requirements; complete responses; attach and manage evidence; review completeness; submit; monitor review; receive return-for-information; correct and resubmit; receive decision; resolve financial or reconciliation dependencies; receive authoritative activation; and view standing, expiry, and next required actions.

## V7-03.3 Authority and responsibility per stage

This section is normative.

Every stage separates House authority from Button responsibility. The House determines authoritative outcomes and records governed transitions. The Button collects input and presents the authoritative result. No stage permits the Button to decide, reconcile, or activate on its own.

## V7-03.4 States, errors, and recovery per stage

This section is normative.

Every stage names its states, its foreseeable errors, and a recovery path. Recovery preserves entered work where safe, explains what remains authoritative, and offers a way forward or an escalation route. Foreseeable error conditions include missing representative authority, duplicate organization, jurisdiction mismatch, validation errors, failed upload, stale projection, submission conflict, returned submission, payment or accounting mismatch, and activation delay.

## V7-03.5 Accessibility, bilingual, and privacy per stage

This section is normative.

Every stage records its accessibility dependencies, its bilingual-semantics requirement of English and French equivalence, and its privacy constraints. Sensitive stages, such as evidence handling and financial reconciliation, carry stronger privacy constraints and disclose the minimum necessary information.

## V7-03.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
