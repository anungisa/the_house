## V7-00.1 Purpose and inheritance

This section is normative.

Volume 7 defines the experience and service-design foundation for the club-affiliation experience. It inherits the corrected Volume 6 release baseline, published as the tag central-registration-volume-6-v1.0.1, and every frozen and released volume beneath it. The original Volume 6 tag central-registration-volume-6-v1.0.0 remains immutable and is not moved by any Volume 7 work.

Volume 7 does not alter, reopen, or weaken any inherited volume. It adds an experience layer of governed obligations above the inherited system-of-record foundation. Where an inherited volume already governs a rule, Volume 7 defers to it and references it rather than restating or superseding it.

## V7-00.2 Experience-definition authority

This section is normative.

The authority defined by Volume 7 is the authority to state experience, journey, service, information-architecture, content, accessibility, bilingual, privacy, and recovery obligations. It is not the authority to design final interfaces, produce production content, implement a design system, build executable workflows, or authorize construction.

The House remains the governed authority and system-of-record foundation. The Button is the participant- and club-facing operating experience. Volume 7 obligations constrain how the experience must behave; they do not transfer any governed decision authority into the experience layer.

## V7-00.3 Relationship among business rules, service, interaction, content, accessibility, bilingual meaning, and implementation

This section is normative.

Governed business rules originate in the inherited volumes and in The House. Service design, interaction design, content, accessibility obligations, and bilingual meaning are downstream expressions that must remain faithful to those governed rules. Implementation is a further downstream activity that Volume 7 does not authorize.

No experience obligation may contradict an inherited governed rule. Where an experience obligation appears to require a change to a governed rule, the change must be raised against the governing volume and is out of scope for Volume 7.

## V7-00.4 Amendment and supersession rules

This section is normative.

Volume 7 records are amended additively. A ratified record is never overwritten; it is superseded by a new record that preserves the prior record as history. Package freezes are preserved. A narrow post-merge provenance amendment may be recorded as a later chapter without reopening frozen content.

Volume 7 is not tagged after Package 1. Later packages continue the volume under the same inheritance and amendment discipline.

## V7-00.5 Evidence standards

This section is normative.

Package 1 records carry a self-attested and author-verified evidence label. No record asserts implementation, accessibility conformance, bilingual validation, usability, or stakeholder validation. Such claims require independent evidence recorded against a future gate and are explicitly deferred.

## V7-00.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow. It authorizes no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled Volume 7 record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture and authorizes no construction.
