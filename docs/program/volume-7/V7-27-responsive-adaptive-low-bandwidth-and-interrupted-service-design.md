## V7-27.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines responsive, adaptive, low-bandwidth, and interrupted-service design for the club-affiliation experience. It records design specifications in register REG-702 and preserves the governed obligations, status meaning, and recovery paths across all conditions.

## V7-27.2 Responsive and adaptive design

This section is normative.

The experience is designed to remain usable across small and large viewports and across pointer and keyboard interaction. Responsive design reflows content while preserving reading order, task grouping, status meaning, and evidence provenance. Adaptive design never hides an institutional obligation, a required confirmation, or a recovery path behind density, disclosure, or motion.

## V7-27.3 Low-bandwidth design

This section is normative.

The experience is designed to convey governed meaning under low-bandwidth and degraded conditions. Status, task, and evidence information remain legible without reliance on heavy media, and colour-carried meaning is always paired with text and structure. Low-bandwidth presentation discloses when displayed information may be cached or stale rather than authoritative and live.

## V7-27.4 Interrupted-service design

This section is normative.

Interrupted-service design preserves entered work and governed context when a session is interrupted, a submission fails, or a dependency is unavailable. The experience distinguishes a transient interruption from a governed refusal, routes to a recovery path, and never presents an interrupted action as a completed governed outcome. Recovery preserves provenance and never fabricates a decision or activation.

## V7-27.5 Degraded and stale disclosure

This section is normative.

When the experience must operate in a degraded mode or present information that may be stale, it discloses that condition rather than implying live authority. A degraded or stale presentation names its governed semantic source and its point-in-time posture and routes the actor to the authoritative record for confirmation.

## V7-27.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, caching implementation, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. It defines documentary responsive, adaptive, low-bandwidth, and interrupted-service design only, pending Gate V7-G3.
