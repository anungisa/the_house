## V7-18.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter specifies the governed staff workbench model for reviewer, finance, support, privacy and records, and administrative functions. It records the workbenches and their authority constraints in register REG-701 and the associated reviewer command and query intents in register REG-702.

## V7-18.2 Reviewer workbench

This section is normative.

The reviewer workbench presents the assigned queue within jurisdictional scope, case context, and disposition controls within reviewer authority. Reading the queue expresses a restricted query intent. Recording a review outcome and returning a submission for information express command intents within authority. A review recommendation is not itself a governed decision, and reviewers do not activate standing or reconcile finance.

## V7-18.3 Finance workbench

This section is normative.

The finance workbench presents obligation status, acknowledgements, and reconciliation. Finance records accounting confirmation and resolves reconciliation mismatches within the House finance function. Finance does not decide affiliation outcomes and does not activate standing.

## V7-18.4 Support workbench

This section is normative.

The support workbench presents user context and non-sensitive diagnostics within support boundaries. Support provides guidance, assisted navigation, and escalation to the correct authority. Support acquires no governed authority and does not approve, decide, reconcile, activate, inspect restricted evidence, disclose restricted information, or mutate history.

## V7-18.5 Privacy, records, and administrative workbenches

This section is normative.

The privacy and records workbench presents access, correction, legal-hold, and disposition dependencies through governed processes, where operational access is not disclosure authority. The administrative workbench presents privileged-action confirmation and audit evidence within governed limits and is not case-level decision authority. Each workbench declares its authority constraints and prohibited actions.

## V7-18.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter does not authorize production user interface, executable workflows, interface or integration contracts, final visual design, branded mockups, design tokens, production content, translations, procurement, staffing, cost commitments, pilots, rollout, or a master plan. It defines documentary interaction behaviour only, pending Gate V7-G2.
