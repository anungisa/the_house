## V7-07.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the accessibility, bilingual-equivalence, and inclusive-experience obligations for the club-affiliation experience. The obligations are recorded in register REG-702 as accessibility and bilingual records, each with a requirement and, for accessibility, a verification method. This chapter claims no conformance and no validated translation.

## V7-07.2 Accessibility obligations

This section is normative.

The experience carries accessibility obligations for keyboard interaction, focus sequence, semantic structure, forms and instructions, error prevention and recovery, status announcements, evidence interaction, authentication and recovery, time-sensitive tasks, alternate formats, and accommodation paths. Each obligation applies to primary, exception, and recovery paths.

## V7-07.3 Verification methods

This section is normative.

Each accessibility obligation names a future verification method. Verification is deferred to a future gate and is not asserted as complete in Package 1.

## V7-07.4 Bilingual equivalence

This section is normative.

English and French governed meanings must remain equivalent. Bilingual obligations are recorded with both an English semantic requirement and a French semantic requirement. Equivalence is an obligation of the experience; validated translation is deferred.

## V7-07.5 Inclusive experience

This section is normative.

Users requiring accommodation must be able to complete every governed task through accessible equivalents, including on exception and recovery paths. A documented accommodation path exists for governed tasks.

## V7-07.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. It asserts no accessibility conformance or validated translation. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
