## V7-01.1 Governed truth originates in The House

This section is normative.

All governed truth about affiliation originates in The House. The Button presents governed truth and collects input, but it does not independently decide affiliation outcomes, reconcile finances, or activate standing. The experience must never present the Button as a source of institutional truth.

## V7-01.2 The Button presents and collects, and does not independently decide

This section is normative.

The experience must keep presentation and collection distinct from decision. Where the experience shows a status or an outcome, it shows a value determined by The House. Where the experience collects input, that input becomes authoritative only through a governed transition recorded by The House.

## V7-01.3 Users see status, action, reason, consequence, and recovery

This section is normative.

For each context, the experience must make available the current status, the required or available action, the reason a state or requirement applies, the consequence of acting or not acting, and the recovery path when something goes wrong. These five elements are the minimum a user needs to act responsibly.

## V7-01.4 Progressive disclosure without concealing obligations

This section is normative.

The experience may disclose information progressively to reduce complexity, but it must never conceal an institutional obligation. A required action, a consequence, or a restriction must remain discoverable even when detail is deferred.

## V7-01.5 Minimum necessary information

This section is normative.

The experience presents the minimum information necessary for a context and collects the minimum information necessary for a task. Sensitivity and privacy posture constrain what is shown to whom.

## V7-01.6 Accessibility and bilingual equivalence by design

This section is normative.

Accessibility and English and French governed-meaning equivalence are obligations of the experience by design, across primary, exception, and recovery paths. This chapter states the obligation; it makes no conformance or validated-translation claim.

## V7-01.7 Errors preserve entered work

This section is normative.

When a recoverable error occurs, the experience preserves entered work where it is safe to do so and presents a clear path to correct and continue.

## V7-01.8 Interruption and degraded states remain visible

This section is normative.

Interruptions and degraded states are disclosed rather than hidden. The experience shows what remains authoritative, what is temporarily unavailable, and how to proceed or recover.

## V7-01.9 Support assists without acquiring decision authority

This section is normative.

Support assists users in understanding status, actions, and recovery. Support never acquires decision, reconciliation, activation, disclosure, or disposition authority through the experience.

## V7-01.10 Consequential actions require confirmation and traceable outcomes

This section is normative.

Consequential actions require clear confirmation before they proceed, and they produce a traceable governed outcome. The experience must not allow a consequential action to occur without confirmation or without a traceable result.

## V7-01.11 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
