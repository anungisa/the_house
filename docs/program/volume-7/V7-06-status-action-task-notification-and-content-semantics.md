## V7-06.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the controlled semantics of status, action, task, notification, and content for the club-affiliation experience. Each semantic concept is recorded in register REG-702 with its canonical concept, user-facing meaning, institutional consequence, permitted action, prohibited inference, English semantic requirement, French semantic requirement, accessibility requirement, source of truth, and staleness posture.

## V7-06.2 Governed status and action concepts

This section is normative.

The following concepts carry governed semantics: current status, required action, optional action, completed action, blocked action, awaiting review, returned for information, resubmitted, approved, reconciliation pending, activation pending, active, expired, withdrawn, refused, and reconsideration. Each is recorded with its meaning and consequence.

## V7-06.3 Prohibited inferences

This section is normative.

Each semantic concept names a prohibited inference that the experience must not allow a user to draw. In particular, approval is not activation, a pending state is not an outcome, and a completed action is not a decided affiliation.

## V7-06.4 English and French semantic requirements

This section is normative.

Each semantic concept records an English semantic requirement and a French semantic requirement. The governed meaning must be equivalent in both official languages. This chapter states the requirement; it makes no validated-translation claim.

## V7-06.5 Source of truth and staleness

This section is normative.

Each semantic concept names its source of truth and its staleness posture. Where presented information may be stale, the experience discloses that possibility and offers a way to obtain the authoritative state.

## V7-06.6 Consequential confirmation and content

This section is normative.

Content that precedes a consequential action states what will happen and that the outcome is traceable. Content never implies an outcome that The House has not determined.

## V7-06.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It creates no final visual design, production content, coded interface, design-system implementation, or executable workflow, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
