## V7-54.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter transfers the controlled constraints of the Volume 7 experience definition to the downstream volumes that will govern interfaces, testing, delivery, operations, and release, and it provides an executive experience brief. It hands off constraints and briefs the executive authority; it authorizes no downstream work and asks for no approval it is not entitled to request.

## V7-54.2 Handoff to Volume 8

This section is normative.

Volume 7 hands off to Volume 8 the controlled constraints for application programming interfaces, events, integrations, and provider and exchange contracts. Every governed action and query intent, every authority constraint, and every state and evidence obligation that an interface must honour is transferred as a constraint Volume 8 inherits. Volume 8 defines the contracts; Volume 7 defines the governed meaning those contracts must preserve.

## V7-54.3 Handoff to Volume 9

This section is normative.

Volume 7 hands off to Volume 9 the controlled constraints for experience, security, accessibility, bilingual, resilience, and acceptance testing. Every validation protocol, every evidence boundary, every accessibility and bilingual obligation, and every acceptance authority is transferred as a constraint Volume 9 inherits. Volume 9 executes and accepts validation; Volume 7 defines what must be validated and by whom.

## V7-54.4 Handoff to Volumes 10, 11, and 12

This section is normative.

Volume 7 hands off to Volume 10 the controlled constraints for delivery and implementation planning; to Volume 11 the controlled constraints for support, content operations, monitoring, interruption, and service recovery; and to Volume 12 the controlled constraints for release evidence, conformance evidence, acceptance, and material commitment. Each handoff transfers the governed obligations the receiving volume must honour, and each preserves the non-implementation posture of the experience definition.

## V7-54.5 Executive experience brief

This section is normative.

The executive brief explains, for the executive acceptance authority, what Volume 7 defined, what remains unvalidated, and what the authority is not yet being asked to approve. Volume 7 defined the complete experience and service-design baseline for club affiliation. It did not validate usability, accessibility, bilingual equivalence, or service readiness; it did not approve production content or a production interface; and it authorized no implementation. The authority is not being asked to approve implementation, a production design, or a master development plan.

## V7-54.6 Neutrality of handoff

This section is normative.

The handoff transfers constraints; it does not authorize downstream execution. Naming a downstream volume and a forward gate for a constraint does not begin the work at that gate, and it does not approve the work in advance. Every handed-off constraint remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture until the receiving volume executes and accepts it at a valid gate.

## V7-54.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It performs no validation and accepts no result. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
