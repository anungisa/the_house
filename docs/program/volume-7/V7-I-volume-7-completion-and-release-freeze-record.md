## V7-I.5 Integrated conclusion

This section is normative.

The integrated conclusion of Volume 7 is that the club-affiliation experience and service design are defined completely enough to hand off to downstream interface, integration, validation, and operational volumes, and that this definition authorizes no implementation. The House remains the governed system of record and the sole lifecycle authority; the Button remains a stakeholder experience surface that may request actions but never owns governed state. Holding an account is not membership, membership is not authority to act, and a stakeholder action is a governed request that resolves to a House command intent rather than an independent state change. Every House P0 finding has a corresponding experience-definition coverage, and every unresolved concern names a valid downstream destination.
