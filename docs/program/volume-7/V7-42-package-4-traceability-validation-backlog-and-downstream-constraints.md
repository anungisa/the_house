## V7-42.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter closes the Package 4 definition set by establishing traceability across the validation and handoff corpus, recording the validation backlog that Package 4 leaves for future execution, and naming the downstream constraints that future work inherits. It performs no validation and authorizes no implementation.

## V7-42.2 Traceability

This section is normative.

Every Package 4 artifact traces backward to the frozen Package 1, 2, and 3 records it validates or hands off, and forward to the future gate at which its execution or acceptance may occur. Validation families trace to the obligations they test; scenarios trace to the interaction and design records they exercise; handoff artifacts trace to the design sources they package; and issues trace to what raised them. The traceability is recorded in registers REG-701 through REG-704 and projected non-authoritatively under the generated validation-handoff directory.

## V7-42.3 Validation backlog

This section is normative.

Package 4 defines validation; it does not execute it. The validation backlog records, as governed items with owners and forward gates, every validation that remains to be performed: usability research, accessibility verification, bilingual validation, content approval, measurement collection, implementation conformance, and service readiness. No backlog item points to a gate that has already been dispositioned, and no item is assumed resolved. Two open Package 3 concerns that had pointed to Gate V7-G4 are advanced to the next forward gate, since Package 4 defines rather than closes them.

## V7-42.4 Downstream constraints

This section is normative.

Future work inherits the constraints Package 4 records: validation must be executed under the doctrine and evidence boundaries of chapter V7-32; no family's evidence may substitute for another's; acceptance must be performed by the named authority at the named forward gate; and every inherited audit, evidence, accessibility, bilingual, privacy, and non-implementation control is preserved. Implementation planning may not begin until the validations defined here have been executed and accepted by their future gates.

## V7-42.5 What Package 4 does not establish

This section is normative.

Package 4 establishes no validation result. It does not prove usability, accessibility conformance, bilingual equivalence, service readiness, or implementation conformance; it does not approve content; and it does not accept any stakeholder or production decision. Every controlled record remains not proven, and every reference prototype remains a reference candidate that is not approved.

## V7-42.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It executes no validation and accepts no result. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
