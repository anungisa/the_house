## V7-48.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter synthesises the accessibility, responsive, privacy, evidence, and inclusive-service obligations of Packages 1 through 4 into one settled account. It ensures that these obligations are represented across every path of the experience, not only the primary flow. It synthesises obligation; it creates no conformance claim and performs no validation.

## V7-48.2 Coverage across paths

This section is normative.

The synthesis consolidates obligations across every governed path: the primary affiliation flows; the exception paths; the staff workbenches; authentication and recovery; evidence interaction; documents; narrow and wide layouts; low-bandwidth conditions; interruption; alternate formats; accommodation; restricted evidence; disclosure; and support access. Each obligation is recorded for every path where it applies, so that accessibility, responsiveness, privacy, and inclusion are continuous rather than confined to the happy path.

## V7-48.3 Accessibility obligations

This section is normative.

The synthesis records the accessibility obligations that every surface and component must meet across primary, exception, staff, interruption, and recovery paths. These obligations include the governed behaviours required for assistive technology, keyboard operation, alternate formats, and accommodation. The synthesis records the obligations and the pending verification; it asserts no accessibility conformance.

## V7-48.4 Privacy and evidence obligations

This section is normative.

The synthesis records the privacy and evidence obligations that govern sensitive interactions. Restricted evidence carries its access constraints wherever it is presented; disclosure follows the governed posture; and evidence remains bound to the requirement and record it supports. The synthesis records these constraints for every path where sensitive information or evidence is handled, so that no path silently relaxes a privacy or evidence obligation.

## V7-48.5 Responsive and inclusive service

This section is normative.

The synthesis records the responsive and inclusive-service obligations that keep the experience usable under narrow and wide layouts, low bandwidth, interruption, and the need for alternate formats and accommodation. Support access is recorded as a governed path with its own obligations. These obligations trace to the frozen accessibility, responsive, and support definitions that establish them.

## V7-48.6 No conformance claim

This section is normative.

The synthesis defines obligations and records pending verification; it creates no conformance or validation claim. A static scan is not a manual test, a manual test is not an assistive-technology test, and none of these alone is complete evidence or a conformance claim. Every accessibility, responsive, privacy, evidence, and inclusive-service record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture until verified and accepted at a valid downstream gate.

## V7-48.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It performs no validation and accepts no result, and it creates no accessibility, bilingual, privacy, or usability conformance claim. It creates no production user interface, production content, validated translation, coded interface, design-system implementation, executable workflow, or interface or integration contract, and no procurement, sequencing, staffing, cost, pilot, rollout, or master development plan. Every controlled record remains in a not-implemented-or-not-proven posture.
