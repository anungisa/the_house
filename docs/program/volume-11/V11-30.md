## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the provider operations, continuity, return, deletion,
substitution, and exit model of the affiliation vertical. It defines provider
obligations only and authorizes no provider engagement and no operation.

## Synthesis

The provider-operational coverage in REG-1101 consolidates provider contexts and
provider-operational requirements, including incident and continuity obligations,
subcontractor dependency, return requirement, deletion evidence, residual copy
posture, backup posture, substitution dependency, reconciliation requirement, and exit
procedure. Defining provider-operational requirements does not establish provider
assurance; assurance requires independent evidence of provider continuity, deletion,
and exit.

## Controlling distinctions

> Provider custody is not Curling Canada authority.
>
> Provider certification is not operational assurance.
>
> Provider availability is not end-to-end service availability.

## Posture

Every provider record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
provider is engaged and no provider integration is created, and no record authorizes
implementation or operations.
