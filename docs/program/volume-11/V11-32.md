## Purpose

This chapter records the integrated traceability and closure assessment for the whole
of Volume 11. It assesses definitional sufficiency only and authorizes no operation,
no migration, no recovery, no adoption, no provider engagement, and no release.

## Integrated traceability

Every coverage record in REG-1101 and every operational requirement in REG-1102 traces
to the affiliation vertical scope and to a Volume 12 destination through the future
gate V12-G1. Every whole-volume backlog disposition in REG-1104 names its owner,
required action or evidence, dependencies, acceptance authority, Volume 12 destination,
and readiness disposition, and points only to the future blocking gate V12-G1.

## Closure assessment

The affiliation operating model is assessed as sufficiently authoritative, traceable,
ownership-bound, execution-neutral, migration-aware, supportable, recoverable,
accessible, bilingual, provider-governed, and evidence-disciplined to govern Volume 12
release and acceptance-evidence definition. This assessment is documentary.

## Controlling distinctions

> Definition completeness is not operational readiness.
>
> Documentary approval is not operational proof.
>
> Volume 11 closure is not release acceptance.

## Posture

No record in Volume 11 asserts that any operational capability has been implemented,
exercised, or accepted. Volume 11 closure defines what Volume 12 must prove; it proves
no operational capability itself.
