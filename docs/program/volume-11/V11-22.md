## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the authority, ownership, accountability, and decision-rights
model of the affiliation vertical across all twelve ownership dimensions. It defines
accountable roles only and confers no capacity, staffing, or authorization.

## Synthesis

The authority and ownership map in REG-1101 consolidates institutional authority,
service ownership, product, domain, technical, data, security, privacy, accessibility,
bilingual, support, and provider ownership. Recording an owner defines accountability;
it does not assign staff, allocate capacity, or make a role available.

## Controlling distinctions

> Service ownership is not institutional decision authority.
>
> Technical custody is not data authority is not records authority.
>
> Provider custody is not Curling Canada authority.
>
> Support access is not mutation authority.

## Posture

Every ownership record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
ownership record authorizes implementation or operations, and no owner assignment is
a staffing, procurement, or capacity commitment.
