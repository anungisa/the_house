## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the continuity, backup, restoration, recovery, and
degraded-operation model of the affiliation vertical. It defines continuity
obligations only and authorizes no operation and no backup or recovery exercise.

## Synthesis

The continuity-recovery coverage in REG-1101 consolidates continuity procedures,
backup verification, restoration, recovery, and degraded operation. Every continuity
procedure defines its failure stimulus, affected service, authoritative state at risk,
preserved work, prohibited action, degraded mode, fallback, recovery, and required
evidence. Recovery definition is not recovery capability; capability requires executed
recovery exercises with evidence.

## Controlling distinctions

> Backup generated is not backup verified is not backup restorable.
>
> Restore completed is not service recovered.
>
> Service recovered is not data reconciled.

## Posture

Every continuity record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
backup, restore, or recovery is executed, and no record authorizes implementation or
operations.
