## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the migration, coexistence, rehearsal, cutover, rollback,
acceptance, and source-retirement model of the affiliation vertical. It defines
migration obligations only and authorizes no migration and no operational execution.

## Synthesis

The migration-operational coverage in REG-1101 consolidates migration runbooks,
rehearsal, cutover, rollback, acceptance, reconciliation, and source retirement. Every
migration runbook preserves source authority, provenance capture, mapping dependency,
uncertainty treatment, quarantine posture, rehearsal requirement, cutover boundary,
acceptance criteria, rollback dependency, and source-retirement boundary. Defining
migration readiness does not authorize migration, rehearsal, cutover, or source
retirement.

## Controlling distinctions

> Mapping complete is not identity resolved.
>
> Rehearsal completed is not migration authorized.
>
> Cutover completed is not business accepted.
>
> Business accepted is not source retired.

## Posture

Every migration record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
migration, rehearsal, cutover, reconciliation, or source retirement is executed, and
no record authorizes implementation or operations.
