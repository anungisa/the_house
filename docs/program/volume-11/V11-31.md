## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the operational-readiness, independent-assurance, and
Volume 12 handoff model of the affiliation vertical. It defines evidence and handoff
obligations only and authorizes no operation, no assurance engagement, and no release.

## Synthesis

The operational-readiness requirements in REG-1102 define operational-readiness,
operational-acceptance, migration-acceptance, recovery-evidence, reconciliation-evidence,
training-competence-evidence, adoption-evidence, provider-assurance-evidence,
independent-assurance, Volume 12 release-evidence, and material-commitment requirements.
Every readiness and acceptance requirement binds its capability reference, observed
result binding, evidence location, evidence integrity, executor role, reviewer role,
independence requirement, and defect disposition.

## Volume 12 handoff

The Volume 12 handoff coverage in REG-1101 records the destinations for integrated
gate, release, assurance, material-commitment, and acceptance-evidence definition.
House P0 findings have operational-proof and release-evidence destinations recorded in
REG-1101. Volume 12 may assess evidence but may not infer missing evidence.

## Controlling distinctions

> Readiness defined is not readiness demonstrated.
>
> Independent assurance required is not independent assurance obtained.
>
> Volume 12 may assess evidence but may not infer missing evidence.

## Posture

Every readiness, assurance, and handoff record is not implemented, not operational, and
not executed. No assurance is engaged and no release is authorized, and no record
authorizes implementation or operations.
