## Purpose

This chapter defines documentary support, service-request, escalation, incident, and
problem runbooks for the affiliation vertical. It defines obligations only and
authorizes no operation, support queue, or tooling.

## Support classes and runbooks

Documentary runbooks are defined for account and access assistance,
representative-authority disputes, club-identity conflicts, jurisdiction and season
issues, requirements and evidence questions, submission uncertainty, review and
assignment issues, return and resubmission support, decision disclosure, payment and
accounting issues, reconciliation mismatch, activation and standing issues, provider
and exchange failures, privacy, records, accessibility, and bilingual support, and
incident and problem escalation. Each support class in REG-1101 and each runbook in
REG-1102 records its permitted requester, receiving role, triage criteria, authority
boundary, information classification, minimum-necessary access, priority-method
dependency, escalation path, diagnostic evidence, communication obligations,
accessibility and bilingual obligations, privacy restriction, resolution authority,
closure evidence, reopening criteria, and future gate.

## Controlling distinctions

> Support access is distinct from mutation authority and from decision authority.
>
> Triage is distinct from diagnosis and from resolution.
>
> Workaround is distinct from defect remediation.
>
> Ticket closure is distinct from institutional obligation completion.

A support role may assist and diagnose within a minimum-necessary boundary but never
holds mutation authority over governed state or decision authority over affiliation
outcomes. A workaround is not a defect remediation, and a closed ticket is not a
completed institutional obligation. These distinctions are recorded as decision
ADR-V11-006.

## Boundary

This chapter defines documentary runbooks. A defined runbook is not an executed
runbook. No support operation is performed and no support tooling is created.
