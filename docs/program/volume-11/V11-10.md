## Purpose

This chapter describes the generated, non-authoritative projections of the Volume
11 corpus. The projections are derived views produced by the deterministic control
scripts from the source-controlled chapters and registers. They are never a source
of truth, never confer ratification, and never authorize any operation. The
Markdown chapters, YAML registers, and JSON schemas remain the authoritative
record.

## Foundation projections

The operational-governance foundation control emits ten JSON projections and one
Markdown report under `docs/program/volume-11/generated/foundation/`:

- `service-operating-model-and-ownership-map.json` — services, capabilities,
  owners, and governed ownership gaps;
- `service-lifecycle-state-map.json` — the operating states and their
  distinctions;
- `support-incident-problem-and-escalation-map.json` — the support classes and
  escalation routes;
- `observability-audit-and-incident-response-map.json` — the observability
  signals and their evidence bindings;
- `continuity-backup-restore-and-recovery-map.json` — the continuity scenarios and
  backup, restore, and recovery obligations;
- `migration-coexistence-rehearsal-and-reconciliation-map.json` — the migration
  stages and reconciliation obligations;
- `training-communications-adoption-and-accessibility-map.json` — the training
  audiences and adoption measures;
- `provider-operations-continuity-and-exit-map.json` — the providers and their
  certification and assurance boundaries;
- `operational-evidence-assurance-and-acceptance-map.json` — the evidence
  requirements and acceptance criteria;
- `volume-12-handoff-and-unresolved-item-map.json` — the handoffs and unresolved
  items carried to Volume 12;

together with `package-1-operational-governance-report.md`, a human-readable
summary of the same content.

## Gate and provenance projections

The gate control emits `gate-v11-g1-readiness.json` under the same foundation
directory, reporting the readiness of Gate V11-G1 against its conditions with the
disposition target
`OPERATIONS_MIGRATION_ADOPTION_AND_ASSURANCE_GOVERNANCE_FOUNDATION_READY`. The
provenance-integrity control emits projections under
`docs/program/volume-11/generated/provenance/`, describing the package chronology,
the authoring-and-closure separation, the gate-and-freeze effectiveness, and the
provenance-role classification.

## Control report

The orchestrator emits `governance-control-report.md` under
`docs/program/volume-11/generated/`, aggregating the findings of the structural,
reference, foundation, provenance-integrity, and gate controls. Like every other
projection, it is non-authoritative and regenerated from the corpus on each run.
