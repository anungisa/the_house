## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the training, onboarding, communications, accessibility,
bilingual, and adoption model of the affiliation vertical. It defines training and
adoption obligations only and authorizes no training delivery and no operation.

## Synthesis

The training-adoption coverage in REG-1101 consolidates training requirements,
onboarding, adoption audiences, and adoption measures. Accessibility and bilingual
obligations apply to training, support, communications, documents, and operational
procedures. Training and adoption plans do not establish competence or sustained
adoption; both require executed assessment and adoption evidence.

## Controlling distinctions

> Content authored is not content approved is not training delivered.
>
> Training delivered is not competence demonstrated.
>
> Account created is not authority established.
>
> Onboarding completed is not sustained adoption.

## Posture

Every training and adoption record is not implemented, not operational, and not
executed. No training is delivered, no account is created, and no record authorizes
implementation or operations.
