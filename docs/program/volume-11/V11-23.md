## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the thirteen operating states and the readiness, acceptance,
deployment, stabilization, operation, degradation, recovery, and retirement model of
the affiliation vertical. It defines lifecycle obligations only and authorizes no
operation.

## Synthesis

The service-lifecycle coverage in REG-1101 consolidates the operating states DEFINED,
IMPLEMENTED, VERIFIED, RELEASE_CANDIDATE, OPERATIONALLY_PREPARED,
OPERATIONALLY_ACCEPTED, DEPLOYED, STABILIZING, OPERATING, DEGRADED, SUSPENDED,
RECOVERING, and RETIRED. Operational-definition completeness is not operational
readiness; readiness requires executed, environment-bound evidence produced in a
future authorization-gated volume.

## Controlling distinctions

> Operationally prepared is not operationally accepted.
>
> Operationally accepted is not deployed.
>
> Deployed is not stabilized is not operating successfully.
>
> Service available is not business obligations completed.
>
> Retired service is not data disposed is not records obligations closed.

## Posture

Every lifecycle record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
record asserts that any affiliation service is operating, and no record authorizes
implementation or operations.
