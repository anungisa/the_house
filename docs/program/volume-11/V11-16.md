## Purpose

This chapter defines documentary migration qualification, rehearsal, cutover,
rollback, and source-retirement runbooks for the affiliation vertical. It defines
obligations only and authorizes no migration, cutover, rollback, or source retirement.

## Runbooks

Documentary migration runbooks are defined for source qualification, provenance
capture, mapping definition, identity resolution, uncertainty and candidate
treatment, quarantine handling, rehearsal in a non-authoritative environment, cutover
qualification, business acceptance, rollback, and source retirement. Each runbook and
stage requirement in REG-1102 records its source, source authority, target service,
entry criteria, data-quality dependencies, identity-resolution dependencies,
provenance capture, mapping dependency, uncertainty treatment, identity-candidate
treatment, quarantine posture, evidence-reference posture, rehearsal requirement,
cutover boundary, acceptance criteria, rollback dependency, source-retirement
boundary, operational dependency, and future gate.

## Controlling distinctions

> Mapping complete is not identity resolved.
>
> Rehearsal completed is not migration authorized.
>
> Cutover completed is not business accepted.
>
> Business accepted is not source retired.

A completed mapping does not establish identity resolution. A completed rehearsal does
not authorize migration. A completed cutover does not establish business acceptance,
and business acceptance does not authorize source retirement. These distinctions are
recorded as decision ADR-V11-008.

## Boundary

This chapter defines documentary runbooks. No migration is qualified, rehearsed, cut
over, rolled back, or retired, and no source is modified.
