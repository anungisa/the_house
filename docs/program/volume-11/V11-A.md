## Purpose

This chapter is the closure record for Volume 11 Package 1. It records that the
operations, migration, adoption, and assurance governance foundation has been
authored, that Gate V11-G1 has been dispositioned, that Package 1 is frozen, and
that a bounded authorization for Package 2 has been granted. It authorizes no
operation.

## What Package 1 delivered

Package 1 delivered the documentary operations, migration, adoption, and assurance
governance foundation: the service operating model and ownership boundary, the
lifecycle and operating states, the support model, the observability and
incident-response model, the continuity/backup/restore/recovery model, the
migration/coexistence/reconciliation model, the training/adoption/accessibility
model, the provider operations and exit model, and the operational evidence,
assurance, acceptance, and Volume 12 handoff model. It delivered these as
governance obligations recorded in registers REG-1100 through REG-1105, with
deterministic controls and non-authoritative projections.

## Posture at closure

Every controlled record remains not-implemented, not-operational, and
not-executed. No record authorizes implementation or operations. Nothing has been
operated, migrated, backed up, restored, recovered, trained, onboarded, engaged
with a provider, or accepted. The foundation is documentary. Operational execution
remains a future concern, distinct from the operational definition recorded here.

## Gate V11-G1 disposition

Gate V11-G1 is dispositioned
`OPERATIONS_MIGRATION_ADOPTION_AND_ASSURANCE_GOVERNANCE_FOUNDATION_READY` in
REG-1105. The disposition attests only that the documentary foundation is complete
and internally coherent against the gate's conditions. It confers no readiness to
operate.

## Package 1 freeze

Package 1 is frozen in REG-1105 as `PACKAGE-11-1`. The frozen artifacts are the
Volume 11 chapters and registers at version 1.0.0. The freeze is a point-in-time
snapshot; later packages are additive and do not reopen the frozen substantive
content of Package 1.

## Bounded Package 2 authorization

Package 2 of Volume 11 is authorized for definition work only, bounded as follows:

> AUTHORIZED FOR AFFILIATION OPERATING MODEL, SUPPORT MODEL, MIGRATION RUNBOOK
> DEFINITION, RECONCILIATION MODEL, CONTINUITY AND RECOVERY PROCEDURE DEFINITION,
> TRAINING AND ADOPTION PLAN, PROVIDER OPERATIONS, AND OPERATIONAL-EVIDENCE PLAN
> ONLY.

The following are explicitly NOT AUTHORIZED by this closure: any runtime,
environment, or credential; any migration execution; any backup, restore, or
recovery execution; any training delivery; any provider engagement; and any pilot
or rollout. Package 2 continues the documentary discipline of Package 1: it
defines, and it authorizes no operation.

## Handoff

The unresolved items recorded in REG-1104 are carried to Volume 12 through the
handoff recorded in REG-1102, so that no obligation is lost across the boundary.
