## Purpose

This chapter is the closure record for Volume 11 Package 3 and for the whole of
Volume 11. It records that the integrated operating baseline, the operational and
acceptance requirements, and the Volume 12 handoff have been authored, that Gate
V11-G3 has been dispositioned, that Package 3 and the whole of Volume 11 are frozen,
and that a bounded authorization for Volume 12 has been granted. It authorizes no
operation.

## What Package 3 delivered

operating-definition baseline for the club-affiliation vertical: the integrated
operations, migration, adoption, and assurance baseline (V11-21); the authority,
ownership, accountability, and decision-rights synthesis (V11-22); the
service-lifecycle readiness, acceptance, and stabilization synthesis (V11-23); the
support, incident, problem, escalation, and communications synthesis (V11-24); the
observability, audit, reconciliation, and operational-evidence synthesis (V11-25); the
continuity, backup, restoration, recovery, and degraded-operation synthesis (V11-26);
the migration, coexistence, cutover, rollback, acceptance, and retirement synthesis
(V11-27); the data-quality, quarantine, reconciliation, and obligation synthesis
(V11-28); the training, onboarding, accessibility, bilingual, and adoption synthesis
(V11-29); the provider-operations synthesis (V11-30); the operational-readiness,
independent-assurance, and Volume 12 handoff synthesis (V11-31); and the integrated
traceability and Volume 11 closure assessment (V11-32).

## Posture at closure

Every controlled record remains not-implemented, not-operational, and not-executed. No
record authorizes implementation or operations. Nothing has been operated, migrated,
backed up, restored, recovered, reconciled, trained, onboarded, engaged with a
provider, assured, released, or accepted. The integrated operating baseline is
documentary. Operational execution, migration execution, recovery execution, adoption,
provider assurance, and release acceptance remain future concerns, each distinct from
the operational definition recorded here.

## Amendment and release-tag doctrine

The substantive authoring of Package 3 is recorded in one commit; the closure, gate
disposition, and dual freeze are recorded in a second commit; the pre-merge provenance
binding is recorded in a third commit; and the original-merge commit integrates
Package 3 into the mainline. Two post-merge amendments follow: a Package 3 provenance
amendment that binds the pre-merge and merge commits, and a provenance-role
classification that binds every provenance role to exactly one commit. Only after the
provenance-role classification is merged is the release tag
`central-registration-volume-11-v1.0.0` applied. The release tag records a
documentary release of the Volume 11 corpus; it authorizes no operation, migration,
recovery, adoption, provider engagement, or launch.
