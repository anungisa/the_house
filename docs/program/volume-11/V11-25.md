## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the observability, audit, incident-response, and
reconciliation model of the affiliation vertical. It defines observability
obligations only and authorizes no operation and no monitoring configuration.

## Synthesis

The observability-operational coverage in REG-1101 consolidates telemetry, alerting,
incident declaration, audit, and reconciliation. Every signal binds its source,
required correlation context, sensitivity, alert destination, owner, escalation
authority, containment action class, recovery dependency, reconciliation requirement,
and required evidence.

## Controlling distinctions

> Telemetry emitted is not signal observed is not alert delivered is not incident declared is not incident handled.
>
> Incident resolved is not underlying problem eliminated.
>
> Service restored is not business reconciled.

## Posture

Every observability record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
telemetry pipeline, alerting configuration, or monitoring platform is created, and no
record authorizes implementation or operations.
