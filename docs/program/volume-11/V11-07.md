## Purpose

This chapter defines how the audiences of the governed services would be trained,
communicated with, and brought to sustained adoption, and the accessibility and
bilingual obligations that govern all of it. It defines these obligations in
documentary form only; it delivers no training and measures no adoption.

## Training audiences

Training audiences are recorded in REG-1102 as training-audience records. For each
audience the model distinguishes five concerns that must not be collapsed:

- **content** — the knowledge the audience requires;
- **delivery** — the method by which that content is provided;
- **competence** — the demonstrable ability the audience must hold, which is
  distinct from mere exposure to content;
- **onboarding** — the path by which a new member of the audience reaches initial
  competence;
- **sustained adoption** — the continued, competent use of the service over time,
  which is distinct from initial onboarding.

Content is not delivery. Delivery is not competence. Onboarding is not sustained
adoption. Each is defined and evidenced separately.

## Adoption measures

Adoption measures are recorded as adoption-measure records. Sustained adoption is
defined as continued governed use with demonstrated competence, distinct from a
one-time onboarding event. An adoption measure defines what sustained adoption
would look like; it measures nothing operationally in this package.

## Accessibility and bilingual obligations

Accessibility and bilingual obligations are not optional add-ons; they apply
across the board. Specifically, accessibility and bilingual obligations apply to
training, support, communications, and procedures alike. Every training artifact,
every support interaction, every communication, and every procedure must be
accessible and must be available in both official languages. A measure or a
material that disadvantages users with accessibility needs, or that serves only
one official language, does not satisfy these obligations.
