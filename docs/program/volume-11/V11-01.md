# V11-01 — Service Operating Model, Ownership, and Institutional Authority

## Purpose

This chapter defines the operating model for the governed affiliation services:
what a service is, what a capability is, who owns each, and how operational
ownership relates to institutional authority. It defines obligations only and
authorizes no operation.

## Services and capabilities

A service is a governed unit of operational value with an owner, a set of
operating states, and support and evidence obligations. A capability is a governed
ability a service depends upon. Services and capabilities are recorded in
REG-1101. A service or capability that materially affects affiliation outcomes is
recorded as a material service capability.

## Ownership versus institutional authority

The controlling distinction of this chapter is:

> Service ownership is distinct from institutional authority.

An operational service owner holds operational decision rights within governed
policy — the authority to run the service as defined — but never institutional
authority over affiliation outcomes, which remains with the sanctioning body.
Operational ownership can be assigned, delegated, and rotated without ever
transferring the institutional authority that governs whether an affiliation is
granted, suspended, or revoked. This distinction is recorded as decision
ADR-V11-002.

## Decision rights and escalation

Every owner recorded in REG-1101 carries explicit decision rights and an explicit
escalation route. Decision rights state what an owner may decide within governed
policy. The escalation route states to whom a decision beyond those rights is
escalated. Escalation always terminates at the executive sponsor. No owner may
silently exceed their decision rights; any matter beyond them follows the
escalation route.

## Governed ownership gaps

Every material service capability must have either an operational owner or an
explicitly recorded governed ownership gap. A governed ownership gap is an honest
statement that a material capability currently lacks an assigned owner and must be
resolved before operation. Silence is not permitted: an unowned material
capability is either owned or recorded as a gap.

## Evidence control

Ownership includes accountability for the operational evidence obligations of the
owned service, as defined in Chapter V11-09 and REG-1102. An owner is accountable
for the availability of the governed operational definition, the support model,
and the evidence obligations of the service, but this accountability confers no
authority to operate before operation is separately authorized.
