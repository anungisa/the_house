## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the support, service-request, incident, problem, escalation,
communications, and closure model of the affiliation vertical. It defines support
obligations only and authorizes no operation and no support tooling.

## Synthesis

The support-operational coverage in REG-1101 consolidates support classes, service
requests, incident and problem runbooks, escalation routes, communications, and
closure evidence. Every support class defines its requester, receiving role, authority
boundary, sensitivity, escalation route, communication obligation, resolution
authority, and closure evidence.

## Controlling distinctions

> Support access is not mutation authority is not decision authority.
>
> Support request received is not support request resolved.
>
> Service request is not incident is not problem.

## Posture

Every support record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No support
queue, service-desk tooling, or communication channel is created, and no record
authorizes implementation or operations.
