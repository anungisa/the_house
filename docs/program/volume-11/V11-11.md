## Purpose

This chapter defines the documentary operating catalogue, ownership, and operating
boundaries for the club-affiliation vertical. It defines obligations only and
authorizes no operation, implementation, migration, or engagement.

## Inheritance

Package 2 inherits the released Volume 10 baseline
`central-registration-volume-10-v1.0.0`, the Package 1 governance foundation and
its closure record V11-A, and the Package 1 provenance amendments V11-B and V11-B-1.
Nothing in Package 2 reopens, modifies, or supersedes any frozen Package 1 artifact.

## First operational vertical

> Club affiliation is the first operational vertical.

Package 2 translates the Package 1 governance foundation into the detailed
documentary operating system for the club-affiliation vertical only, and for no
other vertical. This is recorded as decision ADR-V11-004.

## Service catalogue

The affiliation operating catalogue covers representative authority, club and
organization identity, affiliation cases, requirements and evidence, submission,
review and return, correction and resubmission, decision, finance and
reconciliation, activation, standing and expiry, the Button experience, staff
workbenches, support, providers and exchanges, and migration and data quality.
Each governed service and capability is recorded in REG-1101 with its institutional
authority, service owner, product owner, domain owner, technical owner, data owner,
security owner, privacy and records owner, accessibility and bilingual owner,
support owner, provider owner, operational boundaries, upstream and downstream
dependencies, evidence responsibility, escalation authority, and
operational-acceptance authority.

## Ownership doctrine

The controlling distinctions of this chapter are:

> House authority is distinct from Button interaction.
>
> Service ownership is distinct from institutional decision authority.
>
> Technical custody is distinct from data or records authority.
>
> Provider custody is distinct from Curling Canada authority.

House institutional authority over affiliation outcomes is distinct from the Button
experience layer, which requests actions but never owns governed lifecycle rules.
Service ownership confers operational decision rights within governed policy but
never institutional decision authority. These distinctions are recorded as
decisions ADR-V11-005 and are reflected in the twelve owner-assignment requirements
in REG-1101.

## Boundary

This chapter defines a documentary operating model. An operating model is not an
operating service. No affiliation service is operated, provisioned, or engaged by
this chapter.
