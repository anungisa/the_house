## Purpose

This chapter defines a documentary training, onboarding, communications, bilingual
delivery, accessibility, and adoption plan for the affiliation vertical. It defines
obligations only and authorizes no training delivery, onboarding, or communication.

## Plan

Documentary requirements are defined for training content, content approval, delivery
method, competence assessment, club and member-association onboarding, staff and
reviewer onboarding, provider onboarding, communications, and adoption measurement.
Each requirement in REG-1101 and REG-1102 records its audience, role and authority,
required knowledge, required task competence, delivery-method dependency, English and
French requirement, accessibility requirement, practice or simulation requirement,
assessment method, support dependency, onboarding evidence, adoption measure,
reinforcement requirement, readiness criterion, and future gate.

Accessibility and bilingual obligations apply to training, support, communications,
documents, and operational procedures. Every audience-facing artifact carries an
accessibility obligation and a bilingual (English and French) delivery obligation.

## Controlling distinctions

> Content written is not content approved is not training delivered.
>
> Training delivered is not competence demonstrated.
>
> Onboarding completed is not sustained adoption.

Written content is not approved content, and approved content is not delivered
training. Delivered training does not demonstrate competence, and completed onboarding
does not establish sustained adoption. These distinctions are recorded as decision
ADR-V11-009.

## Boundary

This chapter defines a documentary plan. No training is delivered, no audience is
onboarded, and no communication is sent.
