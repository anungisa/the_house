## Purpose

This chapter defines documentary data-quality, quarantine, reconciliation, and
outstanding-obligation operations for the affiliation vertical. It defines obligations
only and authorizes no correction, reconciliation, or data mutation.

## Operations

Documentary operations are defined for data-quality assessment, quarantine of
uncertain or conflicting records, correction proposal and authority, reconciliation of
financial and business state, projection rebuild, and treatment of outstanding
obligations. Each operation in REG-1101 records its authoritative source, detection
dependency, institutional decision owner, quarantine status, permitted actions,
prohibited actions, correction-method dependency, reconciliation method, escalation
authority, closure authority, required evidence, residual-risk posture, and future
gate.

## Controlling distinctions

> Detection is not correction.
>
> Acknowledgement is not reconciliation.
>
> Projection rebuilt is not authoritative state corrected.
>
> Quarantine is not deletion.

Detecting a data-quality issue is not correcting it. Acknowledging a mismatch is not
reconciliation. Rebuilding a projection is not correcting authoritative state, and
quarantine is not deletion. Correction and reconciliation remain institutional
decisions with recorded authority and evidence.

## Boundary

This chapter defines documentary operations. No data is corrected, reconciled, or
deleted, and no authoritative state is mutated.
