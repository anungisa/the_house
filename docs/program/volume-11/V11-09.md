## Purpose

This chapter defines what operational evidence must bind to, how operational
acceptance is assured independently, and how the Volume 11 foundation hands off to
Volume 12. It defines these obligations in documentary form only; it accepts
nothing and asserts no readiness.

## Operational evidence binding

Operational-evidence requirements are recorded in REG-1102 as evidence-requirement
records. Operational evidence is only meaningful when it binds to the full context
in which it was produced. Each evidence requirement binds to the environment, the
configuration, the version, the identity, the organization, the jurisdiction, the
data classification, the provider state, and the time of production. Evidence that
cannot be bound to all of these cannot support an operational claim.

## Independence and operational acceptance

Acceptance criteria are recorded as acceptance-criterion records. The controlling
principle is independence: operational acceptance is judged by an authority whose
independence from delivery and operations is required and recorded. Each acceptance
criterion names its independence requirement and its acceptance authority.
Independence means the accepting authority is not the party that delivered or
operates the service; delivery cannot accept its own work. Without recorded
independence, an acceptance is not a governed acceptance.

## No unproven claims

Nothing in this package asserts that a service is ready, that a recovery is proven,
that a migration is complete, that adoption is achieved, or that assurance is
granted. Every such claim requires bound evidence and independent acceptance, both
of which are future obligations. The posture of every record remains
not-operational and not-executed.

## Volume 12 handoff

Handoffs are recorded as handoff records. The Volume 11 operations, migration,
adoption, and assurance governance foundation hands off to Volume 12, which will
define operational readiness on top of this foundation. Each handoff names its
destination and its target volume. The handoff to Volume 12 carries the unresolved
items recorded in REG-1104, so that no obligation is lost across the boundary and
Volume 12 begins from an honest statement of what remains.
