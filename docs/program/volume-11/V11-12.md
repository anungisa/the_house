## Purpose

This chapter defines the documentary operating states, lifecycle transitions, and
stabilization model for the affiliation vertical. It defines obligations only and
authorizes no operation.

## Operating states

The affiliation vertical is defined across the operating states DEFINED,
IMPLEMENTED, VERIFIED, RELEASE_CANDIDATE, OPERATIONALLY_PREPARED,
OPERATIONALLY_ACCEPTED, DEPLOYED, STABILIZING, OPERATING, DEGRADED, SUSPENDED,
RECOVERING, and RETIRED. Each transition is recorded in REG-1101 with its affected
service, entry conditions, required implementation evidence, required test
evidence, required operational evidence, decision authority, permitted operations,
prohibited operations, rollback or suspension dependency, outstanding obligations,
exit criteria, and future gate.

## Controlling distinctions

> Operationally prepared is distinct from operationally accepted.
>
> Deployed is distinct from stabilizing.
>
> Stabilizing is distinct from operating.

Operational preparation records readiness of a documentary definition;
operational acceptance is an independent judgment supported by evidence. A deployed
service is not yet stabilizing; a stabilizing service is not yet operating. No state
in this chapter asserts that any affiliation service is operating.

## Degraded, suspended, recovering, and retired

The model defines specific handling for the degraded, suspended, recovering, and
retired states, including submission intake interruption, reviewer unavailability,
reconciliation backlog, provider outage, delayed event delivery, stale projections,
restricted-evidence unavailability, suspended activation, degraded support, and
stabilization after migration or deployment. A degraded service preserves
authoritative state and outstanding obligations; a suspended service holds
activation; a recovering service is not yet operating; a retired service is closed
under governed authority.

## Boundary

This chapter defines documentary operating states. No state transition is executed
and no operation is authorized.
