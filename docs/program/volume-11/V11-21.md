## Purpose

This chapter consolidates the Package 1 operations, migration, adoption, and
assurance governance foundation and the Package 2 affiliation operating model into a
single authoritative operational-definition baseline for the affiliation vertical.
It consolidates definitions only and authorizes no operation.

## Inherited baseline

This baseline inherits the released Volume 10 baseline
central-registration-volume-10-v1.0.0 and the frozen Package 1 corpus, including its
post-merge amendment V11-B and its provenance-role classification V11-B-1. It
inherits the frozen Package 2 corpus, including its post-merge amendment V11-D and
its provenance-role classification V11-D-1. Package 1 and Package 2 remain frozen and
unchanged; this chapter adds a consolidation layer above them.

## Consolidation

This baseline consolidates the service operating model, ownership, institutional
authority, operating states, support, incident and problem management, observability,
continuity, backup and restoration, recovery, migration, data quality, quarantine,
reconciliation, training, onboarding, adoption, provider operations, operational
evidence, independent assurance, acceptance, and the Volume 12 handoff. Every
material operational obligation is recorded in REG-1102 with its authoritative
source, institutional purpose, institutional owner, operational owner, decision
authority, affected operating states, required procedure or runbook, environment,
configuration, data, and provider dependencies, required evidence, independence
requirement, acceptance authority, and Volume 12 destination. The consolidated
coverage maps are recorded in REG-1101.

## Controlling distinction

> Operating model defined is not operating service.
>
> Consolidation is not implementation, migration, recovery, training, provider engagement, or release.

## Posture

Every consolidated record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
record authorizes implementation or operations. The integrated baseline defines what
must later be proven; it proves nothing itself.
