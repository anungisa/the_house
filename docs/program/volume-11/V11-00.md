## Purpose

Volume 11 establishes the operations, migration, adoption, and assurance
governance foundation for The House. It defines, in documentary form only, the
governance obligations under which the affiliation services could later be
operated, migrated onto, adopted by their audiences, and independently assured.

This is Package 1 of Volume 11. It defines obligations. It authorizes nothing.

## Inheritance

Volume 11 inherits, without modification, the frozen Volume 0 foundation and the
released Volume 1 through Volume 10 baselines. In particular, it builds directly
on the released Volume 10 delivery-planning and master-development-plan governance
foundation, whose inherited release baseline is the tag
`central-registration-volume-10-v1.0.0`. Volume 0 through Volume 10 remain
frozen and released; nothing in Volume 11 alters them.

The Volume 10 corpus defined how delivery would be planned and governed. Volume 11
begins the next concern: how a delivered service would be operated, how legacy
records would be migrated, how audiences would adopt the service, and how
operational claims would be independently assured — all defined here as governance
obligations, none of them performed.

## The operational-governance boundary

The controlling principle of this volume is recorded as decision ADR-V11-001:

> Operational definition is distinct from operational execution.

Authoring an operational definition never constitutes, authorizes, or implies
operational execution. Defining a backup obligation is not taking a backup.
Defining a recovery procedure is not recovering a service. Defining a migration
stage is not migrating data. Defining an adoption measure is not achieving
adoption. Defining an acceptance criterion is not accepting anything.

Accordingly, every Volume 11 record carries three non-authorizing posture fields —
implementation status, operational status, and execution status — each fixed in a
not-implemented, not-operational, and not-executed posture. No record authorizes
implementation. No record authorizes operations.

## Scope of Package 1

Package 1 defines:

- the service operating model, ownership, and its distinction from institutional
  authority;
- the service lifecycle and its operating states;
- the support, incident, problem, and escalation model;
- the observability, audit, and incident-response model;
- the continuity, backup, restore, and recovery model;
- the migration, coexistence, rehearsal, and reconciliation model;
- the training, communications, adoption, and accessibility model;
- the provider operations, continuity, and exit model;
- the operational evidence, assurance, acceptance, and Volume 12 handoff model.

## Out of scope

Package 1 creates no runtime, no environments, no credentials, no monitoring, no
queues, no migration tooling, no training platforms, and no provider integrations.
It executes no migration, backup, restore, recovery, training, onboarding,
incident, provider, or operational exercise. It makes no readiness, recovery,
migration, adoption, assurance, or acceptance claim, and it fabricates no evidence.
