## Purpose

This chapter is the closure record for Volume 11 Package 2. It records that the
affiliation operating model, migration runbooks, continuity procedures, training and
adoption plan, provider operations, and operational-evidence plan have been authored,
that Gate V11-G2 has been dispositioned, that Package 2 is frozen, and that a bounded
authorization for Package 3 has been granted. It authorizes no operation.

## What Package 2 delivered

Package 2 delivered the detailed documentary operating system for the club-affiliation
vertical: the affiliation service catalogue and ownership boundaries (V11-11), the
operating states and stabilization model (V11-12), the support, incident, and problem
runbooks (V11-13), the observability and reconciliation runbooks (V11-14), the
continuity, backup, restore, and recovery procedures (V11-15), the migration
qualification, rehearsal, cutover, rollback, and source-retirement runbooks (V11-16),
the data-quality, quarantine, reconciliation, and obligation operations (V11-17), the
training, onboarding, communications, accessibility, bilingual, and adoption plan
(V11-18), the provider operations and exit runbooks (V11-19), and the integrated
coverage and operational-evidence plan (V11-20). It delivered these as governance
obligations recorded in registers REG-1100 through REG-1105, with deterministic
controls and non-authoritative projections.

## Posture at closure

Every controlled record remains not-implemented, not-operational, and not-executed. No
record authorizes implementation or operations. Nothing has been operated, migrated,
backed up, restored, recovered, trained, onboarded, engaged with a provider, or
accepted. The operating model is documentary. Operational execution remains a future
concern, distinct from the operational definition recorded here.

## Gate V11-G2 disposition

Gate V11-G2 is dispositioned
`AFFILIATION_OPERATING_MODEL_MIGRATION_CONTINUITY_ADOPTION_AND_OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_PLAN_READY`
in REG-1105. The disposition attests only that the documentary operating model is
complete and internally coherent against the gate's conditions. It confers no readiness
to operate.

## Package 2 freeze

Package 2 is frozen in REG-1105 as `PACKAGE-11-2`. The frozen artifacts are the Package
2 chapters V11-11 through V11-20, the closure record V11-C, and registers REG-1100
through REG-1105 at their current versions. The freeze is a point-in-time snapshot;
later packages are additive and do not reopen the frozen substantive content of
Package 1 or Package 2.
