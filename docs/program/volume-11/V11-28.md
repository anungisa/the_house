## Purpose

This chapter synthesizes the data-quality, quarantine, reconciliation, and outstanding
obligation model of the affiliation vertical. It defines data-quality obligations only
and authorizes no operation and no correction execution.

## Synthesis

The data-quality-reconciliation coverage in REG-1101 consolidates data-quality
operations, quarantine, reconciliation, and outstanding obligations. Every data-quality
operation defines its authoritative source, detection dependency, operational owner,
institutional decision owner, quarantine status, permitted actions, prohibited actions,
correction method dependency, reconciliation method, escalation route, and closure
authority.

## Controlling distinctions

> Quarantine is not rejection is not correction is not institutional resolution.
>
> Acknowledgement is not accounting confirmation is not reconciliation.
>
> Projection rebuilt is not authoritative state corrected.
>
> Compensation is not history rewrite.

## Posture

Every data-quality record is not implemented, not operational, and not executed. No
correction, reconciliation, or compensation is executed, and no record authorizes
implementation or operations.
