## Purpose

This chapter records the whole-volume readiness dispositions for every active
unresolved item carried across Packages 1, 2, and 3. It normalizes the assumptions,
risks, exceptions, defects, waivers, remediations, retests, regressions, and test
obligations of the whole volume into a single, uniformly attributed disposition
layer. It is a documentary disposition only and authorizes no execution.

## Additive convergence, frozen records unchanged

The Package 1, Package 2, and Package 3 backlog records are frozen and are not
reopened, edited, or superseded. This chapter adds a whole-volume disposition record
for every active unresolved item, each of which references its historical source
record and carries the full, uniform attribution set. The historical frozen records
remain exactly as ratified; the convergence is additive only.

## Uniform attribution set

Each whole-volume readiness disposition names, for its active unresolved item: the
historical source record; the current owner; the required evidence; the applicable
test level; the environment dependency; the data dependency; the implementation
dependency; the operational-proof dependency; the independent-assurance dependency;
the acceptance authority; the downstream volume; the future gate; and the readiness
disposition. No active unresolved item is left without an owner, a required evidence
expectation, a valid downstream forward gate, and a readiness disposition.

## Forward-gate discipline

No active unresolved item points to a completed Volume 9 gate. Every whole-volume
readiness disposition names a forward gate in Volume 10, Volume 11, or Volume 12.
Gate V9-G1, Gate V9-G2, Gate V9-G3, and Gate V9-G4 are completed or completing gates
of Volume 9 and are never named as the blocking gate of an unresolved item. Readiness
is expressed as an accepted input to a downstream volume, never as a proven outcome.

## Documentary boundary

These dispositions normalize attribution; they resolve nothing. No evidence is
produced, no defect is closed, no readiness is proven, and no execution is authorized
by this chapter. The dispositions are recorded as additive whole-volume records in
register REG-904.
