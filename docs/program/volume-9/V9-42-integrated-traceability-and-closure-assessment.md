## Purpose

This chapter records the integrated traceability across the whole Volume 9 corpus and
the deterministic closure assessment that confirms the master-test definition is
complete, coherent, and traceable. It is a documentary assessment only and authorizes
no execution.

## Integrated traceability

The integrated traceability binds every material requirement to an authoritative
source and an institutional invariant, to a test obligation or a governed non-test
disposition, to an oracle where a test is defined, to an evidence expectation, to an
independence level, to an acceptance authority, and to a forward gate. It binds every
House P0 finding to a test mapping and an evidence mapping. It binds every active
unresolved item to a whole-volume readiness disposition. The traceability is recorded
across registers REG-900 through REG-905 and projected by the deterministic
final-closure analysis control.

## Deterministic closure assessment

The deterministic final-closure analysis control reads the whole source-controlled
Volume 9 corpus and completes the closure assessment without blocking defects. The
control is non-authoritative: it reports coherence and traceability and never confers
ratification or acceptance. It generates a set of closure projections that record the
integrated baseline summary, the master-test catalogue summary, the affiliation and
assurance synthesis summaries, the prerequisite matrix, the evidence and
claim-boundary summary, the defect-lifecycle summary, the coverage, independence,
acceptance, and release-evidence summary, the House P0 master-test matrix, the
whole-volume disposition index, the downstream-handoff summary, and the integrated
traceability index. A clean assessment records internal coherence and fail-closed
discipline; it records no behaviour, no effectiveness, and no readiness.

## Closure conditions

The closure assessment confirms, from concrete corpus evidence, that Packages 1, 2,
and 3 remain frozen and inherited; that a single integrated master-test baseline
exists; that every material requirement has a test obligation or a governed non-test
disposition; that the distinct object types remain distinct; that oracles derive from
governed authority; that the affiliation and assurance dimensions are fully
dispositioned; that prerequisites, evidence binding, and claim boundaries are
controlled; that the defect lifecycle and independence and acceptance authorities are
controlled; that House P0 findings have complete mappings; that every active
unresolved item has an owner, required evidence, a valid forward gate, and a readiness
disposition; that no unresolved item points to a completed Volume 9 gate; that no
executable artifact, environment, dataset, credential, tool, service, implementation,
procurement, or release was created; and that the authoring, closure-and-freeze, and
pre-merge provenance-binding separations are preserved.

## Documentary boundary

This assessment reads and reports; it changes nothing and proves nothing about
behaviour. It confers no ratification, no acceptance, and no authorization to execute.
