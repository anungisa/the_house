## Purpose

This record closes Volume 9 Package 1, the Quality and Master-Test-Governance
Foundation. It records the disposition of Gate V9-G1, the freeze of the package,
and the bounded authorization carried forward to Package 2.

## Scope closed

Package 1 authored the Volume 9 quality doctrine and evidence hierarchy, the
quality attributes and institutional invariants, the verification, validation, and
assurance distinction, the test-level taxonomy, the master test model, the
environment and test-data governance, the functional and cross-cutting assurance
foundations, the defect and disposition model, and the traceability, independence,
and release-evidence handoff. The corpus is recorded in chapters V9-00 through
V9-10 and registers REG-900 through REG-905.

## Deterministic assessment

The self-contained Volume 9 controls report structural and schema conformance,
cross-reference integrity, foundation coverage, and Gate V9-G1 readiness with no
blocking findings, and no prohibited implementation, executable-test, or
coded-artifact leakage. The controls are non-authoritative; a clean assessment
records internal coherence and fail-closed discipline, not proof of any behaviour.

## Gate V9-G1 disposition

Gate V9-G1 is dispositioned QUALITY_AND_MASTER_TEST_GOVERNANCE_FOUNDATION_READY.
This disposition confers foundation readiness only. It authorizes no
implementation, no executable test, no test environment, no test data, no provider
or product selection, and no test execution or acceptance. Documentary
effectiveness is distinct from implementation effectiveness and from operational
effectiveness.

## Freeze

The Package 1 corpus is frozen under the artifact PACKAGE-9-1. Amendments to the
frozen package are made only through a recorded, additive amendment process; the
frozen chapters and registers are not reopened. The provenance of this package is
bound in a separate pre-merge provenance-binding commit, and the original-merge and
post-merge provenance amendment are recorded separately in a later amendment
(V9-B). Volume 9 is not tagged after Package 1.

## Bounded authorization to Package 2

Package 2 is authorized to a bounded scope only: the definition of club-affiliation
functional, contract, workflow, data-quality, and migration tests. Package 2
remains test-definition only and authorizes no construction, execution,
environment, test data, provider selection, or acceptance. Any expansion beyond
that bounded scope requires a separate, explicit authorization.
