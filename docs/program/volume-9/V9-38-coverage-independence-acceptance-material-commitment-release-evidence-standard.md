## Purpose

This chapter defines the standards for coverage, independence, acceptance, material
commitment, and release evidence in the master-test baseline. It records how coverage
is measured without implying effectiveness, who may accept, and what material
commitment and release evidence would require. It is a documentary definition only
and authorizes no execution.

## Coverage

Coverage is measured by the governed presence of a disposition for every material
requirement, not by any passing result. Documentary coverage completeness is distinct
from passing, from control effectiveness, and from operational readiness. A complete
coverage map demonstrates that nothing material is undispositioned; it demonstrates no
behaviour.

## Independence

Independence is a governed property of a claim, scaled to the materiality of the
claim. Author self-check, peer review, domain review, security or privacy review,
accessibility or bilingual review, operational review, independent assurance, and
executive acceptance are distinct independence levels. The independence required is
determined by the materiality of the claim, and a more material claim requires a
higher independence level. Independence authorities and acceptance authorities are
controlled and named.

## Acceptance

Acceptance is a governed executive determination and is distinct from a passing
result and from documentary completeness. Only the named acceptance authority may
accept, and acceptance is conferred only against admissible evidence within its claim
boundary. Documentary acceptance of a definition is distinct from acceptance of
executed evidence.

## Material commitment

A material commitment — a procurement, a staffing commitment, a cost commitment, a
sequencing commitment, a pilot, a rollout, a release, or a launch — is outside the
authority of this volume. The standard records that material commitments are pending
and that no record in Volume 9 authorizes one. A material-commitment requirement
names what a later volume would have to establish; it commits nothing.

## Release evidence

Release evidence is the governed body of admissible, in-boundary evidence that a
future release volume would require before a release could be accepted. This standard
names what release evidence must bind and how independent it must be; it produces no
release evidence and authorizes no release.

## Documentary boundary

This standard defines coverage, independence, acceptance, material commitment, and
release evidence; it measures no coverage against results, confers no acceptance,
makes no commitment, and produces no release evidence.
