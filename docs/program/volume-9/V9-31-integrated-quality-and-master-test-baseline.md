## Purpose

This chapter establishes the single integrated master-test baseline for Volume 9.
It consolidates the Package 1 quality-and-test foundation, the Package 2 affiliation
test definition, and the Package 3 cross-cutting assurance test definition into one
integrated master-test baseline against which every material requirement in the
governed system is dispositioned. The baseline is a documentary definition only. It
authorizes no implementation, no test execution, no environment, no dataset, no
credential, no tool, no service, and no acceptance.

## The single integrated baseline

There is one integrated master-test baseline for Volume 9. It does not restate the
frozen Package 1, Package 2, and Package 3 corpora; it inherits them without change
and binds them into a single integrated baseline that is complete, coherent, and
traceable. The baseline is expressed as coverage-shaped quality records in REG-901,
requirement-, scenario-, case-, oracle-, and evidence-shaped test-model records in
REG-902, governed decisions in REG-903, and whole-volume dispositions in REG-904.

## Material requirements and their obligations

Every material requirement carried forward from Volumes 1 through 8 and from the
Volume 9 foundation resolves, in this integrated baseline, to either a governed test
obligation or a governed non-test disposition. A material requirement is never left
without a disposition. A test obligation names an object under test, an applicable
test level, an authoritative source, an institutional invariant, an expected
outcome, a negative outcome, a required evidence tier, and an independence
requirement. A governed non-test disposition records why a requirement is met by a
means other than test — for example a documentary control, a governed acceptance, or
a structural guarantee — and names the authority that accepts that disposition.

## Authoritative grounding

Every obligation in the baseline traces to an authoritative source and to a
protected institutional invariant. No obligation is self-justified. The authoritative
source is a released Volume 1 through 8 contract, obligation, or specification, or a
Volume 9 institutional invariant that itself traces to a released contract. An
obligation that cannot name an authoritative source and an institutional invariant is
not admitted to the baseline.

## Documentary boundary

Documentary completeness of the master-test baseline is distinct from implementation
completeness and from operational effectiveness. A complete baseline demonstrates
that every material requirement has a governed disposition; it demonstrates no
behaviour, no control effectiveness, no conformance, no compatibility, and no
operational readiness. Those remain the province of later, separately authorized
volumes. This baseline confers no authorization to build, to configure, to run, or
to accept.
