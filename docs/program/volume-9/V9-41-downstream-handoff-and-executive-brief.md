## Purpose

This chapter defines the downstream handoff from Volume 9 to Volumes 10, 11, and 12,
and provides the executive brief summarizing what Volume 9 delivers and what it
withholds. It is a documentary handoff only and authorizes no execution, no
sequencing, and no release.

## The handoff

Volume 9 hands off a complete, coherent, and traceable master-test definition: the
integrated master-test baseline, the master-test catalogue, the affiliation and
assurance master-test syntheses, the prerequisite matrix, the evidence and
claim-boundary standard, the defect-lifecycle standard, the coverage, independence,
acceptance, material-commitment, and release-evidence standard, the House P0
master-test matrix, and the whole-volume readiness dispositions. It hands off a
definition to be planned, provisioned, executed, and accepted downstream — never an
execution, an environment, a dataset, or an acceptance.

## Bounded downstream scope

- **Volume 10** may plan delivery and release, sequence work, and design the master
  development plan against this handoff. Volume 10 may plan; it may not infer release
  authorization from this definition, and no record here authorizes a release.
- **Volume 11** would provision and qualify environments, identities, data, and
  provider sandboxes, and would execute the defined tests under separate
  authorization. Nothing here provisions or executes.
- **Volume 12** would gather independent assurance and release evidence and would
  seek acceptance under separate authorization. Nothing here accepts.

## Executive brief

Volume 9 delivers a governed, documentary master-test definition for the House
platform: what must be tested, against what authority, judged by what oracle, with
what evidence, at what independence, and accepted by whom. Volume 9 delivers no
executable test, no environment, no dataset, no credential, no tool, no service, no
implementation, no procurement, no staffing, no cost, no pilot, no rollout, no
release, no launch, and no master development plan. Volume 9 confers
no release authorization. Documentary completeness is distinct from implementation
completeness and from operational effectiveness. The downstream volumes carry the
burden of proof; Volume 9 carries the burden of definition, and discharges it.

## Documentary boundary

This handoff transfers a definition; it transfers no authority to build, run, commit
materially, or release. No downstream action is authorized, sequenced, staffed,
costed, or scheduled by this chapter.
