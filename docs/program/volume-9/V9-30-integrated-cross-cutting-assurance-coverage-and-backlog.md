## Purpose

This chapter integrates the Package 3 cross-cutting assurance domains into a single
coverage view, records the governed evidence standard and independence requirement,
and carries the forward validation backlog. It defines what must be assured, not how
any assurance is constructed or accepted, and authorizes no execution, environment,
dataset, tool, provider testing, procurement, or release.

## Integrated coverage

The nine coverage records in register REG-901 span security, privacy and records,
accessibility, bilingual semantics, financial control, resilience and recovery,
operational assurance, provider assurance, and the House priority-zero assurance
coverage. Each requirement in register REG-902 traces to a governed institutional
invariant and to a coverage record, so that every cross-cutting obligation is bound
to both the principle it protects and the coverage dimension it satisfies.

## Evidence standard and environment binding

Operational assurance evidence is admissible only when it is bound to a named
environment, a named configuration, an artifact version, and the identity,
organization, jurisdiction, dataset, and time under which it was produced.
Documentary definition alone is never operational evidence. Every domain names the
environment class and configuration binding that a future, separately authorized
execution would require, and no such environment, configuration, or dataset is
created or provisioned by this package.

## Independence

The overall cross-cutting assurance obligation carries an independent-assurance
requirement: acceptance of the integrated posture requires independent evidence and
independent judgement rather than author self-attestation. Each domain requirement
also names the minimum independence appropriate to its risk.

## Validation backlog

The forward validation backlog in register REG-904 records the assurance context and
authorities to be confirmed, the risks that the environments, datasets, and provider
continuity paths are not yet authorized or proven, the cross-cutting assurance test
work to be defined, and the readiness disposition for the next package. Every backlog
item names an owner and a future blocking gate and moves only forward. No item in
this chapter asserts that any control, environment, dataset, or provider path is
implemented, operating, or proven; each is a documentary obligation only.
