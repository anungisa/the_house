## Purpose

This record closes Volume 9 Package 4, the Integrated Master-Test Baseline, and it
closes the whole of Volume 9. It records the disposition of Gate V9-G4, the freeze
of Package 4, the whole-volume freeze of Volume 9, and the bounded authorization
carried forward to Volume 10. Unlike the Package 1, Package 2, and Package 3 closure
records, this record additionally establishes the Volume 9 release tag on the
whole-volume corpus once the provenance amendments are recorded.

## Freeze

The Package 4 corpus is frozen under the artifact PACKAGE-9-4, and the whole Volume 9
corpus is frozen under the artifact VOLUME-9. Amendments to the frozen package and to
the frozen volume are made only through a recorded, additive amendment process; the
frozen chapters and registers are not reopened. The provenance of Package 4 is bound
in a separate pre-merge provenance-binding commit, and the original-merge and
post-merge provenance amendments are recorded separately in the V9-H and V9-H-1
amendments. After the V9-H-1 amendment is recorded, the whole-volume corpus is tagged
central-registration-volume-9-v1.0.0 on the V9-H-1 merge commit.

## Bounded authorization to Volume 10

Volume 10 is authorized to a bounded scope only: AUTHORIZED FOR DELIVERY AND RELEASE
PLANNING, SEQUENCING, AND MASTER DEVELOPMENT PLAN DEFINITION ONLY. Volume 10 remains
planning and definitional only. Executable tests, test execution, test environments,
test data, assurance services, implementation, procurement, staffing, cost
commitment, pilot, rollout, release, and launch are NOT AUTHORIZED. Volume 10 may
plan; it may not infer release authorization from this definition, and no record here
authorizes a release. Any expansion beyond that bounded scope requires a separate,
explicit authorization.

## Amendment process

Amendments to the frozen Package 4 corpus and to the frozen Volume 9 corpus are made
only through a recorded, additive amendment; the frozen chapters and registers are
not reopened. The whole-volume freeze preserves every prior gate disposition, package
freeze, and provenance amendment in Volume 9 without alteration.
