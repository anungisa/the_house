## Purpose

This chapter records the Package 1 traceability assessment, the deterministic
governance assessment, and the validation backlog carried forward. The backlog is
held in register REG-904.

## Traceability assessment

Package 1 establishes definitional traceability across the corpus: quality
attributes and institutional invariants in REG-901; the test-level taxonomy,
evidence tiers, and independence levels in REG-901; the test model in REG-902; the
decisions in REG-903; and the backlog in REG-904. Every test requirement resolves
to a governed invariant and every test case resolves to an authoritative oracle.

## Governance assessment

The deterministic Volume 9 controls assess structural and schema conformance,
cross-reference integrity, foundation coverage, provenance integrity, and Gate
V9-G1 readiness. The controls are non-authoritative: they report findings and never
confer ratification. A green assessment records that the corpus is internally
coherent and fail-closed, not that any behaviour is proven.

## Validation backlog

The validation backlog records the assumptions, risks, exceptions, defects,
waivers, remediations, retests, regressions, and readiness items carried forward.
Each item names an owner and a forward blocking gate, and no item authorizes
implementation, execution, or acceptance. The backlog is honest: it records known
gaps and deferrals rather than concealing them.

## Package 2 disposition

Package 1 dispositions the next package to a bounded scope only: the definition of
club-affiliation functional, contract, workflow, data-quality, and migration tests.
Package 2 remains test-definition only; it authorizes no construction, execution,
environment, test data, provider selection, or acceptance. Any expansion beyond
that bounded scope requires a separate, explicit authorization.
