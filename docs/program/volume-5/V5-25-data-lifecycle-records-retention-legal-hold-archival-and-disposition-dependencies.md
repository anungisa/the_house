## V5-25.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the lifecycle of governed data from creation through active use,
supersession, inactivity, archival eligibility, archival, legal hold, disposition
eligibility, disposition authorization, and disposal. It defines the dependencies that
must be satisfied before archival or disposition. The authoritative catalogue is REG-501
and the authoritative rules are REG-502. This chapter approves no retention period and no
deletion schedule and authorizes no implementation.

## V5-25.2 Lifecycle stages

This section is normative.

The governed lifecycle stages are: created, active, superseded, inactive,
archival-eligible, archived, legal-hold, disposition-eligible, disposition-authorized, and
disposed. Each data lifecycle record (REG-501, DLC-V5-001 through DLC-V5-005) names its
applicable stages, a lifecycle trigger, and a records authority. Movement between stages is
a governed decision, not an automatic effect of elapsed time.

## V5-25.3 Records authority and retention dependency

This section is normative.

No archival or disposition proceeds without a records-policy authority (CTRL-V5-011).
Retention dependency records (REG-501, RETD-V5-001 and RETD-V5-002) name the records
authority, the privacy dependency, and the archival expectation. Retention periods,
archival schedules, and disposition schedules are not approved here; their approval is a
downstream records-policy decision (REG-504, ASM-V5-004).

## V5-25.4 Legal hold

This section is normative.

A legal hold supersedes any archival or disposition eligibility regardless of elapsed time
(RULE-V5-015). While a hold is active, disposition is blocked. A hold is released only by
the authority that placed it. Disposition executed before a records-policy authority exists
is a recognized risk (REG-504, RISK-V5-006).

## V5-25.5 Disposition

This section is normative.

Disposition requires a disposition authority and a deletion authority, an absence of active
legal holds, and satisfied privacy and records dependencies. Disposition is a governed,
evidenced decision. No record in Package 3 authorizes any specific disposition.

## V5-25.6 Downstream constraints and no authorization

This section is normative.

Downstream volumes must implement retention and disposition only against a governed records
policy and must enforce legal hold over disposition. No record in this chapter approves a
retention period or deletion schedule, authorizes deletion, or authorizes implementation.
The lifecycle and disposition validation obligations remain open in REG-504
(TEST-V5-021).
