## V5-52.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter records the authoritative handoff from Volume 5 to the remaining volumes and
provides an executive data brief. It is documentary and authorizes no implementation. It records
what each downstream volume inherits and what it must still decide; it makes no downstream
decision on their behalf.

## V5-52.2 Downstream handoffs

This section is normative.

The handoff records, for each downstream volume, the inherited governed data and the obligations
that volume must still resolve:

- Volume 6 (security, privacy, compliance, accessibility, trust): inherits classifications,
  privacy authorities, purpose limitations, and disclosure restrictions; must decide security and
  privacy controls, retention periods, and disposition schedules.
- Volume 7 (experience): inherits governed projections and consumer posture, including The Button
  as a governed consumer; must decide experience contracts without owning governed lifecycle
  rules.
- Volume 8 (contracts and interfaces): inherits logical records, scope keys, and exchange
  definitions; must decide external and internal interface contracts.
- Volume 9 (verification and testing): inherits the House P0 evidence matrix, the physical
  integrity obligations reassigned to gate V9-G1, and the reassigned test classes; must decide
  and execute the verification programme.
- Volume 10 (delivery and operations): inherits physical, migration, and outbox definitions; must
  decide delivery sequencing and operational runbooks.
- Volume 11 (migration and transition): inherits the migration, reconciliation, and quarantine
  model and the resource-authorization test class reassigned to EXEC-MCG; must decide the
  migration and transition plan.

## V5-52.3 Executive data brief

This section is normative.

The executive brief records, in governance terms:

- what the governed data now authoritatively defines;
- what remains explicitly undecided;
- which authorities must still be appointed, including the records-policy authority;
- which obligations block which downstream gates;
- that no implementation, procurement, retention period, or master development plan is authorized.

## V5-52.4 Handoff posture

This section is normative.

Each downstream volume inherits a defined, controlled, and traceable data baseline and a
register of open obligations bound to its gates. This handoff authorizes no implementation and
transfers no governed authority to any experience layer, custodian, or external system.
