## V5-53.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter records the integrated traceability assessment and the final closure assessment for
Volume 5. It is documentary and authorizes no implementation. It aggregates the deterministic
closure projections produced by the governance tooling and records the closure conclusion. This
assessment is governed by decision ADR-V5-049, which records that closure projections are
non-authoritative.

## V5-53.2 Integrated traceability

This section is normative.

The assessment demonstrates conceptual-to-logical-to-physical traceability across the full
Volume 5 corpus: every conceptual entity traces to one or more logical records, every logical
record traces to one or more documentary physical structures, and every physical structure names
a resolvable logical source and owning domain. Every rule and control traces to a decision or a
governed record, every decision traces to a chapter, and every backlog obligation names an owner
and a downstream blocking gate. The traceability projection is produced deterministically by the
closure tooling and is recorded as non-authoritative.

## V5-53.3 Closure assessment

This section is normative.

The closure assessment answers the central question of Volume 5: whether the complete data
definition is sufficiently authoritative, consistent, traceable, controlled, and
implementation-neutral to govern security, privacy, experience, contracts, testing, delivery,
migration, and operations in the remaining volumes. The assessment concludes that it is, subject
to the open obligations recorded in REG-504 and the unresolved-readiness register, each of which
names an owner and a downstream blocking gate and none of which names the now-passing Gate
V5-G5.

## V5-53.4 Closure projections

This section is normative.

The deterministic closure tooling produces the following non-authoritative projections, which
support but do not replace the governed registers:

- identifier counts;
- domain authority and stewardship coverage;
- conceptual-logical-physical traceability;
- identity, relationship, and scope analysis;
- temporal, versioning, and lineage analysis;
- evidence, decision, financial, and activation analysis;
- reference, quality, records, and lifecycle analysis;
- physical integrity and index analysis;
- migration, reconciliation, and exchange analysis;
- projection, analytics, and export analysis;
- House P0 data coverage;
- downstream handoff coverage;
- unresolved-readiness register;
- Volume 5 closure report.

These projections are rebuildable from the governed registers and authorize no implementation.
