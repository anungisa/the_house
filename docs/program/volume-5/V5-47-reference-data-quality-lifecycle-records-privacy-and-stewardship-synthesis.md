## V5-47.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates the reference-data, quality, lifecycle, records, privacy, and
stewardship definitions of Volume 5 into a single synthesis. It is documentary and authorizes no
implementation. It establishes no retention periods, deletion schedules, or legal conclusions.
It consolidates the Package 3 data-lifecycle, stewardship, and reference-data model.

## V5-47.2 Consolidated subjects

This section is normative.

The synthesis consolidates:

- master, reference, transactional, evidentiary, audit, derived, analytical, external-authority,
  and transitional classifications;
- controlled vocabularies;
- stable identifiers;
- bilingual labels;
- quality-rule lifecycle;
- stewardship issues;
- exceptions and remediation;
- archival eligibility;
- legal hold;
- disposition dependency;
- purpose limitation;
- minimization;
- disclosure and analytics restrictions.

Each subject resolves to governed catalogue and rule records already ratified in Package 3.

## V5-47.3 Governed control families

This section is normative.

For every governed control family the synthesis identifies the control objective, information
domain, authority, steward, trigger, evidence, exception authority, correction route, privacy
dependency, records dependency, future validation, and implementation status. Every quality rule
names a correction authority; every data-lifecycle record names a records authority; every data
issue names a resolution authority; every reconciliation context names a conflict authority; and
every data use names a permitted purpose. Controlled vocabularies carry a version posture and
bilingual semantics.

## V5-47.4 Retention, hold, and disposition dependency

This section is normative.

Retention periods, deletion schedules, legal holds, archival eligibility, and disposition remain
dependent on a properly appointed records-policy authority. This synthesis records those
dependencies as pending; it establishes no retention period, no deletion schedule, and no legal
conclusion, and it authorizes no implementation. The retention and disposition validation remains
an open backlog obligation carried forward to a downstream gate.
