## V5-30.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the governance model for data operations, observability, issue
evidence, and stewardship measures. It defines these as governed concepts and future
obligations, not as implemented operational controls. The authoritative catalogue is
REG-501 and the authoritative rules are REG-502. This chapter authorizes no implementation
and claims no operational proof.

## V5-30.2 Stewardship measures

This section is normative.

A stewardship measure (REG-501, SMEA-V5-001 through SMEA-V5-004) names an accountable owner,
a baseline status, a target status, and a future operational-proof requirement. A stewardship
measure asserts no operational assurance until future operational evidence is provided
(CTRL-V5-012). No measure concept is treated as an implemented assurance.

## V5-30.3 Observability and issue evidence

This section is normative.

Observability of governed data is expressed as the future ability to detect data issues,
measure quality, and evidence corrections. Issue evidence is the retained record supporting
a data issue and its resolution. Evidence is held under the custody and evidentiary rules of
Packages 1 and 2. This chapter records the requirement for observability and issue evidence;
it does not implement any monitoring, alerting, or dashboard.

## V5-30.4 Data operations

This section is normative.

Data operations describe the future governed handling of intake, correction, reconciliation,
retention, and disposition. Operational assurance is a future obligation validated against
future operational evidence. No operational proof, reconciliation proof, or compliance proof
is claimed here.

## V5-30.5 Downstream constraints and no authorization

This section is normative.

Downstream volumes must validate stewardship measures against operational evidence before
claiming assurance and must not treat a measure concept as an implemented control. No record
in this chapter authorizes implementation, tooling selection, or procurement. The
stewardship-measure operational-proof validation obligation remains open in REG-504
(TEST-V5-025).
