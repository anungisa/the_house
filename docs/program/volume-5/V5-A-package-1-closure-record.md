## V5-A.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This closure record consolidates Volume 5, Package 1 — the Data Governance and
Conceptual Information Foundation — records the Gate V5-G1 disposition, authorizes
Package 2, and freezes the Package 1 corpus. It authorizes no implementation.

## V5-A.2 Package 1 deliverables

This section is normative.

Package 1 delivers, at version 1.0.0:

- V5-00 volume control, inheritance, and data-definition authority;
- V5-01 data doctrine, principles, and governance model;
- V5-02 information domain catalogue and authority matrix;
- V5-03 conceptual identity, organization, party, and relationship model;
- V5-04 affiliation, season, policy, requirement, and evidence conceptual model;
- V5-05 tenant, jurisdiction, season, and information-scope model;
- V5-06 temporal truth, versioning, provenance, and lineage model;
- V5-07 data classification, minimization, access, and records dependencies;
- V5-08 data quality, correction, reconciliation, and stewardship model;
- V5-09 projection, reporting, analytics, export, and derived-data model;
- V5-10 data validation backlog and downstream constraints; and
- the Volume 5 registers REG-500 through REG-505.

## V5-A.3 Gate V5-G1 disposition

This section is normative.

Gate V5-G1 is dispositioned DATA_GOVERNANCE_FOUNDATION_READY (APP-V5-013). The
disposition affirms that the corrected Volume 4 release provenance is inherited;
data-definition authority and amendment rules are controlled; data principles and
governance responsibilities are defined; information domains carry explicit
authority, stewardship, and system-of-record posture; conceptual identities and
relationships are defined without physical schema design; organization,
jurisdiction, season, and affiliation scope are explicit; policy, requirement,
evidence, submission, decision, reconciliation, and activation relationships are
defined; temporal truth, versioning, provenance, and lineage are represented;
classification, minimization, access, and records dependencies are defined; data
quality, correction, identity resolution, and reconciliation are distinguished;
projections, analytics, reports, and exports remain non-authoritative; unresolved
assumptions carry owners, evidence requirements, and future gates; Volume 4
architecture constraints trace into Volume 5; no record authorizes implementation;
no physical artifact is created; and Package 1 receives line-level review with a
separate freeze commit.

## V5-A.4 Package 2 authorization

This section is normative.

With Gate V5-G1 dispositioned ready, Volume 5 Package 2 is authorized to proceed on
the data-governance foundation established here. Package 2 authorization is limited
to continued data-definition and validation work and does not authorize
implementation, physical design, procurement, or delivery sequencing.

## V5-A.5 Freeze

This section is normative.

Package 1 (PACKAGE-5-1) is frozen at version 1.0.0 across all deliverables
(APP-V5-014). After freeze, changes to Package 1 require the recorded amendment
process (V5-00.5). The freeze is committed separately from authoring, satisfying the
final Gate V5-G1 condition.
