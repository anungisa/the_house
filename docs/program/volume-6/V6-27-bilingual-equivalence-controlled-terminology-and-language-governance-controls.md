## V6-27.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the bilingual-equivalence control model for the affiliation
service: the requirement that governed meaning be equivalent across both official
languages, the governance of controlled terminology through language-neutral
identifiers, and the verification of equivalence. It defines controls only. It writes
no production translation, selects no term as authoritative content, and authorizes no
implementation.

## V6-27.2 Semantic equivalence, not literal translation

This section is normative.

Bilingual equivalence requires that the governed meaning conveyed in each official
language be the same, not that the text be a literal word-for-word translation. Each
bilingual obligation records the equivalent concept and the semantic-equivalence
requirement it protects. Equivalence is a property of meaning and governed outcome; a
literal rendering that changes governed meaning is not equivalent.

## V6-27.3 Controlled terminology

This section is normative.

Governed concepts are identified by a language-neutral identifier — a canonical
concept identifier that is independent of any language expression. Language
expressions in English and French attach to the canonical concept as equivalent
renderings. Where a term is deprecated, the control records the deprecated expression
and its replacement expression so terminology change is governed. The controlled
terminology recorded here is a governance identifier set, not production content or an
authoritative glossary.

## V6-27.4 Language-neutral identifiers

This section is normative.

Governed identity, state, and decision concepts must be represented by
language-neutral identifiers so that no official language is privileged as the system
of record for meaning. A language-neutral identifier carries no user-facing text and
is never treated as a translation. The control records the requirement for such
identifiers; it defines no identifier scheme for implementation.

## V6-27.5 Verification of equivalence

This section is normative.

Every bilingual obligation records a verification method so that semantic equivalence
can be independently reviewed. No equivalence is claimed on the strength of this
chapter, and no content pair is deemed equivalent without recorded verification
evidence produced under a future gate.

## V6-27.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It writes no production translation,
publishes no glossary, selects no authoritative content, and claims no bilingual
equivalence or official-language compliance. Every record introduced by this chapter
remains `authorizes_implementation: false` and `implementation_status:
NOT_IMPLEMENTED_OR_NOT_PROVEN`.
