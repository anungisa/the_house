## V6-E.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This closure record consolidates Volume 6, Package 3 — the Compliance,
Accessibility, Bilingual-Equivalence, and Inclusive-Service Control Model —
records the Gate V6-G3 disposition, authorizes Package 4, and freezes the
Package 3 corpus. It authorizes no implementation.

## V6-E.2 Inheritance

This section is normative.

## V6-E.6 Gate V6-G3 disposition

This section is normative.

Gate V6-G3 is dispositioned
COMPLIANCE_ACCESSIBILITY_AND_INCLUSIVE_SERVICE_CONTROL_MODEL_READY (APP-V6-041).
The disposition affirms that the compliance applicability model is defined; the
obligation-source families and authority model are defined and applicability
requires evidence; policy, control, evidence, exception, and assurance are mapped
without writing executable policy or granting exceptions; records controls define
a records authority with retention, legal-hold, archival, and disposition
requirements and set no retention period; legal hold supersedes disposition;
financial and affiliation authority are separated with segregation of duties and
required approval and no funds are moved; accessibility is defined by user need
and workflow with a verification method and no interface is built; accessible
content, forms, documents, and evidence carry alternative representations and no
content is authored; bilingual equivalence is semantic and language-neutral with
controlled terminology and no production translation is written; inclusive-service
accommodation and support preserve equivalent outcomes with governed exception
paths and no accommodation is granted; verification, defect classification,
remediation, and retest are defined and no verification is run; conformance
claims require evidence and none is claimed; every control objective carries an
owner, required evidence, and a future blocking gate; no record authorizes
implementation; every record is not-implemented or not-proven; no executable
compliance, records, retention, disposition, financial, accessibility, content,
translation, accommodation, verification, or remediation artifact is created; no
legal conclusion, conformance, operational proof, or independent assurance is
claimed; and Package 3 receives line-level review with a separate freeze commit.

This section is normative.

With Gate V6-G3 dispositioned ready, Volume 6 Package 4 is authorized to proceed
