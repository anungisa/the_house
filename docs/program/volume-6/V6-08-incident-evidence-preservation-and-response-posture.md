## V6-08.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the incident families, the evidence-preservation posture, and
the response intent that must govern The House. It authorizes no monitoring,
detection, or incident-response implementation.

## V6-08.2 Incident families

This section is normative.

The incident families recorded in REG-602 are: data breach and unauthorized
disclosure (INC-V6-001); integrity and tampering incident (INC-V6-002); and
availability and continuity incident (INC-V6-003). Each incident family names a
trigger, an evidence-preservation posture, containment and recovery objectives, and
a decision authority.

## V6-08.3 Evidence preservation

This section is normative.

Every incident family requires preservation of relevant logs, audit records, and
forensic evidence with integrity. Evidence preservation is a governed obligation so
that incidents can be investigated and accountability maintained. Evidence
preservation depends on the integrity and logging control objectives (CTRL-V6-006,
CTRL-V6-005).

## V6-08.4 Response posture

This section is normative.

Response intent is recorded as containment and recovery objectives with a named
decision authority. Operational ownership, severity classification, breach
notification, and post-incident review are recorded as pending later volumes. The
monitoring and detection control objective (CTRL-V6-010) records the intent to make
misuse and compromise detectable.

## V6-08.5 Privacy and notification dependency

This section is normative.

Data-breach incidents must assess data-subject notification obligations
(INC-V6-001). Notification obligations are recorded as pending validation and are
not asserted as satisfied.

## V6-08.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no monitoring, detection, alerting, or incident-response
implementation. It records incident families, evidence-preservation posture, and
response intent only. Future validation (TEST-V6-004) must prove incident response
preserves evidence and meets containment and recovery objectives.
