## V6-A.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This closure record consolidates Volume 6, Package 1 — the Security, Privacy,
Compliance, Accessibility, and Trust Foundation — records the Gate V6-G1
disposition, authorizes Package 2, and freezes the Package 1 corpus. It authorizes
no implementation.

## V6-A.2 Inheritance

This section is normative.

Volume 6 inherits, without modification, the frozen Volume 0 foundation and the
released baselines of Volumes 1 through 5. The inherited data baseline is the
released Volume 5 governed-data foundation, `central-registration-volume-5-v1.0.0`.
The machine-readable provenance of this package is completed by the provenance
amendment (V6-B) after mainline merge.

## V6-A.3 Package 1 deliverables

This section is normative.

## V6-A.4 Gate V6-G1 disposition

This section is normative.

Gate V6-G1 is dispositioned TRUST_AND_PROTECTION_FOUNDATION_READY (APP-V6-013). The
disposition affirms that the released Volume 5 governed-data baseline is inherited;
protection-definition authority and amendment rules are controlled; protection
doctrine and trust principles are defined; assets, actors, trust boundaries, and
classification carry explicit authority; every trust boundary is named by a threat
or abuse scenario; every threat resolves preventive, detective, and corrective
intent with an owner; the authorization baseline is resource-aware and fails closed;
privacy purposes map to inherited information domains and disclosure authority
without setting a retention period; compliance obligations carry applicability,
authority, control reference, evidence, and future gates; accessibility and
bilingual obligations carry verification methods and semantic equivalence; incident
families preserve evidence and name containment and recovery objectives; assurance
requirements and the validation backlog carry owners, evidence, classification, and
future gates; no record authorizes implementation; every record is not-implemented
or not-proven; no executable protection, IAM, cryptographic, or infrastructure
artifact is created; no legal, security, privacy, or accessibility conformance,
operational proof, or independent assurance is claimed; and Package 1 receives
line-level review with a separate freeze commit.

## V6-A.5 Package 2 authorization

This section is normative.

With Gate V6-G1 dispositioned ready, Volume 6 Package 2 is authorized to proceed on
the protection and trust foundation established here. Package 2 authorization is
limited to continued obligation-definition and validation work and does not
authorize control implementation, executable policy, identity or access
configuration, infrastructure, procurement, or delivery sequencing.

## V6-A.6 Freeze

This section is normative.

Package 1 (PACKAGE-6-1) is frozen at version 1.0.0 across all deliverables
(APP-V6-014). After freeze, changes to Package 1 require the recorded amendment
process (V6-00.5). The freeze is committed separately from authoring, satisfying the
final Gate V6-G1 condition.
