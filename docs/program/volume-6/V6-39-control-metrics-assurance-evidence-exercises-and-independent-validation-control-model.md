## V6-39.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the control model for control metrics, assurance evidence, exercises,
and independent validation for the affiliation service. It defines the obligations that must
exist before any operational, monitoring, or assurance claim may be made. It defines a control
model only. It defines no metric value, runs no exercise, and authorizes no implementation.

## V6-39.2 Control metrics and their interpretation limits

This section is normative.

A control metric is a governed measure intended to indicate whether a control is operating.
Each assurance requirement of the metric family records an objective, the scope it measures,
and an operational-proof limitation stating that a metric indicates operation but does not by
itself prove effectiveness. No metric value or threshold is defined by this chapter.

## V6-39.3 Exercises validate obligations, not readiness claims

This section is normative.

An exercise is a governed rehearsal of an obligation such as incident response or recovery.
Each exercise-related assurance requirement records a scope and a required environment and a
defect-treatment statement so that defects found in an exercise are treated as findings
requiring remediation, not as proof of readiness. No exercise is designed or run here.

## V6-39.4 Assurance evidence has a producer and an acceptance authority

This section is normative.

Assurance evidence must have a defined producer and a defined authority that accepts it. Each
assurance requirement records an evidence producer, evidence that is accepted, and evidence
that is prohibited from being treated as assurance, so that the source and standing of
assurance evidence is unambiguous. No assurance evidence is produced or accepted here.

## V6-39.5 Independent validation is distinct from self-attestation

This section is normative.

Self-attestation by the party operating a control is not independent assurance. Each assurance
requirement records an assurance classification distinguishing self-attested evidence from
evidence requiring independent validation, and a period dependency describing that assurance
is time-bounded and must be renewed. This volume is itself self-attested and author-verified,
and claims no independent assurance.

## V6-39.6 No operational or assurance claim is made

This section is normative.

Recording metric, exercise, and assurance obligations makes no claim that any control is
operating, effective, exercised, or independently validated. Every such claim is deferred to a
future gate with evidence produced and accepted under this model. Assurance obligations are
recorded as defined and unproven.

## V6-39.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It defines no metric value, threshold, or
dashboard, designs or runs no exercise, produces or accepts no assurance evidence, and engages
no assessor or auditor. It makes no claim of operational readiness, control effectiveness, or
independent assurance. Every record introduced by this chapter remains
`authorizes_implementation: false` and `implementation_status: NOT_IMPLEMENTED_OR_NOT_PROVEN`.
