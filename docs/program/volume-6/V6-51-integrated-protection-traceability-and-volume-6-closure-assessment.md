## V6-51.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter records the final deterministic integrity review of the Volume 6
corpus and the whole-volume closure assessment. It describes the final-closure
tooling and its projections, states the closure result, and records the
constraints closure imposes. It authorizes no implementation and closes no
validation item beyond confirming the integrity of the corpus.

## V6-51.2 Deterministic final-closure tooling

This section is normative.

The final-closure tooling projects the whole Volume 6 corpus deterministically from
the registers and chapters and writes non-authoritative machine-readable
projections and a closure report. The registers and chapters remain authoritative;
the projections carry no authority and make no decision. The tooling reports any
unmapped, ownerless, gate-inconsistent, classification-missing, leakage-bearing, or
implementation-authorizing record as a blocking error, and the corpus is closed
only when the tooling reports zero blocking errors and zero implementation
authorizations.

## V6-51.3 Projected closure dimensions

This section is normative.

The final-closure tooling projects the following dimensions:

- identifier counts across all registers;
- asset authority and classification coverage;
- boundary, threat, and control traceability;
- identity, authorization, and privilege analysis;
- privacy, evidence, records, and disclosure analysis;
- compliance, accessibility, bilingual, and accommodation analysis;
- incident, vulnerability, notification, and communications analysis;
- resilience, backup, restore, recovery, and continuity analysis;
- provider trust, exit, return, and deletion analysis;
- metrics, exercises, operational-proof, and assurance analysis;
- House P0 protection coverage;
- downstream-handoff coverage; and
- the unresolved-readiness register.

## V6-51.4 Closure assessment result

This section is normative.

The final-closure assessment confirms that every material protection capability is
traceable to a protected asset or right, an owner, and required evidence; that every
authority separation is preserved; that every House P0 finding is defined with a
future test class and future gate; that every downstream volume receives its
transferred constraints; that every unresolved item is owned, evidence-bound, and
routed to a future destination; that no active record references a completed gate;
that every record is marked not-implemented or not-proven; and that no record
authorizes implementation. The corpus is internally consistent, traceable, and
evidence-disciplined.

## V6-51.5 Consolidation discipline

This section is normative.

The closure assessment confirms that Package 5 is a consolidation. It introduces no
protection capability, control, or obligation beyond those established and frozen in
Packages 1 through 4, and it represents no consolidation as implementation,
remediation, or proof.

## V6-51.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter implements no control; creates no executable artifact; makes no
operational-readiness, conformance, or assurance claim; produces no evidence; and
authorizes no implementation. The projections it describes are non-authoritative.
