## V6-09.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter records the assurance requirements, the validation backlog, and the
provider-assurance obligations that must be satisfied before any protection control
is relied upon. It claims no independent assurance or operational proof.

## V6-09.2 Assurance requirements

This section is normative.

The assurance requirements recorded in REG-602 are: independent security validation
(ASSR-V6-001); privacy impact assessment (ASSR-V6-002); accessibility conformance
validation (ASSR-V6-003); and provider-assurance evidence (ASSR-V6-004). Each names
an owner, required evidence, an assurance classification, and a future gate. Every
assurance requirement is recorded with a pending validation status; none is
satisfied.

## V6-09.3 Provider assurance

This section is normative.

External providers gain reliance only through validated assurance and contractual
data-protection obligations (CTRL-V6-009). Provider data exposure (THREAT-V6-005)
and unverified provider assurance (RISK-V6-002) are open risks until third-party
assurance evidence is validated. No provider is selected, and no procurement is
authorized, by this chapter.

## V6-09.4 Validation backlog

This section is normative.

The validation backlog recorded in REG-604 defines the future tests that will prove
protection obligations: authorization fail-closed validation (TEST-V6-001); privacy
minimization validation (TEST-V6-002); accessibility and bilingual equivalence
validation (TEST-V6-003); and incident-response and evidence-preservation validation
(TEST-V6-004). Each names an owner and a future gate.

## V6-09.5 No assurance claim

This section is normative.

Volume 6 claims no independent assurance, operational proof, security
certification, privacy compliance, or accessibility conformance (ADR-V6-004). All
such states are recorded as pending validation.

## V6-09.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no assurance activity, vendor selection, procurement, or
control. It records assurance requirements, provider-assurance obligations, and the
validation backlog only.
