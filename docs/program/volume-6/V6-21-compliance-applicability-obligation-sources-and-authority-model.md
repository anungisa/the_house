## V6-21.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the compliance-applicability model for the affiliation
service: the families of obligation sources that may govern the service, the
authority that owns each source, and the applicability states an obligation may
occupy before it may be relied upon. It defines a control model only. It reaches
no legal conclusion, asserts no compliance or conformance, sets no retention
period, and authorizes no implementation.

## V6-21.2 Obligation-source families

This section is normative.

Compliance obligations governing the affiliation service are categorized into
source families for governance and traceability. The recognized families are
organizational policy, privacy, information security, records management,
financial control, accessibility, official-language and bilingual service,
contractual, audit and assurance, incident and breach, continuity and recovery,
and governance approval. Assigning a source family to an obligation records where
the obligation originates and which authority owns its validation. The family
assignment is a control-model classification; it is not a determination that the
obligation applies, that it has been satisfied, or that any legal instrument has
been interpreted.

## V6-21.3 Applicability states

This section is normative.

Every recorded compliance obligation carries an applicability status drawn from a
fixed set: applicable and confirmed by evidence; applicability validation pending;
policy validation pending; legal validation pending; contract validation pending;
and not applicable with recorded evidence. An obligation may be relied upon only
when its applicability is confirmed by validation evidence owned by the accountable
authority. Until then, the obligation is recorded as pending. No applicability
determination in this volume constitutes legal advice or a legal conclusion, and no
statute, regulation, standard, or contract is interpreted here.

## V6-21.4 Authority model

This section is normative.

Each obligation source family names an accountable authority owner responsible for
confirming applicability, commissioning validation, and dispositioning exceptions.
Authority to determine that an obligation applies, to confirm that it has been
satisfied, or to accept an exception rests with the named authority owner and not
with the platform, with any domain module, or with any experience layer. The
authority model separates obligation ownership from control operation and from any
future implementation authority.

## V6-21.5 Applicability requires evidence

This section is normative.

An obligation is neither in force nor discharged by its presence in the register.
Applicability, satisfaction, and exception each require evidence produced and
validated by the accountable authority under a future gate. Absence of evidence is
recorded as a pending state and never as compliance, conformance, or exemption.

## V6-21.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It reaches no legal conclusion,
interprets no statute, regulation, standard, or contract, asserts no compliance or
conformance, sets no retention period, selects no counsel, auditor, or provider,
and configures no policy or workflow. Every record introduced by this chapter
remains `authorizes_implementation: false` and `implementation_status:
NOT_IMPLEMENTED_OR_NOT_PROVEN`.
