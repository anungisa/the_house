## V6-45.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates the compliance-applicability, financial-control,
segregation-of-duties, accessibility, bilingual-equivalence, inclusive-service,
accommodation, and verification-and-remediation definitions established in Package
3 (V6-21 through V6-29) into a single compliance and inclusive-service synthesis.
It is a consolidation and introduces no new obligation. It authorizes no
implementation.

## V6-45.2 Distinctions preserved

This section is normative.

The synthesis preserves, without weakening, the following governed distinctions: a
policy definition is not a control operation; an automated scan is not a completed
accessible task; conformance is not usable service; a translation is not a governed
semantic equivalence; an accommodation is not a weakened authority; and a
remediation claim is not a validated retest. Each distinction is recorded as a
decision in REG-603 and preserved unchanged.

## V6-45.3 Obligation-family record

This section is normative.

For every obligation family consolidated here, the synthesis records the following
attributes by reference to the frozen source: the applicable-source status; the
authority owner; the control owner; the user or institutional need; the affected
workflow; the required evidence; the permitted verification; any exception
authority; any remediation dependency; the retest requirement; the
operational-proof dependency; the independent-assurance dependency; and the
implementation status. The deterministic final-closure tooling (V6-51) projects
this record set and reports any obligation family missing a required attribute as a
blocking error.

## V6-45.4 Compliance applicability and financial controls

This section is normative.

Compliance obligations carry an applicability status that may require policy,
legal, or contract validation, and no obligation is treated as confirmed without
that validation. Financially relevant decision context (ASSET-V6-012) and
financial-status data (ASSET-V6-006) are governed by the financial-decision
auditability and segregation-of-duties definitions of V6-24, under which financial
authority is separated from affiliation authority and no actor approves its own
financial decision. No compliance conclusion is reached and no financial control is
operated.

## V6-45.5 Accessibility and inclusive service

This section is normative.

Accessibility obligations carry a user need, an affected workflow, and a
verification method, and preserve the rights to complete governed workflows without
exclusion (RIGHT-V6-006) and to accommodation preserving an equivalent outcome
(RIGHT-V6-007). An accommodation preserves the equivalent governed outcome and
never weakens a governed authority. No accessibility conformance is claimed and no
accessible task is completed here.

## V6-45.6 Bilingual equivalence

This section is normative.

Bilingual obligations preserve governed semantic equivalence between official
languages for controlled terminology and language-neutral concepts
(ASSET-V6-016), as defined in V6-27. A translation is accepted only where it
preserves the governed semantic equivalence of the canonical concept. No bilingual
validation is claimed and no content is translated here.

## V6-45.7 Verification, remediation, and retest

This section is normative.

Every consolidated obligation records the permitted verification method, any
remediation dependency, and the retest requirement defined in V6-29. A remediation
claim is not accepted as resolved until the required retest is defined and, in a
future volume, performed and evidenced. No verification, remediation, or retest is
performed here.

## V6-45.8 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter implements no compliance, financial, accessibility, bilingual,
inclusive-service, accommodation, or remediation control; reaches no compliance or
legal conclusion; makes no accessibility-conformance or bilingual-validation claim;
performs no scan, translation, verification, remediation, or retest; sets no
service level; makes no operational-readiness or assurance claim; and authorizes no
implementation.
