## V6-34.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the control model for breach assessment, notification,
incident communications, and disclosure for the affiliation service. It defines the
obligations that must exist before any notification or communication capability may
be operated. It defines a control model only. It reaches no legal conclusion, sets no
notification timeline, drafts no notice, and authorizes no implementation.

## V6-34.2 A breach is a governed determination, not an incident label

This section is normative.

Whether an incident constitutes a breach requiring notification is a governed
determination distinct from the incident classification in V6-33. Each notification
obligation records a breach-applicability status that begins pending legal and factual
validation. An incident is not a breach by default, and a breach is not established by
this volume. No breach is determined here.

## V6-34.3 Notification obligations and their dependencies

This section is normative.

Each notification obligation names the affected party a notification would concern, a
potential notification obligation describing the family of duty that may apply, and the
legal-validation and contractual-validation dependencies that must be satisfied before
any notification duty is confirmed. These dependencies are recorded as pending; no
statute, regulation, or contract is interpreted and no notification duty is asserted.

## V6-34.4 Minimum-necessary disclosure and communication authority

This section is normative.

Any communication arising from an incident or breach must disclose only the
minimum necessary information and must be issued under a named communication authority.
Disclosure that exceeds the minimum necessary, or that is issued without the governing
authority, is prohibited by the model. No communication is drafted, approved, or issued
by this chapter.

## V6-34.5 Accessible, bilingual, and records-bearing communications

This section is normative.

Any communication issued to affected parties carries a language-and-accessibility
requirement so that it is accessible and semantically equivalent across official
languages, and a records requirement so that the fact and content of the communication
are recorded as governed evidence. These requirements inherit the accessibility and
bilingual obligations of Package 3. They are defined as obligations; no communication
is produced here.

## V6-34.6 Separation of notification from response

This section is normative.

The obligation to assess and, where required, notify is distinct from the obligation to
contain and resolve an incident. Notification does not resolve an incident, and incident
closure does not discharge a notification obligation. Each is owned and evidenced
separately. This chapter records that separation and discharges neither obligation.

## V6-34.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation. It reaches no legal conclusion, interprets no
statute, regulation, or contract, determines no breach, sets no notification timeline or
threshold, drafts or issues no notice or communication, and names no counsel or regulator.
It makes no claim that any notification duty applies or is discharged. Every record
introduced by this chapter remains `authorizes_implementation: false` and
`implementation_status: NOT_IMPLEMENTED_OR_NOT_PROVEN`.
