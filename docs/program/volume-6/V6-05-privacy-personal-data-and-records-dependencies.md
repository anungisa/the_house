## V6-05.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the privacy baseline for personal data: the purposes for which
personal data is processed, the minimization principle, and the records
dependencies. It claims no privacy compliance and sets no retention period or
destruction schedule.

## V6-05.2 Processing purposes

This section is normative.

Personal data is processed only for stated, governed purposes, recorded in REG-602:
affiliation administration (PRIV-V6-001); identity and representative processing
(PRIV-V6-002); financial-status processing (PRIV-V6-003); communications and
support (PRIV-V6-004); and audit and accountability (PRIV-V6-005). Each purpose maps
to inherited Volume 5 information domains and names a disclosure authority. Legal
basis, notice, and privacy validation are recorded as pending.

## V6-05.3 Minimization

This section is normative.

Personal data must be collected and used under the minimum-necessary principle
(CTRL-V6-008). Each processing purpose records that only data necessary for that
purpose is processed. Minimization is a governed obligation whose proof is deferred
to future validation (TEST-V6-002).

## V6-05.4 Records and retention dependencies

This section is normative.

Volume 6 sets no retention period and no destruction schedule. Where a processing
purpose implies retention — for example, audit and accountability processing
(PRIV-V6-005) — the purpose names a records dependency on a validated records and
retention authority. The records-and-retention obligation (COMP-V6-002) is
future-gated pending that authority (ADR-V6-003).

## V6-05.5 Data-subject rights dependency

This section is normative.

The processing purposes depend on the data-subject rights recorded in V6-03:
notice and transparency, access, correction, and complaint. Fulfilment of these
rights is a governed obligation whose control design is deferred to later volumes.

## V6-05.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no privacy workflow, data-subject-request mechanism,
retention period, or destruction schedule, and claims no privacy compliance. It
records privacy purposes, minimization, and records dependencies only.
