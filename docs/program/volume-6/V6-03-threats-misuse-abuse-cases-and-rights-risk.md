## V6-03.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter records the threats, misuse and abuse cases, and rights risks that
the protection obligations must address. It defines risk and control intent only;
it authorizes no control implementation.

## V6-03.2 Threats

This section is normative.

The threats recorded in REG-601 are: cross-tenant governed-data access
(THREAT-V6-001); credential compromise and impersonation (THREAT-V6-002); evidence
or audit tampering (THREAT-V6-003); privileged-access misuse (THREAT-V6-004); and
provider data exposure (THREAT-V6-005).

Each threat names an affected asset or right, a trust boundary, and a preventive,
detective, and corrective objective, together with an accountable owner. These
objectives are governed intent for future controls; no control is implemented or
proven by this chapter.

## V6-03.3 Misuse and abuse cases

This section is normative.

The abuse cases recorded in REG-601 are: reviewer over-access beyond assignment
(ABUSE-V6-001) and re-identification through projection or export
(ABUSE-V6-002). Abuse cases capture misuse by otherwise-authorized actors and
drive least-privilege and minimization obligations.

## V6-03.4 Rights risk

This section is normative.

The rights recorded in REG-601 are: the right to notice and transparency
(RIGHT-V6-001); the right of access (RIGHT-V6-002); the right to correction
(RIGHT-V6-003); the right to complaint and escalation (RIGHT-V6-004); and the right
to accessible and bilingual service (RIGHT-V6-005). Each right names a protected
outcome and the control implication that a future control must satisfy. Rights are
protection targets on the same footing as technical assets.

## V6-03.5 Coverage discipline

This section is normative.

Every trust boundary is covered by at least one threat or abuse scenario, and
every threat resolves preventive, detective, and corrective intent. The
non-authoritative deterministic controls (V6-10) verify this coverage over the
governed corpus.

## V6-03.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no control, monitoring, detection, or response capability.
It records risk and control intent only.
