## V6-06.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the compliance-obligation framework: how obligations are
recorded, their applicability determined, and their evidence and future gates
tracked. It claims no legal conformance and authorizes no implementation.

## V6-06.2 Obligation structure

This section is normative.

Every obligation recorded in REG-602 names an applicability status, an authority
owner, a control-objective reference, required evidence, and a future blocking
gate. Policy obligations (OBL-V6-001 personal-data protection; OBL-V6-002 access
control) and compliance obligations (COMP-V6-001 Canadian privacy law; COMP-V6-002
records and retention; COMP-V6-003 accessibility law) share this structure.

## V6-06.3 Applicability

This section is normative.

Applicability is recorded explicitly and honestly. Where legal or policy
applicability is not yet validated, the obligation carries a pending applicability
status (for example, legal-validation-pending or policy-validation-pending) and an
independent validation requirement. No obligation is claimed as satisfied.

## V6-06.4 Governance-approval control

This section is normative.

Protection, privacy, and assurance obligations must be dispositioned through the
governed gate sequence before reliance (CTRL-V6-011). The governance-approval
control objective binds obligations to the gate sequence and to executive
acceptance.

## V6-06.5 No conformance claim

This section is normative.

Volume 6 claims no legal, privacy, or accessibility conformance (ADR-V6-004). All
compliance obligations are recorded as pending validation with independent review
requirements and future gates. Applicability determination and legal review are
future work owned by the named authorities.

## V6-06.6 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no compliance control, legal determination, or conformance
claim. It records the obligation framework and applicability posture only.
