## V6-30.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter records the Package 3 traceability of the compliance, records,
accessibility, bilingual-equivalence, and inclusive-service control model, the
validation backlog it leaves open, and the constraints it imposes on downstream work.
It authorizes no implementation and closes no validation item.

## V6-30.2 Completed-gate reference review

This section is normative.

Before the Gate V6-G3 disposition, every active future-gate reference in the
protection registers was inspected. No active record in REG-601, REG-602, or REG-604
references a completed gate (Gate V6-G1, Gate V6-G2, or Gate V6-G3); all active
future-gate references resolve to later volumes. No additive gate reassignment was
required, and no superseded-gate history was created. The frozen Package 1 and Package
2 chapters were not reopened.

## V6-30.3 Traceability

This section is normative.

Every Package 3 obligation traces to an authority owner, an applicability status, a
control objective, required evidence, and a future blocking gate. Every accessibility
and bilingual obligation traces to a user need or content asset, a verification method,
and a protected right. Every control objective traces to an owner, required evidence,
and a future blocking gate. The deterministic compliance-accessibility tooling projects
this traceability and reports any unmapped, gate-inconsistent, or leakage-bearing
record as a blocking error.

## V6-30.4 Validation backlog

This section is normative.

The following validations remain open and are recorded in REG-604: compliance
applicability confirmation, records-authority and retention-schedule validation,
financial-control segregation validation, accessibility conformance verification,
bilingual-equivalence verification, and inclusive-service accommodation verification.
Each remains pending and gated to a future volume.

## V6-30.5 Downstream constraints

This section is normative.

No downstream work may claim an implemented, compliant, conformant, operationally
proven, or independently assured control on the strength of a Package 3 obligation or
control objective. No executable compliance policy, retention schedule, disposition
action, accessibility implementation, production translation, accommodation grant, or
provider selection is authorized by this package. Executable work begins only under the
gate sequence following Gate V6-G3 and the subsequent packages.

## V6-30.6 Package 4 direction

This section is normative.

Package 4 is authorized, subject to the Gate V6-G3 disposition, to continue
obligation-definition and validation-planning work on the control model established
here. Package 4 does not implement any control, does not select technology, counsel,
auditor, or provider, and does not authorize implementation.

## V6-30.7 Explicit non-authorizations

This section is normative.

This chapter authorizes no implementation and closes no validation item. It records
traceability, the validation backlog, and downstream constraints only. Every Package 3
record remains `authorizes_implementation: false` and `implementation_status:
NOT_IMPLEMENTED_OR_NOT_PROVEN`.
