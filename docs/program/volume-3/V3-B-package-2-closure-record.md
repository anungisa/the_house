## V3-B.3 Seasonal operating cycle coverage

This section is normative.

The twelve-phase seasonal affiliation operating cycle is defined from prior-season review
through readiness, opening, active processing, escalation and remediation, expiry and
closure, reconciliation and archival review, and post-season assurance (V3-08). Each
phase carries an objective, accountable function, participating functions, inputs,
readiness conditions, outputs, operating controls, escalation, unresolved validation, and
completion evidence, without fabricated calendar dates or lead times.

## V3-B.4 Recognition, pathway triage, and case-opening

This section is normative.

Recognition, jurisdiction determination, pathway triage, and seasonal case-opening are
defined with five determination classes and explicit authorities, so that pathway
selection is a classified determination rather than informal reviewer discretion (V3-09).

## V3-B.5 Queue and handoff model

This section is normative.

Queue ownership, jurisdictional assignment, reviewer eligibility, assignment and
reassignment, conflict and absence handling, work aging, review, return and resubmission,
and inter-function handoffs are defined. Every handoff records its sending and receiving
functions, trigger, transferred information, and retained versus transferred authority,
and no handoff silently changes decision authority (V3-10).

## V3-B.6 Remediation and exception model

This section is normative.

Applicant remediation, operational exceptions, policy exceptions, administrative
corrections, decision reconsideration, data-quality corrections, and incident recovery
are defined as seven distinct categories, each with its own authority, evidence, audit
expectation, and escalation, and none substitutes for a governed lifecycle decision
(V3-11).

## V3-B.7 Financial and activation operations

This section is normative.

Financial reconciliation and activation operations preserve the authority boundaries
among policy, the House, the payment provider, the accounting system, finance operations,
and the affiliation authority, and activation executes an approved lifecycle effect
exactly once, without fabricated amounts or accounting policy (V3-12).

## V3-B.8 Support and recovery operations

recover service without acquiring authority to change pathway, approve evidence, record
decisions, waive fees, activate affiliations, or access restricted evidence without
recorded authority (V3-13).
