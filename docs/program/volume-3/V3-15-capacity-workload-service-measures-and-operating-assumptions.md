## V3-15.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the capacity, workload, and service-measure model for the affiliation
season. It records workload assumptions, capacity and queue risks, and the measures
requiring baseline collection, and it identifies the decisions that cannot be made before
pilot evidence. It asserts no staffing levels or headcount (BR-V3-014, CAP-V3-015). It
authorizes no implementation.

## V3-15.2 Capacity inputs

This section is normative.

The capacity model consumes the following inputs. Each input is an operating driver, not a
committed quantity (FR-V3-016, DATA-V3-005):

- active clubs count
- pathway distribution across continuity confirmation, renewal with remediation, and new
  affiliation
- seasonal opening pattern
- evidence volume
- reviewer complexity
- return and resubmission frequency
- financial exception frequency
- support demand
- jurisdictional differences
- bilingual review demand
- accessibility-support demand
- policy-exception volume
- activation-recovery volume

## V3-15.3 Measure classification

This section is normative.

Every service measure is classified so that unvalidated measures are never treated as
committed (CTRL-V3-014, BR-V3-013). The classifications are:

- **DEFINED** - the measure is defined and baselined.
- **BASELINE_PENDING** - the measure is defined but requires baseline data collection.
- **OPERATIONAL_VALIDATION_PENDING** - the measure requires operational validation.
- **POLICY_VALIDATION_PENDING** - the measure requires policy validation.
- **FINANCIAL_VALIDATION_PENDING** - the measure requires financial validation.
- **STAKEHOLDER_VALIDATION_PENDING** - the measure requires stakeholder validation.
- **PRODUCTION_PROOF_PENDING** - the measure requires production evidence to prove.

## V3-15.4 Workload assumptions

This section is normative.

Workload assumptions describe how the capacity inputs are expected to drive work, stated
qualitatively. They assert no numeric volumes, rates, or targets; quantitative values are
BASELINE_PENDING until collected (BR-V3-013, MEAS-V3-06). Workload assumptions inform
readiness planning (V3-08) but commit no staffing.

## V3-15.5 Capacity and queue risks

This section is normative.

The model records capacity risks and queue risks, including peak-period risk, backlog
risk, and reassignment risk. Aging and backlog thresholds are OPERATIONAL_VALIDATION_
PENDING (MEAS-V3-09, CTRL-V3-018). Risks are qualitative and carry owners.

## V3-15.6 Critical-role concentration and peak-period risks

This section is normative.

The model records where operating capability is concentrated in a small number of
functions or roles and where peak periods concentrate demand. It records the risk without
committing to staffing or headcount to mitigate it; mitigation is a downstream decision
(V3-16, BR-V3-014).

## V3-15.7 Cross-training and continuity dependencies

This section is normative.

The model records cross-training needs and continuity dependencies as operating
assumptions. These identify where continuity depends on shared capability; they authorize
no hiring, staffing, or organizational commitment.

## V3-15.8 Measures requiring baseline collection

This section is normative.

The following operating measures require baseline collection before targets can be set
(BASELINE_PENDING or the relevant validation-pending class):

- MEAS-V3-06 - seasonal workload baseline (BASELINE_PENDING).
- MEAS-V3-07 - bilingual and accessibility recovery measure (ACCESSIBILITY_VALIDATION_
  PENDING).
- MEAS-V3-08 - operating-threshold baseline (BASELINE_PENDING).
- MEAS-V3-09 - queue-aging and exception measure (OPERATIONAL_VALIDATION_PENDING).

Inherited Package 1 measures MEAS-V3-01 through MEAS-V3-05 remain as recorded in V3-07.

## V3-15.9 Decisions that cannot be made before pilot evidence

This section is normative.

The following decisions cannot be made before pilot or production evidence and are
recorded as PRODUCTION_PROOF_PENDING or the relevant validation-pending class:

- committed queue-aging and throughput targets;
- committed financial exception rates;
- committed support-demand and recovery-time targets; and
- committed capacity and staffing levels (also barred by BR-V3-014).

## V3-15.10 Operating assumptions and owners

This section is normative.

Every unresolved operating assumption in this chapter carries an owner and a future gate,
consistent with the validation backlog (V3-07). Seasonal capacity assumptions are owned
with National Operations (Rich); financial measures with Finance and Reconciliation
Operations (Hélène); accessibility and bilingual measures with their content owners;
club-operations measures with Jen and PTSO and club representatives.

## V3-15.11 Validation status

This section is normative.

The capacity and measure model is author-asserted and carries baseline and validation
pending as recorded per measure. No staffing, headcount, or numeric target is committed.
Pending validation blocks only the affected measure or assumption. Unresolved assumptions
are carried into the seasonal operating playbook (V3-16) and the validation backlog.
