## V3-19.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the business-level risk and operating-assurance model for the
affiliation service (CAP-V3-019, OUT-V3-019, FR-V3-019). It is a business-control
framework; it does not define technical security architecture or production monitoring
implementation (BR-V3-018, BR-V3-025). It authorizes no implementation.

## V3-19.2 Risk-register template

This section is normative.

Every material risk is recorded with the following fields:

```
Risk statement
Affected outcome
Risk owner
Control objective
Preventive controls
Detective controls
Corrective controls
Evidence generated
Assurance method
Escalation
Residual-risk status
Validation status
Future blocking gate
```

## V3-19.3 Material risk categories

This section is normative.

The following material risks are governed. Each has a risk owner and a control objective;
a material risk without an owner and control objective is not accepted (RULE-V3-015,
CTRL-V3-023).

- Incorrect organization recognition.
- Incorrect pathway determination.
- Jurisdictional misrouting.
- Unauthorized review or decision.
- Evidence loss or inappropriate access.
- Inconsistent requirement application.
- Unresolved financial mismatch.
- Duplicate or failed activation.
- Support overreach.
- Policy-version drift.
- Bilingual inequivalence.
- Inaccessible service operation.
- Privacy incident.
- External-provider failure.
- Continuity disruption.
- Inadequate audit evidence.

## V3-19.4 Control layers

This section is normative.

Each material risk is addressed by preventive, detective, and corrective controls with a
defined assurance method. Controls draw on the operating controls defined across Package 2
(V3-14) and Package 3, and their effectiveness is reviewed in post-season assurance
(V3-24).

## V3-19.5 Assurance evidence

This section is normative.

Governance and assurance evidence is retained and reconstructable so that the
accountability and effectiveness of the service can be demonstrated (NFR-V3-007,
CTRL-V3-029, MEAS-V3-10). Residual-risk status is dispositioned only by the risk owner.

## V3-19.6 Validation status

This section is normative.

The risk and assurance definitions are author-asserted. Assurance and risk validation
involves the risk and assurance function (STK-V3-018) and the compliance-and-policy
function (STK-V3-007); privacy risk validation involves the privacy authority; financial
risk validation involves Hélène. Residual-risk tolerances require validation at a later
gate. Pending validation blocks only the affected risk entry.
