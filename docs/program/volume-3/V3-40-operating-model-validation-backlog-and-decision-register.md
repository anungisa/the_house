## V3-40.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter creates the final Volume 3 unresolved-validation register (CAP-V3-040, OUT-V3-040,
FR-V3-040). It records what remains unresolved and authorizes no implementation, policy decision,
financial decision, procurement, or executive commitment.

## V3-40.2 Backlog item record

This section is normative.

Each backlog item records:

```
Identifier
Question or assumption
Classification
Current evidence
Confidence
Accountable owner
Consulted functions
Affected service
Affected outcome
Risk if unresolved
Required resolution evidence
Future blocking gate
Target resolution volume
```

## V3-40.3 Required categories

This section is normative.

The backlog covers at least:

- pathway and policy validation;
- jurisdictional operating differences;
- reviewer authority and delegation;
- financial reconciliation;
- sourcing and contracts;
- capacity;
- service economics;
- training;
- bilingual readiness;
- accessibility readiness;
- privacy;
- support ownership;
- continuity;
- management reporting;
- pilot evidence;
- material organizational commitment.

## V3-40.4 Separation of states

This section is normative.

The register clearly separates (BR-V3-043, RULE-V3-034, CTRL-V3-048):

```
Operating model defined
Operating model awaiting validation
Policy decision pending
Financial decision pending
Contractual evidence pending
Architecture input
Delivery-planning input
Executive decision pending
```

Every item records an owner, required resolution evidence, a future blocking gate, and a closure
separation category.

## V3-40.5 Validation status

This section is normative.

The backlog is author-asserted. Governance and assurance validation involves Curling Canada
National Operations (STK-V3-001), the risk and assurance function (STK-V3-018), and, for material
organizational commitment, the executive material-commitment authority (STK-V3-016) at a later
gate. Pending validation blocks only the affected item.
