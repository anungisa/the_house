## V3-29.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines an economic model for the affiliation service (CAP-V3-029,
OUT-V3-029, FR-V3-029) without asserting approved figures. It authorizes no implementation
and approves no fee, charge, margin, revenue, or budget.

## V3-29.2 Cost-driver categories

This section is normative.

The model records the following cost-driver categories:

- Seasonal preparation.
- Policy and content administration.
- Case processing.
- Evidence review.
- Financial reconciliation.
- Support.
- Bilingual content.
- Accessibility assurance.
- Privacy and compliance.
- External services.
- Infrastructure and technology operations.
- Continuity and recovery.
- Audit and assurance.
- Training and adoption.
- Transition and migration.

## V3-29.5 Funding hypotheses and constraints

This section is normative.

Potential funding or recovery mechanisms may be identified as hypotheses (BR-V3-030,
NFR-V3-010), but no fee, charge, margin, revenue, or budget is approved here
(CTRL-V3-036, MEAS-V3-13). An economic record states a hypothesis only, and every hypothesis
records its evidence source and baseline status so that it is reconstructable and can be
validated before any material commitment.
