## V3-34.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates the Package 4 capacity, economics, adoption, and readiness model
into a single playbook and explicitly records the matters outside Package 4 (CAP-V3-034,
OUT-V3-034, FR-V3-034, BR-V3-035). It authorizes no implementation.

This section is normative.

## V3-34.3 Explicit exclusions

This section is normative.

The playbook explicitly excludes, and Package 4 does not authorize, the following
(BR-V3-035, BR-V3-036, CTRL-V3-041):

- Named staffing.
- Headcount approval.
- Salary model.
- Approved budget.
- Approved fees.
- Vendor selection.
- Procurement.
- Technical architecture.
- Software implementation.
- Delivery sequencing.
- Rollout schedule.
- Master development plan.

## V3-34.4 Relationship to the operating model

This section is normative.

The playbook builds on the frozen institutional structure (Package 1), seasonal operations
(Package 2), and governance controls (Package 3) without altering their frozen content. It
determines what would make the operating model sustainable, adoptable, and ready for future
material commitment without making that commitment.

## V3-34.5 Validation status

This section is normative.

The playbook is author-asserted and inherits the validation status of the chapters it
consolidates. Material organizational commitment, permanent organizational form, staffing,
cost, fees, procurement, and vendor selection require executive acceptance at a later gate
(Nolan). Pending validation blocks only the affected capacity, sourcing, financial, or
readiness element.
