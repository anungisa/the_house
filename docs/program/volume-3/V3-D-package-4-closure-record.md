## V3-D.3 Service-delivery and operating-coverage model

This section is normative.

The target service-delivery model defines how each affiliation service function is delivered
across Curling Canada, PTSOs and member associations, clubs, reviewers, finance, policy,
support, product and technology stewardship, and external providers, distinguishing
nationally centralized, jurisdictionally distributed, club-performed, shared-service,
external-execution, manual-transitional, and future-state delivery, without asserting
business hours, service levels, named employees, or headcount (V3-26).

## V3-D.4 Role and capacity model

This section is normative.

Operating functions are converted into role archetypes with purpose, permitted and
prohibited activities, competencies, and segregation-of-duty constraints, and a capacity
model defines workload drivers, capacity units, and calculation concepts without assigning
permanent staff, named individuals, or headcount numbers (V3-27).

## V3-D.5 Sourcing model

This section is normative.

Each business function is given a sourcing classification with retained Curling Canada
authority, failure handling, reconciliation, and assurance, without selecting a vendor,
approving procurement, confirming unverified provider capabilities, or transferring governed
authority to a provider (V3-28).
