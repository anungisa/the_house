## V3-38.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates the Package 4 capacity and economics model into a single synthesis
(CAP-V3-038, OUT-V3-038, FR-V3-038). It introduces no staffing quantity, dollar amount, approved
fee, budget, vendor, or commercial commitment, and it authorizes no implementation.

## V3-38.2 Required outputs

This section is normative.

The synthesis records:

- operating workload model;
- peak-demand assumptions;
- critical-role concentration;
- continuity dependencies;
- cross-training requirements;
- sourcing classifications;
- retained authority;
- external dependency posture;
- cost-driver model;
- funding hypotheses;
- financial sensitivities;
- evidence required before quantitative planning.

## V3-38.4 No-quantification constraint

This section is normative.

The synthesis introduces no staffing quantity, dollar amount, approved fee, budget, vendor, or
commercial commitment. Financial and contractual conclusions remain hypotheses with a
classification and the evidence required before quantitative planning.

## V3-38.5 Validation status

This section is normative.

The synthesis is author-asserted. Economics, funding, and reconciliation validation involves
finance and reconciliation operations (STK-V3-008); material organizational commitment involves
the executive material-commitment authority (STK-V3-016) at a later gate. Pending validation
blocks only the affected capacity, sourcing, or financial conclusion.
