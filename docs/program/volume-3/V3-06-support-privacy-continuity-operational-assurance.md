## V3-06.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the operating model for support, privacy, continuity, and
operational assurance. It establishes that support assists and routes but does not
acquire affiliation decision authority, and it defines privacy, continuity, and
assurance operating paths.

## V3-06.2 Support operating model

This section is normative.

- Support and Service Operations (STK-V3-009) provides intake, case ownership, and
  routing (FR-V3-004).
- Support may progress, annotate, or escalate a case but may not record a review
  outcome, reconciliation confirmation, or lifecycle decision (BR-V3-002, RULE-V3-004,
  CTRL-V3-003).
- A support escalation transfers a case to the accountable decision function without
  transferring decision authority to support (UC-V3-005).
- Support cases are recorded for audit and never substitute for a governed decision.

## V3-06.3 Privacy operating model

This section is normative.

- The Privacy Authority (STK-V3-011) governs restricted-evidence access and privacy
  incidents.
- Restricted-evidence access decisions are recorded; access is granted on a need basis
  consistent with the privacy classification of the evidence.
- Privacy incidents are disposed by the Privacy Authority and escalated to National
  Operations where material.
- Privacy handling never confers affiliation lifecycle authority.

## V3-06.4 Bilingual and accessibility assurance

This section is normative.

- The Bilingual Content Owner (STK-V3-012) owns official-language parity of service
  content; the Accessibility Accountability function (STK-V3-013) owns accessibility.
- Bilingual and accessibility defects are routed to the accountable owners for triage
  and remediation (NFR-V3-002, CTRL-V3-007).
- Bilingual and accessibility conformance is an operating measure (MEAS-V3-05) that is
  validation-pending in V3-07.

## V3-06.5 Continuity and recovery

This section is normative.

- The continuity and recovery posture defines the operating response to service
  interruption and the resumption of queued work (NFR-V3-001, CTRL-V3-005).
- On interruption, in-flight cases are preserved, queued work is resumed, and held or
  returned items re-enter per the work-management model (V3-04).
- No recovery-time objective or availability target is committed in this volume;
  continuity measures are validation-pending (BR-V3-005).

## V3-06.6 Operational assurance

This section is normative.

- Operational assurance confirms that support-boundary, segregation-of-duty, activation-
  dependency, and assurance controls hold in operation.
- Assurance draws on the audit review defined in V3-04 and the operating measures
  defined in V3-07.
- Assurance findings are recorded and escalated to National Operations where they
  indicate a control weakness.

## V3-06.7 Validation status

This section is normative.

The support, privacy, continuity, and assurance operating models are author-asserted
and carry stakeholder and operational validation pending. Support acquires no
affiliation decision authority. Unresolved assumptions are recorded in V3-07.
