## V3-17.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the forums and decision mechanisms required to govern the affiliation
service (CAP-V3-017, OUT-V3-017, FR-V3-017). It defines governance functions without
committing to permanent committees, appointments, or meeting frequencies; a governance
function may be defined while its final organizational form remains pending (BR-V3-016,
CTRL-V3-021). It authorizes no implementation.

## V3-17.2 Governance-forum definition template

This section is normative.

Each governance forum or decision mechanism is defined by the following fields. Every
field is recorded; no field is fabricated where organizational approval is absent.

```
Purpose
Decision scope
Accountable function
Participants
Required evidence
Inputs
Outputs
Decision class
Delegation limits
Escalation route
Meeting or review cadence status
Record retained
Validation status
```

## V3-17.3 Governance contexts

This section is normative.

The following governance contexts are defined for the affiliation service. Each is a
governance function; its final organizational form and cadence remain validation-pending
until organizational approval (BR-V3-016).

- Seasonal-readiness governance disposes seasonal readiness within its decision scope.
- Affiliation-policy governance owns interpretation and change of affiliation policy.
- Operational service review reviews operating performance and service exceptions.
- Financial and reconciliation review reviews reconciliation status and financial
  exceptions within the financial operating boundary (V3-12).
- Exception and escalation review dispositions escalated exceptions and reconsiderations
  (V3-11).
- Privacy and restricted-evidence oversight governs restricted-evidence access and
  privacy incidents.
- Bilingual and accessibility oversight governs official-language parity and accessibility
  of the service.
- External-dependency review oversees external and cross-organizational dependencies
  (V3-21).
- Post-season assurance reviews control effectiveness and lessons learned (V3-24).
- Material organizational commitment escalates matters that would require staffing, cost,
  procurement, or implementation to the executive material-commitment authority
  (STK-V3-016), which is not exercised in Volume 3 definition work.

## V3-17.4 Decision scope and delegation

This section is normative.

A governance forum holds governed decision authority only within its recorded decision
scope and delegation limits; matters beyond scope escalate through the recorded route
(BR-V3-015, RULE-V3-013, CTRL-V3-020). Governance forums do not acquire affiliation
lifecycle authority that the Governance Kernel and the operating model reserve to their
accountable functions.

## V3-17.5 Records and evidence

This section is normative.

Each governance forum retains a record of its decisions, the evidence considered, and the
delegations exercised (DATA-V3-006). Governance records are a business information domain
with a steward and a correction authority (V3-22).
