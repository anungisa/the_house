## V3-13.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines support, communication, notification, and service-recovery
operations for the affiliation season. It ensures that support assists users and recovers
service without acquiring governed affiliation decision authority (BR-V3-012, CAP-V3-013).
It authorizes no implementation.

## V3-13.2 Support intake and classification

This section is normative.

Support intake receives requests, classifies each issue, and correlates it to an existing
case where one exists (FR-V3-014). Classification records the issue type and routes it to
the appropriate operating function. Support owns the support case; it does not own the
affiliation decision.

## V3-13.3 Case correlation

This section is normative.

A support issue is correlated to the affiliation case it concerns so that the operating
history is reconstructable (DATA-V3-004, NFR-V3-004). Correlation does not transfer
decision authority to support.

## V3-13.4 Evidence-access and representative-access issues

This section is normative.

Support handles evidence-access and representative-access issues by assisting the user and
escalating to the accountable authority. Support does not grant access to restricted
evidence on its own authority; restricted-evidence access is decided by the privacy
authority under recorded authority (CTRL-V3-017, STK-V3-011).

## V3-13.5 Draft, submission, and recovery issues

This section is normative.

Support assists with draft and submission recovery so that an applicant can complete or
resubmit a case (V3-10). Recovery restores the applicant's ability to act; it does not
approve evidence or record a decision (BR-V3-012).

## V3-13.6 Notification operations and failure

This section is normative.

Notifications inform applicants and functions of case status, requested corrections, and
outcomes (DATA-V3-004). A notification failure is detected and recovered; where a
notification failure affects a case, it is correlated and escalated. Notification content
is bilingual and accessible (V3-13.8).

## V3-13.7 Bilingual-content and accessibility problems

This section is normative.

Bilingual-content and accessibility problems are triaged and routed to their accountable
owners (Bilingual Content Owner and Accessibility Accountability) for remediation
(NFR-V3-005, CTRL-V3-016). Support records the defect and escalates; it does not decide
content policy.

## V3-13.8 Payment-status, reviewer, queue, and activation issues

This section is normative.

Support handles payment-status discrepancies, reviewer and queue issues, and activation
failures by correlating them to the accountable function (finance operations, operations,
or incident recovery) and escalating (V3-12, V3-10, V3-11). Support does not reconcile
payments, record review outcomes, or activate affiliations (BR-V3-012).

## V3-13.9 Privacy concerns and escalation

This section is normative.

A privacy concern is escalated to the privacy authority (STK-V3-011). Support escalates to
policy, finance, privacy, technology, or the service owner according to the issue type.
Escalation follows the handoff template (V3-10) and transfers no governed decision
authority to support.

## V3-13.10 Service interruption and continuity-mode operation

This section is normative.

On service interruption, support operates in a continuity mode that preserves user
assistance and case correlation while normal processing is restored (NFR-V3-006, V3-06
inherited). Continuity-mode operation does not create governed decisions and records the
interruption and recovery.

## V3-13.11 Recovery confirmation

This section is normative.

Service recovery is confirmed and recorded (CTRL-V3-016). A recovered incident that
affected a case is returned to the normal workflow through the correction and incident-
recovery operations (V3-11).

## V3-13.12 Support prohibitions

This section is normative.

Support shall not change pathway, approve evidence, record an affiliation decision, waive
a fee, activate an affiliation, or access restricted evidence without recorded authority
(BR-V3-012, RULE-V3-011, CTRL-V3-013). A support action that would cross these boundaries
is escalated to the accountable authority instead.

## V3-13.13 Validation status

This section is normative.

The support, communication, and service-recovery operations are author-asserted and carry
operational validation pending. Support boundaries require validation with Support and
Service Operations; bilingual and accessibility recovery require validation with the
official-languages and accessibility authorities; privacy handling requires validation
with the privacy authority. Pending validation blocks only the affected operating rule or
measure. Unresolved assumptions are recorded in V3-15.
