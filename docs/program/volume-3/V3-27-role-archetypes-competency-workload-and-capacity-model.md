## V3-27.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter converts the operating functions into role archetypes and defines a workload
and capacity model (CAP-V3-027, OUT-V3-027, FR-V3-027) without assigning permanent staff
or committing headcount. It authorizes no implementation.

## V3-27.2 Role archetypes

This section is normative.

The model defines the following role archetypes:

- Affiliation policy authority.
- Service owner.
- Seasonal readiness coordinator.
- Jurisdictional reviewer.
- National reviewer or escalation authority.
- Compliance administrator.
- Financial reconciliation operator.
- Support operator.
- Privacy authority.
- Bilingual-content steward.
- Accessibility authority.
- Operational assurance function.
- Product steward.
- Technology steward.

## V3-27.3 Role-archetype record template

This section is normative.

For each archetype, the model records:

```
Purpose
Accountable outcomes
Permitted activities
Prohibited activities
Required competencies
Delegation conditions
Segregation-of-duty constraints
Workload drivers
Peak-demand factors
Continuity dependency
Cross-training requirement
Validation status
```

An archetype enforces its permitted, prohibited, and segregation-of-duty constraints
(BR-V3-027, RULE-V3-022). No archetype is populated with a named individual or a headcount.

## V3-27.4 Capacity model

This section is normative.

The capacity model defines workload units such as:

- Club cases opened.
- Pathway determinations.
- Evidence items reviewed.
- Returns for information.
- Resubmissions.
- Policy exceptions.
- Financial mismatches.
- Activation recoveries.
- Support cases.
- Privacy or accessibility escalations.

The capacity model may define calculation concepts and evidence requirements
(BR-V3-028, CTRL-V3-034, MEAS-V3-12), but it does not assert staffing numbers, headcount,
or service-level guarantees.

## V3-27.5 Validation status

This section is normative.

The role-archetype and capacity definitions are author-asserted. Capacity and workload
validation involves the affiliation service owner (STK-V3-017), Curling Canada National
Operations (STK-V3-001), and the risk and assurance function (STK-V3-018); capacity
measures require operating and pilot evidence baselines. Pending validation blocks only the
affected archetype or capacity definition.
