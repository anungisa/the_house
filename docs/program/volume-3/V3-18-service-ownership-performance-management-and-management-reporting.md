## V3-18.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines how the affiliation service is functionally owned and reviewed
(CAP-V3-018, OUT-V3-018, FR-V3-018). Management reports are instruments that support
decisions; they are not independent sources of governed truth (BR-V3-017, NFR-V3-008,
RULE-V3-014, CTRL-V3-022). It authorizes no implementation and commits no staffing.

## V3-18.2 Service ownership and accountability

This section is normative.

The affiliation service has a functional service owner (STK-V3-017) accountable for
operating performance and management review, distinct from affiliation-lifecycle policy
authority. The following accountabilities are defined:

- Product-to-service handoff transfers responsibility for operating the service from
  product-and-technology stewardship (STK-V3-010) to the service owner, recording what is
  transferred and what is retained (V3-10 handoff discipline).
- Service-owner accountability covers end-to-end operating performance.
- National operating oversight is held by Curling Canada National Operations (STK-V3-001).
- PTSO operating accountability is held within each jurisdiction (STK-V3-002).
- Club-facing service responsibility covers support and communication to clubs (V3-13).

## V3-18.3 Management report template

This section is normative.

Every report or management view is defined by the following fields:

```
Purpose
Audience
Accountable owner
Data or evidence source
Measure definitions
Interpretation limits
Decision supported
Escalation threshold status
Validation status
Retention expectation
```

## V3-18.4 Management views

This section is normative.

The following management views are defined. Each carries interpretation limits and an
escalation-threshold status that is baseline-pending until operating evidence exists
(CTRL-V3-022, MEAS-V3-11); no numeric threshold is asserted without a baseline.

- Queue and workload reporting.
- Pathway distribution.
- Aging and stalled work.
- Returns and resubmissions.
- Financial exceptions.
- Activation failures.
- Support demand.
- Policy exceptions.
- Privacy, bilingual, and accessibility issues.
- Seasonal readiness and closure reporting.

## V3-18.5 Reporting is not governed truth

This section is normative.

A management report does not confer governed truth. A governed decision cites the
authoritative record maintained by the accountable function, not the report (RULE-V3-014).
Reporting data domains have a steward (V3-22).
