## V3-35.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates Packages 1 through 4 into a single authoritative business-operating
baseline for the affiliation service (CAP-V3-035, OUT-V3-035, FR-V3-035). It is consolidation
only: it introduces no unsupported operating decision to manufacture completeness, and it
authorizes no implementation, staffing, cost, procurement, architecture, delivery sequencing,
or the master development plan.

## V3-35.2 Scope of consolidation

This section is normative.

The baseline consolidates the ratified content of:

- institutional functions and national, jurisdictional, and club responsibilities (Package 1);
- the three affiliation pathways, the seasonal operating cycle, case and queue operations,
  reviewer and decision authority, remediation and exceptions, financial reconciliation,
  activation, and support and recovery (Package 2);
- service governance, assurance, and controlled change (Package 3);
- capacity and workload, sourcing, economics, training and adoption, onboarding readiness, and
  the material-commitment boundary (Package 4).

## V3-35.3 Operating-capability record

This section is normative.

For every material operating capability, the baseline records:

```
Operating capability
Business outcome
Accountable function
Performing function
Decision authority
Required inputs
Outputs
Controls
Evidence generated
Dependencies
Capacity driver
Sourcing posture
Validation status
Future blocking gate
```

Each field references the ratified Package 1 through 4 chapter and register record that is its
source; the baseline adds no new authority, obligation, or decision.

## V3-35.4 Consolidation-only constraint

This section is normative.

A record may enter the integrated baseline only if it consolidates a capability already ratified
in Packages 1 through 4 (BR-V3-037, RULE-V3-029, CTRL-V3-042). Where evidence is incomplete, the
capability is recorded with its existing validation status rather than resolved by assertion. The
baseline manufactures no completeness.

## V3-35.5 Validation status

This section is normative.

The integrated baseline is author-asserted and inherits the validation status of the content it
consolidates. Operating validation involves Curling Canada National Operations (STK-V3-001) and
the affiliation service owner (STK-V3-017). Pending validation blocks only the affected
consolidated capability.
