## V3-C.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This record closes Volume 3 Package 3 (Operating Governance, Service Assurance, and
Controlled Change). It records the inherited Package 2 baseline and its completed freeze
provenance, defines Gate V3-G3, records the gate disposition, freezes the Package 3
corpus, and authorizes Package 4. It records the operating-governance and assurance model
only; it authorizes no staffing, cost, procurement, vendor selection, technical
architecture, security implementation, monitoring tooling, delivery sequencing, or
implementation, is not a master development plan, and leaves executive organizational
acceptance pending.

## V3-C.3 Operating-governance and decision-forum coverage

This section is normative.

Operating-governance functions and decision forums are defined with a decision-rights
model across governance contexts, distinguishing recommendation, decision, and executive
acceptance authority, without asserting standing committees, permanent organizational
form, or meeting calendars (V3-17). The material organizational commitment context is
identified but not exercised.

## V3-C.4 Service ownership and management-reporting model

This section is normative.

Service ownership, performance management, and management reporting are defined, with the
affiliation service owner accountable for operating performance and a defined set of
management views, where reports are governance instruments and not governed truth, and no
staffing is committed (V3-18).

## V3-C.5 Risk, control, and assurance coverage

This section is normative.

Material business risks carry owners and control objectives, and preventive, detective,
corrective, and assurance control layers are represented in the business risk and
operating-assurance framework, without technical security architecture or monitoring
implementation (V3-19).

## V3-C.6 Controlled-change model

This section is normative.

Operating, policy, financial, privacy, bilingual, accessibility, and authority-boundary
changes are classified, each with its governance path, so that operating change is a
classified governed decision rather than informal adjustment, and no change class
authorizes implementation or procurement (V3-20).

## V3-C.7 Dependency operating model

This section is normative.

External providers and cross-organizational dependencies have owners, failure handling,
reconciliation, and escalation, without vendor selection, procurement, or assertion of
unverified contractual capability (V3-21).

## V3-C.8 Operational-data stewardship

This section is normative.

Operational information domains have business stewards and defined correction authority,
with records and evidence accountability that preserves governed authority, without
tables, schemas, storage technologies, or API payloads (V3-22).

## V3-C.9 Incident, continuity, and recovery governance

This section is normative.

Incidents, problems, continuity events, and support issues are distinguished, and recovery
and post-incident assurance preserve governed authority and evidence, without technical
incident-response or disaster-recovery design (V3-23).

## V3-C.10 Continuous-improvement model

This section is normative.

Continuous improvement, seasonal review, and management assurance are governed so that
improvement proposals are classified by authority and routed to future volumes or the
operating-change process, and no improvement proposal authorizes implementation by virtue
of operational desirability (V3-24). The consolidated playbook records the governance
model and its explicit exclusions of material commitment (V3-25).
