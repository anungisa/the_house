## V3-08.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the seasonal affiliation operating cycle and the readiness model
that prepares each function to enter it. It describes how Curling Canada, PTSOs, clubs,
reviewers, finance, support, and policy functions prepare for, open, operate, and close
each affiliation season. It defines operating phases, not calendar dates, and it
authorizes no staffing, cost, procurement, technical architecture, or implementation
(BR-V3-007, BR-V3-014, CAP-V3-008).

## V3-08.2 Operating-cycle model

This section is normative.

The seasonal affiliation operating cycle proceeds through the following phases. The
phases are ordered but their durations, lead times, and calendar dates are not asserted
here; unspecified timing is validation-pending and is not fabricated (BR-V3-007,
RULE-V3-005).

1. Prior-season review
2. Policy and requirement preparation
3. Jurisdiction and club-roster preparation
4. Fee-policy reference confirmation
5. Content and communication readiness
6. Reviewer and queue readiness
7. Affiliation opening
8. Active processing
9. Escalation and remediation period
10. Expiry and closure
11. Reconciliation and archival review
12. Post-season assurance

## V3-08.3 Phase definition template

This section is normative.

Each phase in this chapter is defined by a common template so that readiness is explicit
and auditable (FR-V3-007, CTRL-V3-008). Each phase states:

- **Objective** - what the phase must achieve.
- **Accountable function** - the single function accountable for phase completion.
- **Participating functions** - functions that contribute to the phase.
- **Required inputs** - the inputs the phase consumes.
- **Readiness conditions** - the conditions that must hold before the phase is entered
  or considered ready.
- **Outputs** - the artifacts or states the phase produces.
- **Operating controls** - the controls that govern the phase.
- **Escalation** - where unmet conditions or blocked work escalate.
- **Unresolved validation** - assumptions carried forward for validation.
- **Evidence of completion** - the evidence that the phase is complete.

## V3-08.4 Phase 1 - Prior-season review

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** review the prior season's operations, defects, exceptions, and measures
  to inform the coming season.
- **Accountable function:** Curling Canada National Operations.
- **Participating functions:** PTSOs, reviewers, finance operations, support, compliance
  and policy.
- **Required inputs:** prior-season case history, exception log, reconciliation outcomes,
  and validation backlog (V3-07).
- **Readiness conditions:** the prior season is closed and its reconciliation and
  archival review (Phase 11) are complete.
- **Outputs:** a review record and a set of preparation inputs for Phase 2.
- **Operating controls:** audit-reconstruction control (CTRL-V3-015).
- **Escalation:** unresolved prior-season items escalate to National Operations.
- **Unresolved validation:** measures requiring baseline collection remain
  validation-pending (V3-15).
- **Evidence of completion:** a recorded prior-season review.

## V3-08.5 Phase 2 - Policy and requirement preparation

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** confirm the affiliation policy and requirements applicable to the coming
  season.
- **Accountable function:** Compliance and Policy Function.
- **Participating functions:** National Operations, PTSOs.
- **Required inputs:** current policy, prior-season policy exceptions, and requirement
  register references (REG-303).
- **Readiness conditions:** policy determinations for the season are settled by the
  policy authority; open policy questions are recorded, not assumed (RULE-V3-006).
- **Outputs:** confirmed seasonal policy and requirement references.
- **Operating controls:** pathway-selection-integrity control (CTRL-V3-009).
- **Escalation:** unresolved policy questions escalate to the Compliance and Policy
  Function and, where material, toward the executive material-commitment authority.
- **Unresolved validation:** policy items pending validation are recorded.
- **Evidence of completion:** a confirmed seasonal policy reference.

## V3-08.6 Phase 3 - Jurisdiction and club-roster preparation

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** confirm jurisdictional assignments and the club roster in scope for the
  season.
- **Accountable function:** PTSO / Member Association, coordinated by National Operations.
- **Participating functions:** clubs, National Operations.
- **Required inputs:** prior club roster, recognition and jurisdiction records (V3-09).
- **Readiness conditions:** jurisdiction determinations for known clubs are recorded with
  their determination class.
- **Outputs:** a prepared jurisdiction and club roster.
- **Operating controls:** pathway-selection-integrity control (CTRL-V3-009).
- **Escalation:** jurisdiction disputes escalate per V3-09.
- **Unresolved validation:** ambiguous or dormant clubs are carried to case-opening
  triage (V3-09).
- **Evidence of completion:** a prepared roster with recorded jurisdictions.

## V3-08.7 Phase 4 - Fee-policy reference confirmation

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** confirm the fee-policy reference used for the season without asserting
  amounts.
- **Accountable function:** Compliance and Policy Function for fee policy; Finance and
  Reconciliation Operations for reconciliation reference.
- **Participating functions:** National Operations.
- **Required inputs:** the governing fee policy and reconciliation model (V3-12, V3-05).
- **Readiness conditions:** the fee-policy reference is confirmed; the House records the
  fee obligation reference but not fee amounts or accounting policy (BR-V3-014).
- **Outputs:** a confirmed fee-policy reference.
- **Operating controls:** threshold-baseline control (CTRL-V3-014).
- **Escalation:** unconfirmed fee policy escalates to the policy and finance functions.
- **Unresolved validation:** fee amounts and financial thresholds remain
  finance-validation-pending.
- **Evidence of completion:** a confirmed fee-policy reference.

## V3-08.8 Phase 5 - Content and communication readiness

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** confirm that seasonal content, notifications, and communications are
  ready in both official languages and are accessible.
- **Accountable function:** Bilingual Content Owner for language parity; Accessibility
  Accountability for accessibility; Support and Service Operations for communication
  operations.
- **Participating functions:** National Operations.
- **Required inputs:** seasonal content, notification templates, and communication plan
  (V3-13).
- **Readiness conditions:** content is bilingual and accessible or its defects are
  recorded and routed to owners (NFR-V3-005, CTRL-V3-016).
- **Outputs:** ready seasonal content and communications.
- **Operating controls:** bilingual-and-accessibility recovery control (CTRL-V3-016).
- **Escalation:** content defects escalate to the accountable content owners.
- **Unresolved validation:** accessibility and bilingual measures remain
  validation-pending.
- **Evidence of completion:** confirmed content and communication readiness.

## V3-08.9 Phase 6 - Reviewer and queue readiness

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** confirm that queues, reviewer eligibility, and assignment rules are ready
  for the season.
- **Accountable function:** National Operations for national queues; PTSOs for
  jurisdictional queues.
- **Participating functions:** reviewers, support.
- **Required inputs:** the work-management model (V3-10), reviewer eligibility, and
  conflict rules.
- **Readiness conditions:** queues exist with recorded ownership; reviewer eligibility and
  conflict rules are recorded (FR-V3-010, RULE-V3-007).
- **Outputs:** ready queues and assignment rules.
- **Operating controls:** queue-aging-and-unresolved-exception control (CTRL-V3-018).
- **Escalation:** reviewer-readiness gaps escalate to the responsible operations function.
- **Unresolved validation:** capacity assumptions remain validation-pending (V3-15).
- **Evidence of completion:** confirmed queue and reviewer readiness.

## V3-08.10 Phase 7 - Affiliation opening

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** open the affiliation service for the season.
- **Accountable function:** National Operations.
- **Participating functions:** PTSOs, support.
- **Required inputs:** completed readiness Phases 1 through 6.
- **Readiness conditions:** all prior readiness phases report their readiness conditions
  as met; unmet conditions hold opening (RULE-V3-005, CTRL-V3-008).
- **Outputs:** an open affiliation season.
- **Operating controls:** readiness-gate control (CTRL-V3-008).
- **Escalation:** unmet readiness escalates to National Operations.
- **Unresolved validation:** opening date is not asserted here.
- **Evidence of completion:** a recorded opening.

## V3-08.11 Phase 8 - Active processing

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** process affiliation cases through recognition, review, reconciliation,
  and activation (V3-09, V3-10, V3-12).
- **Accountable function:** National Operations, with reviewers, finance, and PTSOs
  performing within their authority.
- **Participating functions:** reviewers, finance operations, support, PTSOs.
- **Required inputs:** open cases and ready queues.
- **Readiness conditions:** queues and reviewers are ready (Phase 6).
- **Outputs:** processed cases and activation outcomes.
- **Operating controls:** the full Package 2 control set (V3-14).
- **Escalation:** blocked cases escalate through the queue and handoff model (V3-10).
- **Unresolved validation:** throughput and service measures remain validation-pending
  (V3-15).
- **Evidence of completion:** case processing records.

## V3-08.12 Phase 9 - Escalation and remediation period

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** resolve remediation, exceptions, corrections, and reconsiderations
  (V3-11).
- **Accountable function:** the category authority for each exception class (V3-11).
- **Participating functions:** reviewers, compliance and policy, finance, support.
- **Required inputs:** open exceptions and remediation cases.
- **Readiness conditions:** exception categories and authorities are defined (V3-11).
- **Outputs:** resolved or escalated exceptions.
- **Operating controls:** exception-category control (CTRL-V3-011).
- **Escalation:** unresolved exceptions escalate to their category authority and, if
  material, toward the executive authority.
- **Unresolved validation:** exception-volume measures remain validation-pending.
- **Evidence of completion:** exception disposition records.

## V3-08.13 Phase 10 - Expiry and closure

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** expire or close affiliations at season end through the governance kernel.
- **Accountable function:** National Operations, executing governed terminal transitions
  through the kernel; Volume 3 defines the operating flow around them and does not mutate
  governed state (V3-02).
- **Participating functions:** PTSOs, support, finance.
- **Required inputs:** active-season state and terminal conditions.
- **Readiness conditions:** closure conditions are met per the governed lifecycle.
- **Outputs:** expired or closed affiliations.
- **Operating controls:** audit-reconstruction control (CTRL-V3-015).
- **Escalation:** closure exceptions escalate to National Operations.
- **Unresolved validation:** none specific beyond carried items.
- **Evidence of completion:** recorded expiry and closure.

## V3-08.14 Phase 11 - Reconciliation and archival review

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** complete season-close financial reconciliation and archival review
  (V3-12).
- **Accountable function:** Finance and Reconciliation Operations for reconciliation;
  National Operations for archival review.
- **Participating functions:** external accounting and ledger system, privacy authority.
- **Required inputs:** processor and accounting results, case records.
- **Readiness conditions:** the season is closed (Phase 10).
- **Outputs:** a season-close reconciliation and an archival review record.
- **Operating controls:** activation-idempotency and audit-reconstruction controls
  (CTRL-V3-012, CTRL-V3-015).
- **Escalation:** unresolved balances escalate to finance operations (V3-12).
- **Unresolved validation:** financial exception measures remain validation-pending.
- **Evidence of completion:** a season-close reconciliation record.

## V3-08.15 Phase 12 - Post-season assurance

This section is normative.

- **Objective:** perform operational quality and assurance review of the season (V3-14).
- **Accountable function:** National Operations, with Compliance and Policy and Finance
  operations.
- **Participating functions:** reviewers, support, privacy authority.
- **Required inputs:** season control evidence and audit reconstruction.
- **Readiness conditions:** reconciliation and archival review (Phase 11) are complete.
- **Outputs:** an assurance review that feeds the next prior-season review (Phase 1).
- **Operating controls:** the assurance controls (V3-14).
- **Escalation:** assurance findings escalate to National Operations and the validation
  backlog.
- **Unresolved validation:** measures requiring baseline collection are recorded (V3-15).
- **Evidence of completion:** a recorded post-season assurance review.

## V3-08.16 Validation status
