## V3-A.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This record closes Volume 3 Package 1 (Business Operating Model). It defines

## V3-A.2 Gate V3-G1 - Business Operating-Model Foundation Ready

This section is normative.

Gate V3-G1 is satisfied when all fourteen conditions hold:

1. **Provenance inheritance** - the corrected Volume 2 provenance (v1.0.1) is inherited
   as the Package 1 baseline (V3-00).
2. **Institutional operating functions** - the institutional operating functions are
   defined across national, jurisdictional, club, review, compliance, finance, support,
   stewardship, privacy, bilingual, accessibility, external-provider, and executive
   functions (V3-01, REG-302).
3. **Separation of responsibilities** - responsibilities are separated across functions,
   distinguishing accountable functions from executing and assisting functions
   (V3-01, V3-03).
4. **Pathway operating models** - all three affiliation pathways (continuity
   confirmation, renewal with remediation, new affiliation) have operating models
   across the full service lifecycle (V3-02).
5. **Decision authority versus execution** - decision authority is distinguished from
   operational execution (V3-01, V3-03).
6. **Delegation, escalation, conflicts, and segregation** - delegation, escalation,
   conflict restrictions, and segregation of duties are represented (V3-03, CTRL-V3-001).
7. **Work management** - queues, handoffs, exceptions, and recovery are defined without
   fabricated service-level commitments (V3-04).
8. **Financial operating boundary** - the financial and reconciliation operating
   boundary is defined, including the activation dependency, without fabricated amounts
   (V3-05, CTRL-V3-002).
9. **Support boundary** - support does not acquire governed affiliation decision
   authority (V3-06, BR-V3-002, CTRL-V3-003).
10. **Operating measures** - operating measures are defined or explicitly marked
    validation-pending, with no fabricated targets (V3-07, CTRL-V3-006).
11. **Validation backlog** - unresolved operating assumptions carry owners and future
    gates (V3-07).
12. **No material commitment** - no staffing, cost, procurement, technical architecture,
    delivery sequencing, or implementation is authorized (V3-00, REG-303).
13. **No master development plan** - no master development plan is created.
14. **Line-level review and separate freeze** - Package 1 has received line-level review
    and is frozen at version 1.0.0 through a separate freeze commit.
