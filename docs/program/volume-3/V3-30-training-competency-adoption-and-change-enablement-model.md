## V3-30.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter defines the operating enablement required to adopt the affiliation service
(CAP-V3-030, OUT-V3-030, FR-V3-030). It is enablement design, not final curriculum
production, and it authorizes no implementation.

## V3-30.2 Training audiences

This section is normative.

The model defines enablement for:

- Club representatives.
- PTSO reviewers.
- Curling Canada administrators.
- Finance and reconciliation staff.
- Support operators.
- Policy and compliance functions.
- Assurance functions.
- Product and technology stewards.

## V3-30.3 Training-audience record template

This section is normative.

For each audience, the model records:

```
Required competency
Operating task
Learning objective
Evidence of readiness
Training-content owner
Language obligation
Accessibility obligation
Delivery-channel hypothesis
Refresher requirement
Support dependency
Validation status
```

## V3-30.4 Enablement responsibilities

This section is normative.

The model also defines:

- Operating-procedure ownership.
- Knowledge-base responsibility.
- Seasonal refresher needs.
- Onboarding.
- Role changes.
- Delegation readiness.
- Readiness confirmation.
- Support escalation during adoption.
- Feedback collection.
- Training-content change control.

## V3-30.5 Enablement constraints

This section is normative.

Training and adoption enablement prepares operators to perform governed activities and does
not confer or alter governed decision authority, policy, or evidence rules (BR-V3-031,
RULE-V3-028, CTRL-V3-037). The training-content owner is the training and adoption
enablement function (STK-V3-020). This chapter is enablement design and does not produce a
final curriculum.

## V3-30.6 Validation status

This section is normative.

The enablement definitions are author-asserted. Bilingual obligations involve the
official-languages and content authority (STK-V3-012); accessibility obligations involve the
accessibility authority (STK-V3-013); seasonal operations involve Jen and PTSO and club
representatives. Pending validation blocks only the affected enablement definition.
