## V3-16.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter consolidates the Package 2 seasonal operating model into a single playbook
and explicitly identifies the matters that are outside Package 2. It commits no staffing,
cost, procurement, technical architecture, implementation, migration, deployment, or
operating-service-level commitment (BR-V3-014, CAP-V3-016). It authorizes no
implementation.

## V3-16.2 Readiness activities

This section is normative.

Seasonal readiness follows the twelve-phase operating cycle and readiness model (V3-08).
Readiness is complete when each readiness phase reports its readiness conditions as met;
unmet conditions hold opening (RULE-V3-005, CTRL-V3-008).

## V3-16.3 Daily and weekly operating functions

This section is normative.

During active processing, the operating functions perform recognition, triage, and
case-opening (V3-09); queue assignment, review, and handoffs (V3-10); and reconciliation
and activation (V3-12). Daily and weekly rhythms are operating patterns; no numeric
cadence or service level is committed (BR-V3-013).

## V3-16.4 Case processing

This section is normative.

Cases proceed through the pathways defined in Package 1 (V3-02) using the case-opening,
queue, and handoff operations. Every determination and handoff records its class and
authority (V3-09, V3-10).

## V3-16.5 Exception handling

This section is normative.

Exceptions, remediation, corrections, reconsideration, and incident recovery are handled
under their distinct categories and authorities (V3-11). No category substitutes for a
governed lifecycle decision (BR-V3-010).

## V3-16.6 Reconciliation and activation

This section is normative.

Financial reconciliation and one-time activation preserve the financial authority
boundaries and execute activation exactly once (V3-12, BR-V3-011, CTRL-V3-012).

## V3-16.7 Support and recovery

This section is normative.

Support, communication, notification, and service recovery assist users and recover
service without acquiring governed decision authority (V3-13, BR-V3-012).

## V3-16.8 Assurance

This section is normative.

Operational quality, assurance, audit, and management controls operate at their recorded
frequencies and produce their recorded evidence (V3-14). No numerical thresholds are
invented (BR-V3-013).

## V3-16.9 Season closure and post-season review

This section is normative.

Season closure, reconciliation and archival review, and post-season assurance close the
cycle and feed the next prior-season review (V3-08 Phases 10 through 12).

## V3-16.10 Validation backlog

This section is normative.

Unresolved operating assumptions and measures requiring baseline collection are carried in
the capacity and measure model (V3-15) and the validation backlog (V3-07), each with an
owner and a future gate.

## V3-16.11 Downstream constraints - outside Package 2

This section is normative.

The following are explicitly outside Package 2 and are not authorized or created by it
(BR-V3-014, CTRL-V3-019):

- permanent staffing and approved headcount;
- salary and cost models;
- vendor procurement;
- technical architecture;
- software implementation;
- migration execution;
- deployment planning;
- operating service-level commitments; and
- a master development plan.

These matters remain pending at the executive material-commitment authority and are inputs
to a later volume or gate, not to Package 2.

## V3-16.12 Validation status

This section is normative.

The seasonal operating playbook is author-asserted and consolidates the Package 2 model.
It requires validation across the functions recorded in the individual chapters and
commits no downstream matter. Pending validation blocks only the affected operating rule
or measure. Unresolved assumptions are recorded in V3-15 and the validation backlog.
