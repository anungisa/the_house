## Purpose

This chapter defines the closure of the whole central-registration definition corpus
and the shape of the implementation-authorization handoff that would follow it. It is
recorded in REG-1201 as the `CORPUS_RELEASE_DECISION` and
`IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION_HANDOFF` records and in REG-1202 as
`IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION_INPUT_REQUIREMENT` and
`CORPUS_RELEASE_RECORD_REQUIREMENT` records. The corpus release is a documentary act;
it is not a software release.

## What corpus closure asserts

Corpus closure asserts only that the documentary definition of the program — its
volumes, registers, gates, evidence models, acceptance models, and release-decision
models — is complete, internally consistent, traceable, and frozen. It asserts nothing
about implementation, evidence, acceptance, or production.

## The implementation-authorization handoff

The handoff defines the inputs a separate implementation-authorization decision package
would require: the frozen corpus reference; the master development plan; the integrated
evidence baseline; the gate matrix; the disposition ledger; the identified gaps and
release blockers; the funding, staffing, and procurement decisions required; and the
decision authorities. The handoff is an empty, governed destination; it carries no
authorization.

## Fail-closed distinctions

> Definition corpus complete is not implementation authorized.

> Master development plan complete is not funding approved is not staff assigned.

> Evidence model complete is not evidence available.

> Corpus release is not product release.

The next action after corpus closure is a separate implementation-authorization
decision package, not an implied Volume 13.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter authorizes implementation, approves funding, assigns staff,
engages providers, or releases any system. The handoff is a definition of the next
decision, not that decision.
