## Purpose

This chapter defines the controlled treatment of defects, gaps, exceptions,
waivers, conditions, material commitments, expiry, revocation, and reopening. It
defines how these are governed; it approves no defect, grants no waiver, and
converts no failure into a success.

## Controlled treatments

The following are governed and held distinct, and each treatment is recorded in
register REG-1202 as a finding, condition, waiver, or material commitment:

```
Defect
Evidence gap
Assurance gap
Unresolved dependency
Exception
Waiver
Conditional acceptance
Material commitment
Deferred obligation
Compensating control
Expiry
Revocation
Reopening
```

## Fixed rules

The following rules are fixed and fail closed:

> Waiver approved is distinct from a failure converted to success.
> Conditional acceptance is distinct from unconditional acceptance.
> Commitment recorded is distinct from commitment fulfilled.
> Compensating control defined is distinct from compensating control operating
> effectively.
> An expired waiver or condition is a fail-closed gate condition.

Defect closure requires the applicable retest or re-exercise evidence; a defect is
never closed by assertion.

## Boundary

This chapter records the material-commitment and waiver model. It approves no
defect, grants no waiver, fulfils no commitment, and authorizes no release. Every
record carries the fixed non-authorizing posture.
