# V12-17 — Operational, Continuity, Recovery, Provider, Training, and Adoption Evidence

Document ID: V12-17
Status: RATIFIED
Version: 1.0.0
Owner: Evidence Governance Authority
Approver: Executive Sponsor
Associated Gate: V12-G2
Ratification: Ratified as part of Volume 12 Package 2.

## Purpose

This chapter defines the operational, continuity, recovery, provider, training,
competence, and adoption evidence that would be required to accept the
club-affiliation vertical. Each requirement is recorded in REG-1202 under its
controlled kind. No service is operated, no backup is taken, no recovery is
exercised, no provider is engaged, and no training is delivered by this chapter.

## Operational and observability evidence

Operational evidence would establish that the affiliation vertical can be operated:
supported, observed, and incident-handled under governed conditions. It must address
support, observability, incidents, continuity, backup, restoration, recovery,
reconciliation, and post-incident stabilization.

The fail-closed distinction is: **Telemetry emitted is not alert delivered is not
incident handled.** Emitting telemetry does not establish that an alert was delivered
to a responder, and a delivered alert does not establish that an incident was
correctly handled and resolved.

## Continuity and recovery evidence

Recovery evidence would establish that the vertical survives loss and can be
restored to correct, reconciled service. It must distinguish backup, backup
verification, restorability, restoration, service recovery, and business
reconciliation.

Two fail-closed distinctions govern continuity:

- **Backup completed is not backup verified is not backup restorable.** A completed
  backup is not a verified backup, and a verified backup is not proof that it can be
  restored.
- **Restore completed is not service recovered is not business reconciled.** A
  completed restore is not a recovered service, and a recovered service is not a
  business state reconciled against the governed system of record.

## Provider operational evidence

Provider evidence at the operational level would establish that provider-mediated
capabilities behave correctly across provider incidents, provider continuity,
subcontractor exposure, data return, data deletion, residual copies, provider
substitution, reconciliation, and provider exit.

The fail-closed distinction is: **Provider certification is not end-to-end
operational assurance.** A provider's own certification is not proof that the
end-to-end operation, including exit and substitution, meets the governed
obligations.

## Training, competence, and adoption evidence

Training-and-competence evidence would establish that operators and administrators
are trained and demonstrably competent. Adoption evidence would establish that the
operating community sustains correct use over time.

Two fail-closed distinctions govern this area:

- **Training delivered is not competence demonstrated.** Delivering training does not
  establish that the trainee is competent.
- **Onboarding completed is not sustained adoption.** Completing onboarding does not
  establish that correct use is sustained after onboarding ends.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing here asserts that any service, backup, recovery, provider engagement,
training, or adoption exists or has occurred. Operational, continuity, recovery,
provider, training, competence, and adoption evidence are defined as obligations for
a later, separately authorized package. No operation, backup, restore, recovery,
provider engagement, training, or adoption activity is executed or accepted.
