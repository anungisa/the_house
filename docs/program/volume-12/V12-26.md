## Purpose

This chapter consolidates the required final dossiers and their authoritative sources.
It is recorded in REG-1201 as the `FINAL_DOSSIER_MODEL` and in REG-1202 as
`FINAL_DOSSIER_REQUIREMENT` records. A dossier is a governed container that names its
authoritative contents and decision destination; it is not itself evidence and not
itself an acceptance.

## The required final dossiers

The required final dossiers are: the implementation and build dossier; the functional
and contract dossier; the security and privacy dossier; the accessibility and bilingual
dossier; the data-integrity and financial-control dossier; the migration-acceptance
dossier; the operational-readiness dossier; the provider-assurance dossier; the
training, competence, and adoption dossier; the business-acceptance brief; the
independent-assurance summary; and the executive release-decision brief.

## Per-dossier composition

For every dossier the model identifies its authoritative contents, required evidence
classes, evidence-source records, coverage requirements, known limitations, findings
and conditions, decision authority, independence requirement, expiry and revalidation
dependencies, and gate destination.

## Fail-closed distinctions

> Dossier defined is not dossier populated. Dossier populated is not acceptance granted.

> Generated summaries remain non-authoritative projections.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter populates any dossier or grants any acceptance. The model
defines dossier structure and authoritative sources; the generated dossier catalogue is
a non-authoritative projection of the source-controlled corpus.
