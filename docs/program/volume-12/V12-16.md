# V12-16 — Accessibility, Bilingual, Usability, Content, and Document Evidence

## Purpose

This chapter defines the accessibility, bilingual, usability, content,
notification, and document evidence that would be required to accept the
club-affiliation vertical. Each requirement is recorded in REG-1202 under its
controlled kind. No interface is built, no scan is run, and no translation is
validated by this chapter.

## Accessibility evidence

Accessibility evidence would establish that the applicant and administrative
experiences are usable by people with disabilities. Accessibility evidence must
distinguish, and separately satisfy, automated scanning, manual inspection,
keyboard-only completion, assistive-technology completion, accessible document
handling, and behaviour under interruption and recovery. Automated coverage alone is
never sufficient.

The fail-closed distinction is: **Automated accessibility scan is not manual
inspection is not keyboard completion is not assistive-technology completion is not
conformance determination.** Each layer is a distinct obligation, and a passing
automated scan establishes none of the others; a conformance determination is a
separate authority's decision that rests on all of them.

## Bilingual and semantic-equivalence evidence

Bilingual-semantic evidence would establish that the affiliation experience is fully
and equivalently available in English and French, in content, notifications, and
documents. Bilingual evidence must distinguish string presence, translation accuracy,
semantic equivalence, and a validated bilingual experience.

The fail-closed distinction is: **English and French strings exist is not translation
accuracy is not semantic equivalence is not validated bilingual experience.** The
presence of both-language strings does not establish that the translations are
accurate, that they are semantically equivalent in context, or that the end-to-end
bilingual experience has been validated.

## Usability, content, notification, and document evidence

Usability, content, notification, and document evidence would establish that the
governed content, notifications, and generated documents are correct, complete,
accessible, and bilingual, and that they behave correctly under interruption and
recovery. These obligations inherit both the accessibility and bilingual boundaries
above.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing here asserts that any interface, scan, translation, notification, or document
exists or has been validated. Accessibility, bilingual, usability, content,
notification, and document evidence are defined as obligations for a later,
separately authorized package. No scan is executed, no translation is validated, and
no conformance is determined or accepted.
