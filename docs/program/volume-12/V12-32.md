## Purpose

This chapter is the integrated closure assessment for Volume 12 Package 3 and for the
whole central-registration definition corpus. It states, as a single governed
assessment, whether the corpus is sufficiently traceable, evidence-governed,
decision-segregated, blocker-aware, acceptance-bounded, and release-disciplined to be
closed and published while implementation, evidence acceptance, system release, and
production authority remain withheld. It is projected non-authoritatively by the
Package 3 final-closure control into `generated/final-closure/`.

## What the assessment consolidates

The assessment consolidates: the integrated final-evidence baseline; the whole-program
traceability coverage; the program-gate matrix; the evidence-status and revalidation
ledger; the findings, waivers, conditions, commitments, and release-blocker
disposition; the final-dossier composition; the affiliation acceptance synthesis; the
independence and challenge model; the executive decision and release-authorization
model; the post-release obligation model; and the corpus-closure and handoff model.

- Documentary corpus: COMPLETE_AND_INTERNALLY_CONSISTENT

## Fail-closed prohibitions

The assessment must never be represented as:

> a software release, an evidence acceptance, a gate passage beyond the Volume 12 definition gates, or a production authorization.

No evidence, assurance conclusion, acceptance decision, gate passage, or system-release
authorization is fabricated by this assessment or by any Package 3 chapter.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter releases software, accepts evidence, passes an execution or
acceptance gate, or authorizes production. Documentary corpus release is not software
release.
