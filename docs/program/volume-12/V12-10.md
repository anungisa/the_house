## Purpose

This chapter records the deterministic Package 1 assessment of the gate, release,
and acceptance-evidence governance foundation. It reports coverage and gaps from the
source-controlled corpus. It accepts no evidence and authorizes no release.

## Assessment

The deterministic controls assess the corpus for:

- requirements without evidence requirements;
- evidence requirements without provenance;
- evidence without scope or expiry rules;
- gates without decision authorities;
- acceptance classes without independence requirements;
- defects without retest requirements;
- waivers without authority and expiry;
- material commitments without owners;
- evidence classes without prohibited-inference rules;
- release candidates without configuration and environment evidence;
- acceptance decisions without required evidence;
- contradictory evidence without challenge procedures;
- revoked evidence without downstream-gate effects;
- final dossiers without authoritative sources;
- unresolved items pointing to completed gates;
- fabricated-evidence leakage;
- unsupported readiness, acceptance, conformance, assurance, or release claims;
- records authorizing implementation, operations, or release.
