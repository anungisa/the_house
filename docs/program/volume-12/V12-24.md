## Purpose

This chapter defines the authoritative status vocabulary for evidence and the rules
that keep evidence requirement, availability, validity, sufficiency, acceptance, and
revalidation distinct. It is recorded in REG-1201 as the `EVIDENCE_STATUS_MODEL` and in
REG-1202 as `EVIDENCE_SUFFICIENCY_REQUIREMENT`, `EVIDENCE_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIREMENT`, and
`EVIDENCE_REVALIDATION_REQUIREMENT` records.

## Status vocabulary

The authoritative evidence status vocabulary is: NOT_REQUIRED, REQUIRED,
NOT_AVAILABLE, SUBMITTED, UNDER_REVIEW, INVALID, INCONCLUSIVE, INSUFFICIENT,
CONFLICTED, STALE, ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS, ACCEPTED, REVOKED, and
REVALIDATION_REQUIRED.

## Per-requirement control

For every evidence requirement the ledger controls its evidence state, validity state,
sufficiency state, acceptance state, scope, version, environment, configuration, data
context, provider state, review authority, expiry, revalidation trigger, and affected
gates.

## Fail-closed rules

The ledger enforces the rules that prevent one evidence state from being read as
another:

> Submitted is not valid.

> Valid is not sufficient.

> Sufficient is not accepted.

> Accepted is not current indefinitely.

> Changed configuration or scope requires a revalidation assessment.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter places any evidence into a submitted, valid, sufficient, or
accepted state. The ledger defines the states and their transitions; it records no
evidence and takes no acceptance decision.
