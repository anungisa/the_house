## Purpose

This chapter defines how deficient, contradictory, challenged, invalidated, and
revoked evidence is handled, so that no deficiency is hidden and no revocation is
without downstream effect. It defines these handling obligations; it detects,
revalidates, or revokes nothing.

## Handled conditions

The following conditions are governed, and each is recorded in register REG-1202 as
a finding or condition. Failed, partial, stale, conflicting, and inconclusive
evidence all remain visible:

- missing evidence;
- failed verification;
- stale evidence;
- unverifiable provenance;
- corrupted evidence;
- inconclusive evidence;
- conflicting results;
- partial execution;
- changed configurations;
- changed provider state;
- changed requirements;
- challenged evidence;
- dissenting review;
- invalidated evidence;
- revoked acceptance;
- reopened gates.

## Required record content

For every condition, register REG-1202 identifies:

```
Detection method
Initial disposition
Owner
Affected requirement
Affected decision
Affected gate
Required investigation
Required re-execution or revalidation
Independence requirement
Temporary release effect
Final decision authority
Audit requirement
```

## Fixed rules

The following rules are fixed and fail closed:

> Inconclusive evidence is distinct from a pass and from a fail.
> Conflicting evidence is distinct from evidence averaging.
> Prior acceptance is distinct from permanent acceptance.
> A changed configuration requires evidence-scope reassessment.

Challenged evidence follows a defined challenge procedure; invalidated evidence and
revoked acceptance propagate to their affected gates, and reopened gates reinstate
their unmet conditions. Every challenge, revalidation, invalidation, and revocation
is recorded with an audit requirement.

## Boundary

This chapter records the evidence-deficiency and revocation model. It detects no
deficiency, revalidates no evidence, revokes no acceptance, and authorizes no
release. Every record carries the fixed non-authorizing posture.
