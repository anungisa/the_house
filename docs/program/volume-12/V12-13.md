## Purpose

This chapter defines the identity, authority, security, privacy, records, and audit
evidence that would be required to accept the club-affiliation vertical. Each
requirement is recorded in REG-1202 under its controlled kind. No identity is
created, no credential is issued, no security test is run, and no personal data is
processed by this chapter.

## Identity, account, and authority-boundary evidence

Authorization evidence would establish that every governed action in the affiliation
vertical is permitted only for the correct actor in the correct scope. The evidence
must draw explicit boundaries across the actor model: applicant identity, account
establishment, membership and roster context, authority, delegation, assignment,
finance role, support role, administration role, and service identity. Each boundary
requires both positive evidence that a permitted actor may act and denial evidence
that an impermissible actor may not.

Two isolation boundaries carry mandatory denial-evidence requirements:

- **Cross-organization denial.** Evidence must establish that an actor scoped to one
  organization cannot read, act on, or affect an affiliation belonging to another
  organization.
- **Cross-jurisdiction denial.** Evidence must establish that an actor scoped to one
  jurisdiction cannot read, act on, or affect an affiliation governed by another
  jurisdiction.

The fail-closed distinction that governs this area is: **Authentication evidence is
not authorization evidence.** Proving that an actor is who they claim to be is not
proof that the actor is permitted to perform a governed action in a given scope.

## Security and privacy evidence

Security evidence would establish that the affiliation vertical resists the threats
in its governed threat model. Privacy-and-records evidence would establish that
personal data is collected, used, exported, retained, and disposed of under the
governed privacy and records obligations, including export, retention, and
disposition controls.

The fail-closed distinction is: **Security evidence is not privacy compliance is not
legal compliance.** Demonstrating technical resistance to a threat is not the same as
demonstrating privacy compliance, and neither is equivalent to a legal-compliance
determination, which is a distinct authority's decision.

## Records and audit evidence

Records-and-audit evidence would establish that governed actions produce complete,
attributable, tamper-evident audit records, and that records obligations for
retention and disposition are met. Audit evidence must address completeness, not
merely the presence of some records.

The fail-closed distinction is: **Audit record exists is not audit completeness is
not institutional acceptance.** The existence of an audit record does not establish
that the audit trail is complete, and a complete audit trail is not itself an
institutional acceptance decision.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing here asserts that any identity, credential, security control, privacy
control, record, or audit trail exists or has been exercised or accepted. Identity,
authority, security, privacy, records, and audit evidence are defined as obligations
for a later, separately authorized package. No security or privacy test is executed,
no personal data is processed, and no compliance determination is made or accepted.
