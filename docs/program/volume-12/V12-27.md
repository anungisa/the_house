## Purpose

This chapter defines the documentary acceptance synthesis for the first vertical, the
club-affiliation lifecycle, and records it in REG-1201 as
`AFFILIATION_ACCEPTANCE_SYNTHESIS` records. It draws together every evidence class the
vertical would require across its complete lifecycle without producing, accepting, or
releasing any of it.

## The affiliation lifecycle

The synthesis follows the affiliation lifecycle:

> Representative authority → club identity → requirements and evidence → submission → review and correction → decision → finance and reconciliation → activation → standing and expiry.

## Per-stage synthesis

For each stage the synthesis identifies the implementation evidence, functional
evidence, authorization evidence, contract and event evidence, data-integrity evidence,
accessibility and bilingual evidence, migration evidence, operational evidence, known
findings, acceptance authority, and release-blocking conditions.

## Required posture

The synthesis carries the fail-closed posture for the vertical:

- Affiliation evidence definition: COMPLETE
- Affiliation evidence available: NOT_PROVEN
- Affiliation acceptance: NOT_GRANTED
- Affiliation release authorization: NOT_GRANTED

## Fail-closed distinctions

> Affiliation evidence definition complete is not affiliation evidence available is not affiliation acceptance granted is not affiliation release authorized.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter proves that any affiliation evidence exists, grants any
affiliation acceptance, or authorizes any affiliation release. The synthesis is a
documentary composition of obligations across the lifecycle.
