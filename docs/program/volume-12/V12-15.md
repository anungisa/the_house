## Purpose

This chapter defines the data-integrity, financial-reconciliation, migration,
activation, and standing evidence that would be required to accept the
club-affiliation vertical. Each requirement is recorded in REG-1202 under its
controlled kind. No data is migrated, no payment is settled, and no affiliation is
activated by this chapter.

## Data-integrity evidence

Data-integrity evidence would establish that the affiliation system of record
preserves its governed invariants: tenant and parent scoping, uniqueness, versioning,
history, provenance, and reconciliation against authoritative sources. Integrity
evidence must bind to observed physical behaviour under concurrency, not to declared
constraints alone.

## Financial-reconciliation evidence

Financial-reconciliation evidence would establish that affiliation fees are
determined, settled, recorded, and reconciled correctly across the applicant, the
provider, and the governed accounting system of record.

The fail-closed distinction is: **Provider acknowledgement is not accounting
confirmation is not reconciliation.** A provider's acknowledgement of a payment is not
a confirmation in the accounting system of record, and neither an acknowledgement nor
an accounting entry is a completed reconciliation across the two.

## Activation and standing evidence

Activation-and-standing evidence would establish that an approved affiliation is
activated exactly once and reaches, and maintains, correct active standing.

Two fail-closed distinctions govern this area:

- **Approval is not activation authorization is not activation execution is not
  active standing.** Approving an affiliation does not authorize its activation,
  authorizing activation does not execute it, and executing activation does not by
  itself establish sustained active standing.
- **Exactly-once activation is a business invariant and not an exactly-once transport
  claim.** The requirement that an affiliation is activated exactly once is a property
  of the business system of record; it is not satisfied by any transport-level
  delivery guarantee.

## Migration evidence

Migration evidence would establish that prior affiliation records are migrated with
preserved source authority, provenance, and recorded uncertainty; that identity
candidates are resolved or quarantined; and that cutover is rehearsed, executed,
reversible, accepted, and only then followed by source retirement. The migration
evidence requirement covers source authority, provenance, uncertainty, identity
candidates, quarantine, rehearsal, cutover, rollback, business acceptance, and source
retirement.

Two fail-closed distinctions govern migration:

- **Mapping complete is not identity resolved.** A complete field-level mapping does
  not establish that a real-world identity has been correctly and uniquely resolved.
- **Migration executed is not migration accepted is not source retired.** Executing a
  migration is not the same as accepting its result, and accepting a migration is a
  precondition for — not the same as — retiring the source of record.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing here asserts that any data has been migrated, any payment settled, any
reconciliation completed, or any affiliation activated. Data-integrity,
financial-reconciliation, migration, activation, and standing evidence are defined as
obligations for a later, separately authorized package. No migration, settlement,
reconciliation, or activation is executed or accepted.
