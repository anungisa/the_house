## Purpose

This chapter defines how findings, defects, evidence gaps, exceptions, waivers,
conditions, material commitments, and challenges would be controlled during
acceptance of the club-affiliation vertical. It defines governance; it closes no
finding, approves no waiver, and fulfils no commitment.

## Findings, defects, and evidence gaps

A finding, defect, or evidence gap is a controlled record with an owner, an affected
requirement or invariant, a severity determined by the governed method, the required
remediation, and the retest or re-exercise evidence that closure would require. A
finding is not closed by remediation alone.

The fail-closed distinction is: **Change implemented is not finding closed.**
Implementing a change does not close a finding; closure requires the applicable
retest or re-exercise evidence to be produced and accepted.

## Waivers, conditions, and commitments

A waiver, condition, or material commitment is a controlled record with an approving
authority, an effective period, an expiry, a revalidation condition, and a release
effect. Waivers and conditions are governed exceptions; they do not alter results.

Three fail-closed distinctions govern exceptions:

- **Waiver approved is not failed result converted to pass.** Approving a waiver
  records an accepted, bounded exception; it does not convert a failed or inconclusive
  result into a successful one.
- **Conditional acceptance is not unconditional acceptance.** A conditional acceptance
  carries outstanding obligations and is not equivalent to unconditional acceptance.
- **Commitment recorded is not commitment fulfilled.** Recording a material commitment
  is not evidence that the commitment has been fulfilled.

Expiry is enforced fail-closed: **Expired waiver or condition is a fail-closed release
condition.** An expired waiver or condition, or an unfulfilled commitment past its
date, blocks release until revalidated.

## Challenge and revocation

The challenge procedure governs how accepted evidence and acceptance decisions can be
contested and undone. It defines challenge, contradiction, dissent, invalidation,
revocation, and gate reopening: previously accepted evidence can be challenged,
contradicted by later evidence, dissented from, invalidated, have its acceptance
revoked, and cause a dispositioned gate to be reopened.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing here closes a finding, approves a waiver, grants a conditional acceptance,
fulfils a commitment, or reopens a gate. Findings, defects, gaps, waivers, conditions,
commitments, and challenge procedures are defined as governance obligations for a
later, separately authorized package. No exception is approved and no result is
altered.
