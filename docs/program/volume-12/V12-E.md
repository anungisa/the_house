## Purpose

This chapter is the closure record for Volume 12 Package 3, for the whole of
Volume 12, and for the whole central-registration definition corpus. It records that
the integrated final-evidence baseline, the whole-program traceability coverage, the
program-gate matrix, the evidence-status and revalidation ledger, the disposition
model, the final-dossier model, the affiliation acceptance synthesis, the independence
and challenge model, the executive decision and release-authorization model, the
post-release obligation model, and the corpus-closure and handoff model have been
authored; that Gate V12-G3 has been dispositioned; that Package 3, the whole of
Volume 12, and the whole definition corpus are frozen; and that a bounded authorization
for a separate implementation-authorization decision package has been granted. It
authorizes no implementation, no evidence acceptance, no system release, and no
production.
