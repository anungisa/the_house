## Purpose

This chapter consolidates the controls that keep evidence ownership, execution,
review, acceptance, independent assurance, and release authority segregated, and that
govern conflict, dissent, challenge, contradiction, invalidation, revocation, and
reopening. It is recorded in REG-1201 as the `INDEPENDENCE_AND_CHALLENGE_MODEL` records.

## Segregated roles

The model segregates: evidence ownership; execution; evidence review; domain
acceptance; operational acceptance; business acceptance; independent assurance; and
executive authorization.

## Controls defined

The model defines conflict-of-interest treatment, minimum independence, permitted
delegation, prohibited role combinations, dissent recording, the challenge procedure,
contradictory-evidence treatment, acceptance revocation, gate reopening, and the
required audit evidence for each.

## Fail-closed distinctions

> Evidence producer is not evidence reviewer is not acceptance authority.

> Executive sponsor is not independent assurer.

> Prior acceptance is not irrevocable acceptance.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter appoints a reviewer, grants an acceptance, records a dissent,
revokes an acceptance, or reopens a gate. The model defines the segregation and
challenge controls; it exercises none of them.
