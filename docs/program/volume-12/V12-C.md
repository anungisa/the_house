## Purpose

This chapter is the closure record for Volume 12 Package 2. It records that the
affiliation evidence requirements and acceptance dossiers for the club-affiliation
vertical have been authored, that Gate V12-G2 has been dispositioned, that Package
2 is frozen, and that a bounded authorization for Package 3 has been granted. It
admits no evidence, accepts nothing, passes no gate against a real workload, and
authorizes no release.

## What Package 2 delivered

Package 2 applied the Package 1 governance foundation to a single concrete
vertical. It defined, in documentary form, the affiliation evidence domain and its
acceptance-dossier architecture; the implementation, source, build, database,
environment, configuration, and deployment-path evidence requirements; the
identity, authorization, security, privacy, records, and audit evidence
requirements, including cross-organization and cross-jurisdiction isolation denial;
the functional, contract, integration, event, webhook, and provider evidence
requirements; the data-integrity, financial-reconciliation, migration, activation,
and standing evidence requirements; the accessibility and bilingual semantic
evidence requirements; the operational, continuity, recovery, provider, training,
competence, and adoption evidence requirements; the finding, defect, waiver,
condition, material-commitment, and challenge controls; the affiliation acceptance
classes and their decision authorities; the affiliation evidence dossiers; and the
executive release-decision brief, including the House P0 evidence and
release-destination map. It delivered these as governance obligations recorded in
registers REG-1200 through REG-1205, with deterministic controls and
non-authoritative projections.

## Posture at closure

Every controlled record remains not-implemented, not-operational, not-executed,
with no evidence available or accepted, nothing accepted, and no release
authorized. No record authorizes implementation, operations, or release. Nothing
has been built, tested, migrated, operated, accepted, or released. No evidence has
been produced, submitted, validated, accepted, or approved. The affiliation
evidence definition is documentary. Executed proof remains a future concern,
distinct from the documentary definition recorded here.

## Distinctions preserved at closure

This closure preserves the framework's core distinctions. Evidence-domain coverage
complete is distinct from evidence available and from evidence accepted.
Implementation completed is distinct from implementation evidence accepted. Schema
inspection is distinct from proven database behaviour. Authentication is distinct
from authorization. A mocked or single-provider result is distinct from end-to-end
integration and operational evidence. Migration executed is distinct from migration
accepted. Accessibility automation is distinct from accessibility acceptance.
Bilingual string presence is distinct from validated bilingual experience.
Operational exercise is distinct from operational acceptance. Technical acceptance
is distinct from business, operational, and executive acceptance. Executive
authorization cannot substitute for missing evidence.
