## Purpose

This chapter defines the controlled final decision hierarchy, the decision rights
attached to each decision class, and the segregation of duties that keeps evidence
ownership, evidence review, acceptance, and release authority apart. It defines
decision obligations; it takes no decision and authorizes no release.

## The controlled final decision hierarchy

The following decision classes are defined and held distinct, and each is recorded
in register REG-1201 as a gate criterion or release decision:

```
Evidence-intake decision
Evidence-validity decision
Evidence-sufficiency decision
Domain-acceptance decision
Operational-acceptance decision
Business-acceptance decision
Independent-assurance conclusion
Material-commitment decision
Release-gate decision
Executive release authorization
Deployment authorization
Post-deployment stabilization acceptance
```

## Decision rights

For every decision class, register REG-1201 identifies:

```
Decision authority
Required competence
Required independence
Permitted inputs
Required evidence
Permitted disposition
Prohibited inference
Conflict-of-interest treatment
Delegation rule
Escalation path
Reconsideration rule
Revocation rule
Downstream gate
```

Every gate criterion names a decision authority and an escalation path, so no
decision is unowned and no dispute is without a route.

## Segregation of duties

Segregation of duties is a controlling requirement of this volume. Evidence
ownership, evidence review, evidence acceptance, and release authority are held by
distinct authorities recorded in register REG-1201, each with a segregation
boundary and a conflict-of-interest treatment. The following separations are fixed:

> Evidence reviewer is distinct from evidence owner.
> Evidence acceptance is distinct from business acceptance.
> Operational acceptance is distinct from release authorization.
> Executive sponsorship is distinct from technical verification.
> Release authorization is distinct from deployment execution.

## Boundary

This chapter records a gate and decision architecture with segregation of duties.
It takes no decision, accepts no evidence, passes no gate, and authorizes no
release. Every record carries the fixed non-authorizing posture.
