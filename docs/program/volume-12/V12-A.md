## Purpose

This chapter is the closure record for Volume 12 Package 1. It records that the
gate, release, and acceptance-evidence governance foundation has been authored,
that Gate V12-G1 has been dispositioned, that Package 1 is frozen, and that a
bounded authorization for Package 2 has been granted. It admits no evidence,
accepts nothing, passes no gate against a real workload, and authorizes no
release.

## What Package 1 delivered

Package 1 delivered the documentary gate, release, and acceptance-evidence
governance foundation: the evidence-class taxonomy and evidentiary limits; the
decision-class model and segregation of duties; the evidence-object binding,
integrity, reproducibility, and retention model; the defect, exception, waiver,
condition, and expiry model; the cross-domain evidence-requirement catalogue; the
implementation, environment, configuration, database, deployment-path, and
release-candidate evidence model; the acceptance-class model; the evidence
challenge, revalidation, invalidation, and revocation model; and the final
evidence-dossier, executive-decision, and release-authorization model. It
delivered these as governance obligations recorded in registers REG-1200 through
REG-1205, with deterministic controls and non-authoritative projections.

## Posture at closure

Every controlled record remains not-implemented, not-operational, not-executed,
with no evidence available or accepted, nothing accepted, and no release
authorized. No record authorizes implementation, operations, or release. Nothing
has been built, tested, migrated, operated, accepted, or released. The foundation
is documentary. Executed proof remains a future concern, distinct from the
documentary definition recorded here.

## Distinctions preserved at closure

This closure preserves the framework's core distinctions. Evidence requirement is
distinct from evidence submitted, from evidence valid, and from evidence accepted.
Evidence accepted is distinct from a gate passed. A gate passed is distinct from a
release authorized. A release authorized is distinct from a deployment executed. A
test passed is distinct from operational readiness. Documentary approval is
distinct from executed proof.

## Gate V12-G1 disposition

Gate V12-G1 is dispositioned
`GATE_RELEASE_AND_ACCEPTANCE_EVIDENCE_GOVERNANCE_FOUNDATION_READY` in REG-1205. The
disposition attests only that the documentary foundation is complete and
internally coherent against the gate's conditions. It confers no readiness to
implement, operate, accept, or release, and it is not evidence of any obligation.

## Package 1 freeze

Package 1 is frozen in REG-1205 as `PACKAGE-12-1`. The frozen artifacts are the
Volume 12 chapters and registers at version 1.0.0. The freeze is a point-in-time
snapshot; later packages are additive and do not reopen the frozen substantive
content of Package 1.

## Bounded Package 2 authorization

Package 2 of Volume 12 is authorized for definition work only, bounded as follows:

> AUTHORIZED FOR AFFILIATION IMPLEMENTATION, TEST, SECURITY, PRIVACY,
> ACCESSIBILITY, BILINGUAL, FINANCIAL, DATA, MIGRATION, OPERATIONAL, PROVIDER,
> TRAINING, ADOPTION, BUSINESS, AND EXECUTIVE EVIDENCE-REQUIREMENT AND
> ACCEPTANCE-DOSSIER DEFINITION ONLY.

The following are explicitly NOT AUTHORIZED by this closure: any implementation,
build, or code change; any test, security, privacy, accessibility, bilingual,
financial, data-integrity, or migration execution; any operational, backup,
restore, recovery, or continuity exercise; any provider engagement; any training
delivery or adoption activity; any evidence production, acceptance decision, gate
passage against a real workload, release authorization, or deployment. Package 2
continues the documentary discipline of Package 1: it defines evidence
requirements and acceptance dossiers, and it authorizes no evidence, no
acceptance, and no release.

## Handoff

The unresolved items recorded in REG-1204 are carried forward through the bounded
Package 2 authorization, so that no assumption, risk, evidence gap, material
commitment, acceptance backlog, or release blocker is lost across the boundary.
