## Purpose

This chapter defines the evidence and obligations that would follow a release
authorization: deployment, stabilization, rollback, hypercare, and sustained
post-release obligation. It is recorded in REG-1201 as the
`POST_RELEASE_OBLIGATION_MODEL` and in REG-1202 as `DEPLOYMENT_EVIDENCE_REQUIREMENT` and
`STABILIZATION_EVIDENCE_REQUIREMENT` records. It defines what must be shown after a
release; it authorizes no release and no deployment.

## Scope of the model

The model governs: deployment execution evidence; deployment verification evidence;
stabilization and hypercare evidence; error-budget and incident evidence; rollback and
recovery evidence; provider-continuity evidence; data-reconciliation evidence; support
and operational-outcome evidence; and the disposition of post-release obligations and
their revalidation.

## Per-obligation control

For every post-release obligation the model identifies its trigger, evidence class,
owner, acceptance authority, monitoring period, exit criteria, escalation route, and
revalidation dependency.

## Fail-closed distinctions

> Release authorized is not deployed.

> Deployed is not functioning correctly.

> Service available is not stabilized.

> Stabilized is not institutional outcomes accepted.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter deploys, stabilizes, or operates any service. The model defines
post-release obligations; none has been triggered, met, or accepted.
