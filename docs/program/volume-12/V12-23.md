## Purpose

This chapter defines the complete gate hierarchy of the program and records it in
REG-1201 as `PROGRAM_GATE_MATRIX` records. The matrix distinguishes the definition
gates already dispositioned in Volume 12 from the future execution, evidence,
acceptance, release, deployment, and stabilization gates that remain unpassed.

## The gate hierarchy

The complete gate hierarchy comprises: the definition-completeness gates; the
implementation-authorization gate; the environment-qualification gate; the
test-execution authorization gate; the migration-execution authorization gate; the
operational-exercise authorization gate; the evidence-intake gate; the domain-
acceptance gates; the operational-acceptance gate; the business-acceptance gate; the
independent-assurance gate; the executive release-authorization gate; the deployment-
authorization gate; and the stabilization-acceptance gate.

## Gate specification

For every gate the matrix identifies its purpose, entry criteria, required evidence,
required decisions, decision authority, independence requirement, permitted
dispositions, blocking conditions, escalation route, reconsideration rule, revocation
rule, and downstream authority.

## Fail-closed distinctions

The gate matrix preserves the distinctions that keep authority from leaking across
gates:

> Earlier gate passed is not later gate passed.

> Definition gate is not execution authorization.

> Evidence-intake gate is not evidence-acceptance gate.

> Release authorization is not deployment authorization.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter passes any gate. Defining a gate, its criteria, and its
authority is not passing it, and no gate beyond the Volume 12 definition gates has been
entered, satisfied, or dispositioned.
