## Purpose

This chapter defines the executive release-decision brief and the release-authorization
record that a future, separately authorized release decision would populate. It is
recorded in REG-1201 as the `EXECUTIVE_DECISION_MODEL` and in REG-1202 as
`EXECUTIVE_DECISION_EVIDENCE_REQUIREMENT` records. It defines the shape of a decision;
it takes no decision.

## The decision brief

The executive decision brief identifies the decisions expressly being requested and,
by exclusion, the decisions expressly not being requested. Its required content
includes: the release scope; the accepted dossiers; the outstanding findings and
release blockers; the conditional acceptances and their expiry; the material
commitments; the independence and assurance summary; the residual-risk statement; the
rollback and continuity position; the post-release obligations; the decision options;
the required authorities; the dissent record; the revocation terms; the reconsideration
terms; the effective-date clarification; and the explicit statement of what the
decision does not authorize.

## The release-authorization record

The release-authorization record model defines the fields a granted authorization would
carry — scope, conditions, authorities, effective boundaries, and withheld authorities —
without any field being populated with a decision.

## Fail-closed distinctions

> A decision brief is not a decision. A release-authorization record model is not a granted release authorization.

The documentary corpus must not populate this model with fabricated acceptance, gate-passage, or release decisions.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter authorizes a release. The model defines the executive decision
and authorization artifacts; no decision is requested, taken, or recorded here.
