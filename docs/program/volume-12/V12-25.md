# V12-25 — Findings, Waivers, Conditions, Material Commitments, and Release-Blocker Disposition

Ratification: Ratified as the integrated disposition model of Volume 12 Package 3.

## Purpose

This chapter creates the integrated disposition model for every finding, gap,
exception, waiver, condition, compensating control, material commitment, deferred
obligation, release blocker, expired condition, and reopened finding. It is recorded in
REG-1201 as the `RELEASE_BLOCKER_MODEL` and in REG-1202 as `RELEASE_BLOCKER_REQUIREMENT`
and `MATERIAL_COMMITMENT_EVIDENCE_REQUIREMENT` records.

## Scope of the disposition model

The disposition model governs: defects; evidence gaps; assurance gaps; unresolved
dependencies; exceptions; waivers; conditional acceptance; compensating controls;
material commitments; deferred obligations; release blockers; expired conditions; and
reopened findings.

## Per-record disposition

Every record identifies its historical source, affected requirement, affected
invariant, affected gate, owner, required remediation, required evidence, retest or
re-exercise dependency, approving authority, effective period, expiry, revalidation
condition, release effect, and current disposition.

## Allowed final dispositions

The allowed final dispositions are: REQUIRES_IMPLEMENTATION_EVIDENCE,
REQUIRES_TEST_EVIDENCE, REQUIRES_MIGRATION_EVIDENCE, REQUIRES_OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE,
REQUIRES_PROVIDER_ASSURANCE, REQUIRES_INDEPENDENT_ASSURANCE,
REQUIRES_BUSINESS_ACCEPTANCE, REQUIRES_EXECUTIVE_DECISION, MATERIAL_COMMITMENT_PENDING,
RELEASE_BLOCKER, ACCEPTED_AS_POST_RELEASE_OBLIGATION, and
DEFECT_REQUIRING_CORPUS_AMENDMENT.

## Fail-closed rules

> Waiver approved is not failed result converted to pass. Conditional acceptance is not unconditional acceptance.

> Expired conditions and unresolved release blockers fail closed.

> No active unresolved item may point to a completed Volume 12 gate.

## Prohibited inferences

Nothing in this chapter closes a finding, grants a waiver, satisfies a condition,
discharges a commitment, or clears a release blocker. The model defines how such items
are dispositioned; it disposes none of them into a resolved state.
