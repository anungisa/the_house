## V4-48.1 Purpose and scope

This section is normative.

This chapter provides a concise executive handoff for Nolan, Rich, Hélène, Jen, and the delivery
stakeholders. It explains what Volume 4 defined and what remains undefined or unproven. It introduces no
new authority, no new architecture, and no implementation claim. It is a communication of the ratified
corpus, not an extension of it.

## V4-48.2 What Volume 4 defined

This section is normative.

Volume 4 defined the target architecture for The House: the system context and the House and Button
authority separation; the target modular architecture and bounded contexts; how affiliation operates
technically; how evidence and decisions are protected; how financial authority remains segregated; how
integrations and external systems are constrained; and what security, privacy, resilience, and
verification require. It defined engineering standards, secure-development controls, test architecture,
migration and coexistence, and architecture evolution, and it closed the volume with an integrated
target-architecture baseline.
