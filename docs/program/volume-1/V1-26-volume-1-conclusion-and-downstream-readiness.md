## V1-26.1 Purpose

This section is normative.

This chapter concludes the convergence package and states downstream readiness. It
summarizes what Volume 1 established, what it deliberately did not do, and what is now
potentially authorized versus what remains reserved. The formal closure, freeze, and
Gate V1-G5 disposition are recorded in the closure record V1-E.

## V1-26.2 What Volume 1 established

This section is normative.

Volume 1 converged three ratified evidence streams — Base44 product intelligence
(Package 2), The House implementation truth (Package 3), and Curling Canada operating and
ecosystem reality (Package 4) — into a defensible target disposition:

- A methodology and evidence-precedence discipline in which claim type determines
  controlling evidence (V1-21).
- Twelve unified target capabilities (CAP-037..CAP-048), each dispositioned
  independently at four layers across 29 qualification decisions (QD-037..QD-065)
  (V1-22).
- Target system, data, and reconciliation authority across thirteen governed domains,
  with manual controls dispositioned and three material contradictions retained open
  (V1-23).
- A complete affiliation target operating model, three governed transition pathways
  including historical/goodwill continuity, and an explicit first-release boundary with
  exclusions (V1-24).
- A transition-constraint set and stakeholder-validation backlog, each item owned and
  routed to the material-commitment gate (V1-25).

## V1-26.3 What Volume 1 deliberately did not do

This section is normative.

Volume 1 did not implement the application, author a master development plan, authorize
procurement, retire or retain any vendor by default, or resolve any material
contradiction using the wrong authority type. It did not fabricate stakeholder, vendor,
or executive validation. It did not treat current practice as automatically desirable or
newer evidence as automatically authoritative.
