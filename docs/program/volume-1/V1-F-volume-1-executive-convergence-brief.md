## V1-F.1 Purpose and status of this brief

This section is normative.

This is a concise executive summary of the ratified Volume 1 convergence findings,
written so the executive and operating leadership (Nolan, Rich, Hélène, Jen) can consume
the conclusion without reading all 32 chapters. It **summarizes** ratified findings; it is
**not a new source of authority** and it introduces **no new target decision**. Where a
detail matters, the controlling record is the underlying chapter or register, not this
brief. This brief authorizes no implementation, no procurement, and no master development
plan.

## V1-F.2 What was assessed

This section is normative.

Volume 1 assessed three independent evidence streams and converged them into one
defensible target disposition:

- **Base44 product intelligence** — the current curl-link-hub export, qualified as
  product-design evidence, not production authority (Package 2).
- **The House implementation truth** — the governed backend repository, qualified as
  production-candidate implementation truth at runtime commit `de6312f8` (Package 3).
- **Curling Canada operating and ecosystem reality** — the current operating model,
  systems, authority, data flows, and constraints (Package 4).

The convergence discipline is that **claim type determines controlling evidence**: newer,
implemented, or more polished evidence is not automatically authoritative, and a source
authoritative for one claim type is non-authoritative for another (V1-21).

## V1-F.3 What Base44 contributes

This section is normative.

Base44 contributes validated **product and experience intelligence**: the shape of the
stakeholder-facing affiliation experience, required-action views, and the vocabulary of
what clubs and associations expect to see. It does **not** contribute production
authority, governed lifecycle rules, or data-of-record. Its contribution is concentrated
at the experience layer and is adapted, not adopted wholesale.

## V1-F.4 What The House contributes

This section is normative.

The House contributes demonstrated **implementation truth** where it exists: governed
lifecycle state authority, exactly-once activation, and authoritative status projection
(CAP-039, CAP-045, CAP-048; E3 evidence). These are retained. The House does not yet
contain production-ready capability for club recognition, jurisdiction resolution, or
reviewer routing; those are rebuilt rather than assumed.

## V1-F.5 What current operations require

This section is normative.

Current operations require capabilities that live today in manual controls and external
systems: fee determination and reconciliation, payment execution, accounting/ledger
truth, compliance gating, and jurisdiction boundaries. Volume 1 preserves these as
requirements with named owners (notably Hélène for finance and Jen for operations and
compliance) and does not automate or retire any of them without validation.

## V1-F.6 Why affiliation remains first

This section is normative.

Affiliation is the smallest legitimate first vertical because it exercises the full
governed lifecycle end to end while depending least on unresolved commercial and
migration unknowns. It is where implementation truth is strongest and where a governed
first release can be proven before broader scope is attempted.

## V1-F.7 What the first release includes

This section is normative.

The first affiliation release includes fifteen capabilities: club recognition and
establishment, jurisdiction resolution, seasonal affiliation creation, versioned
requirements, evidence binding, submission, resource-aware authorization, reviewer
routing, return and resubmission, governed decision, the payment-reconciliation boundary,
exactly-once activation, Button status and required-action view, notifications, and
audit/operational visibility (V1-24).

## V1-F.8 What it explicitly excludes

This section is normative.

The first release explicitly excludes six categories until the affiliation vertical is
proven: broader membership and registration beyond affiliation, full accounting/ledger
ownership, learning and accreditation, analytics platforms, general-purpose workflow
building, and non-affiliation domain expansion (V1-24).

## V1-F.9 What must be retained, adapted, rebuilt, and externalized

This section is normative.

- **Retain** — governed state authority, exactly-once activation, and status projection
  from The House (CAP-039, CAP-045, CAP-048).
- **Adapt** — the Base44 experience layer and required-action views into the governed
  model.
- **Rebuild** — club recognition, jurisdiction resolution, reviewer routing, and
  return-for-information where no production-ready capability exists (CAP-037, CAP-038,
  CAP-043, CAP-044).
- **Externalize** — payment execution and accounting/ledger truth to their systems of
  record rather than absorbing them (CAP-046, CAP-047), reconciled across a defined
  boundary.
