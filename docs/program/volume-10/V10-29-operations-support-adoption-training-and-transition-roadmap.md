## 1. Purpose

This chapter defines the planned handoff into Volume 11 for operations, support,
adoption, training, and transition. Operational planning is not operational
readiness.

## 2. Operational scope

The roadmap (register REG-1001, kind `OPERATIONAL_CAPABILITY`) covers service
ownership, monitoring and alerting, incident response, support tiers, case
escalation, reconciliation operations, provider operations, data-quality
operations, privacy and records operations, bilingual content operations,
accessibility support, training, club and MA onboarding, communications, adoption
measurement, stabilization, and continuous improvement.

## 3. Per-capability attributes

For every operational capability the roadmap records planned owner role, required
procedures, required training, required tooling, required evidence, service
dependency, provider dependency, continuity dependency, support-readiness
criterion, adoption-readiness criterion, operational-acceptance authority, and
Volume 11 destination.

## 4. Handoff posture

Every operational capability carries an explicit `volume_11_destination`.
Operational planning must not be represented as operational readiness. Nothing in
this chapter authorizes operation, support activation, or adoption execution.
