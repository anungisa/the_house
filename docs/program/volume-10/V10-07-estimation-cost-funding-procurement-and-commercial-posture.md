## 1. Purpose

This chapter defines the estimation, cost, funding, procurement, and commercial
posture of delivery planning. It records intent without creating any financial,
schedule, or commercial commitment.

## 2. Estimation

Estimates are recorded in REG-1004 as cost-estimate records. Every estimate names
its basis, its range, its confidence, its assumptions, its included scope, its
excluded scope, its dependencies, its source date, its currency, and its approval
status, and carries an `estimate_status` of `PLANNING_ESTIMATE`.

## 3. Estimate boundaries

An estimate is not a budget, a quote, a purchase, or a contract. A target date is
not a committed date. Recording an estimate does not approve a budget, commit a
date, authorize a purchase, or form a contract. Budget approval, if any, is a
separate governed decision made at a future gate with authority and evidence.

## 4. Funding

Funding sources are recorded as prospective and unsecured. Funding is neither
secured nor approved by any record in this package. A funding decision is a
governed decision made at a future gate.

## 5. Procurement and commercial posture

Procurement is recorded as prospective and not authorized. No solicitation, no
selection, no negotiation, and no contract is performed or authorized. Provider
engagement and commercial commitment are governed decisions made at a future gate
with authority, budget, and evidence. In Package 1 every commitment and
procurement record carries a `NOT_COMMITTED` status.
