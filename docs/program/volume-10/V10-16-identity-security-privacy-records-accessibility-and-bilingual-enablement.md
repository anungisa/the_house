## 1. Purpose

This chapter defines the cross-cutting control-enablement work packages that the
club-affiliation vertical requires across identity and authorization, privacy and
records, accessibility, and bilingual enablement. Every entry is a plan; no
identity, credential, secret, or certificate is created by this package.

## 2. Identity and authorization

Delivery work packages are defined for: authentication integration; account state;
organization membership; representative authority; delegation; reviewer
assignment; finance and support boundaries; service identities; privileged
administration; and fail-closed policy and configuration. Actor, organization,
jurisdiction, authority, delegation, assignment, and service-identity boundaries
are each represented as distinct planning concerns.

## 3. Privacy and records

Delivery work packages are defined for: minimum necessary collection; restricted
evidence; audit and trace minimization; exports; access and correction; legal-hold
dependencies; retention and disposition dependencies; and provider-held
information.

## 4. Accessibility

Delivery work packages are defined for: semantic implementation; keyboard
operation; focus management; screen-reader status; error recovery; uploads and
documents; staff workbenches; and degraded states.

## 5. Bilingual enablement

Delivery work packages are defined for: canonical concepts; English and French
content; errors and denials; notifications; decisions; financial concepts;
documents; and content approval and semantic review.

## 6. Per-work-package obligations

For every control-enablement work package, the plan records implementation
evidence, required test evidence, an independent-review dependency, and a
downstream acceptance authority. Security, privacy, records, accessibility,
bilingual, data, financial, and audit obligations are embedded as first-class
planning obligations. These records are held in REG-1001 as control-enablement
slices.

## 7. Boundary

No control-enablement work package authorizes implementation. All records carry the
not-implemented, documentary-plan-only, and not-committed posture and are bound to
a future authorization gate. Fail-closed policy and configuration is planned as an
obligation; no policy, secret, or credential is created or configured by this
package.
