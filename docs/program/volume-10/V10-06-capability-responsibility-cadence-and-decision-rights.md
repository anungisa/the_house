## 1. Purpose

This chapter defines how delivery capability, responsibility, cadence, and
decision rights are planned and governed. It is a planning definition; it engages,
assigns, hires, and commits no one.

## 2. Capability model

Delivery capabilities are recorded in REG-1001 as capability records. Each
capability names its capability domain, its responsible role, its decision
rights, its accountabilities, its required competence, its independence
requirement, its capacity assumption, its availability status, its
conflict-of-interest constraint, and its escalation route. Capability planning is
distinct from resourcing: naming a capability does not assign, hire, or engage a
person.

## 3. Resource modelling boundary

Resource modelling is not staff assignment, hiring, or engagement authority. A
capacity assumption is a planning estimate, not a commitment. No individual is
assigned, no role is hired, and no provider is engaged by any record in this
package. Resourcing decisions are governed and are made at a future gate.

## 4. Decision rights

Decision rights are recorded per capability so that it is clear who may make which
governed decision. Decision rights are bounded: planning-level decision rights do
not include authority to construct, provision, procure, or release. Independence
is preserved: a role that authors an artifact, an estimate, or a readiness
recommendation may not independently approve it for execution, and assurance may
not assess artifacts it authored.

## 5. Cadence

Delivery cadence — planning reviews, readiness checkpoints, and gate reviews — is
defined at planning level. Cadence definitions schedule governance activity; they
commit no delivery date and authorize no execution.
