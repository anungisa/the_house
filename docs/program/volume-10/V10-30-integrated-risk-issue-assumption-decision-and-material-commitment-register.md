## 1. Purpose

This chapter provides one whole-volume convergence view of the planning backlog for
the affiliation vertical. It records unresolved items; it resolves none by fiat.

## 2. Convergence scope

The convergence view (register REG-1004) covers assumptions, dependencies, risks,
issues, blockers, decisions, material commitments, estimates, funding
dependencies, procurement dependencies, capacity constraints, policy dependencies,
provider dependencies, and operational dependencies.

## 3. Per-record attributes

Every active record identifies its historical source, current owner, affected work
packages, affected wave, required evidence, required decision, impact if
unresolved, mitigation or fallback, target decision window, current disposition,
and future gate. No unresolved item points to a completed Volume 10 gate; active
items point to a valid future gate (V11-G1, V11-G2, V12-G1, V12-G2, or EXEC-MCG).

## 4. Embedded cross-cutting obligations

**Security, privacy, records, accessibility, bilingual, financial, data, and audit
obligations remain embedded** in every affected work package, wave, and
operational capability. They are not deferred to a later volume and are not treated
as optional; each remains a first-class planning obligation with its own evidence
requirement.

## 5. Material-commitment posture

Material commitments remain explicit and carry `commitment_status: NOT_COMMITTED`
unless a recorded evidence obligation is satisfied. A named commitment is not an
approved commitment.
