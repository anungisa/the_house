## 1. Purpose

This chapter defines how delivery risk, issues, defects, exceptions, waivers,
evidence gaps, quality, and escalation are governed during delivery. It defines
control flows; it executes nothing.

## 2. Risk and issue governance

Delivery risks and issues are recorded in the planning backlog (REG-1004). Each
names its owner, its current status, its impact if unresolved, the required
action or evidence that resolves it, and the future blocking gate at which it must
be resolved. Risks additionally record likelihood and escalation thresholds.

## 3. Defect, exception, waiver, and evidence-gap flows

Defects, exceptions, waivers, and evidence gaps are governed as controlled flows.
A defect is remediated and retested; an exception or waiver is a governed,
authority-bearing, time-bounded decision; an evidence gap blocks advancement until
the required evidence is produced. These flows are defined here at planning level;
no defect is created and no waiver is granted by this package.

## 4. Quality obligations

Delivery quality obligations inherit from the released Volume 9 quality and
master-test definition. Quality is evidence-bearing: a claim of quality is
supported only by the reproducible, provenance-bearing evidence the relevant gate
requires.

## 5. Escalation

Unresolved risks, issues, blockers, and evidence gaps follow a defined escalation
route to the accountable and executive authorities. Escalation thresholds are
recorded so that escalation is deterministic, not discretionary. Escalation is a
governance flow; it authorizes no execution.
