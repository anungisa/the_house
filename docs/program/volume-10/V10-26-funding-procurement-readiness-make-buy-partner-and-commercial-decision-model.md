## 1. Purpose

This chapter defines procurement and partnership **readiness**, not selection, for
the affiliation vertical. No provider is engaged, selected, or awarded, and no
expenditure is authorized.

## 2. Readiness contexts

Readiness (register REG-1004, kinds `FUNDING` and `PROCUREMENT`) is defined for
internal development, specialist augmentation, infrastructure and cloud services,
security and privacy assurance, accessibility and bilingual validation, payment and
accounting integration, identity services, communication services, migration
support, independent assurance, and operational support.

## 3. Per-context attributes

For every potential procurement or partnership context the model records the
capability need, the reason internal capacity may be insufficient, the required
outcome, minimum control requirements, data and authority boundary, commercial
information required, market-validation status, procurement-route status,
evaluation criteria, exit and portability requirements, decision authority,
decision gate, and commitment status.

## 4. Required distinctions

- **Procurement readiness is not procurement authorization.**
- A provider category is not a selected provider.
- Evaluation criteria are not an award decision.
- A commercial estimate is not a negotiated contract.

Every funding and procurement record carries `commitment_status: NOT_COMMITTED`.
