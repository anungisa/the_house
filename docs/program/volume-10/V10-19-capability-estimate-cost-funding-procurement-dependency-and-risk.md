## 1. Purpose

This chapter records the planning-level capability requirements, effort and cost
estimates, funding and procurement-readiness considerations, dependencies, and
risks for the club-affiliation vertical. Every entry is a planning estimate or a
documentary disposition. No budget, quote, procurement, contract, or staffing
commitment is created by this package.

## 2. Capability requirements

Capability requirements identify the capability type, the responsibility, and the
future gate at which staffing may be considered. A capability requirement is not a
staffing assignment and is not an engagement; a role identified is not a person
assigned and is not a capacity confirmed. Capability requirements are recorded in
REG-1004 as assumptions and dependencies and referenced by the delivery model.

## 3. Estimates

Every estimate identifies its basis, its range, its confidence, its assumptions,
its included scope, its excluded scope, its dependencies, its source date, its
currency, and its approval status. Estimates are recorded in REG-1004 as
cost-estimate records with the planning-estimate status.

An estimate is not a budget, a quote, a procurement, or a contract. No estimate is
represented as an approved commitment, and no target date is represented as an
approved schedule.

## 4. Funding and procurement readiness

Funding and procurement-readiness considerations are recorded as documentary
dispositions only. Procurement readiness identifies what a later,
authorization-gated package would need to evaluate; it selects no vendor, opens no
procurement, and commits no expenditure.

## 5. Dependencies and risks

Dependencies and risks are recorded in REG-1004 with an owner, a current status, an
impact if unresolved, a required action or evidence, a fallback disposition, and a
valid future blocking gate. Every future blocking gate is a downstream
authorization gate; none is a gate dispositioned in this package.

## 6. Boundary

No capability, estimate, funding, procurement, dependency, or risk record
authorizes implementation, expenditure, procurement, or staffing. All records carry
the not-implemented, documentary-plan-only, not-committed, and planning-estimate
posture and are bound to a future authorization gate.
