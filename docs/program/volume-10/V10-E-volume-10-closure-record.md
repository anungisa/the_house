procurement, release, or deployment.

rollout, deployment, release, or launch are NOT AUTHORIZED. Any expansion beyond
that bounded definitional scope requires a separate, explicit authorization gate.

Documentary completeness is distinct from implementation completeness, operational
effectiveness, and commitment effectiveness. Implementation plan complete is not
implementation authorized. The Volume 10 documentary definition is effective as of
its closure; no implementation, operational, or commitment effective date is
asserted, and Package 3 commits no schedule, cost, staffing, or delivery date.
