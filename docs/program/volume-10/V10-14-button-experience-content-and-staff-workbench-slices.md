## 1. Purpose

This chapter translates the frozen Volume 7 experience definition into documentary
client-delivery slices for the club-affiliation vertical. Each slice is a plan; no
slice authorizes construction or execution.

## 2. Experience slices

Delivery slices are defined for: representative onboarding; club and organization
selection; affiliation initiation; requirement completion; evidence handling;
progress and completeness; consequential-action confirmation; submission receipt;
return for information; correction and resubmission; decision and standing;
financial and reconciliation visibility; activation confirmation; support and
recovery; and staff review, finance, support, privacy, and administrative
workbenches. These slices are recorded in REG-1001 as experience-delivery-slice
records.

## 3. Per-slice obligations

For each experience slice, the delivery model defines: the experience outcome; the
relevant Volume 7 action; the House command or query dependency; the
authorization-context dependency; the states and error states; the loading and
degraded states; the accessibility obligations; the English and French content
dependencies; the privacy and disclosure limits; the telemetry requirement; the
required functional evidence; the required usability evidence; and the required
acceptance evidence.

## 4. Governed-consumer principle

The Button remains a governed consumer of House contracts. Every Button slice
depends on a House command or query and on an authorization context; no Button
slice performs an independent institutional mutation. A Button interaction is not
an independent institutional mutation, and a provider acknowledgement surfaced in
the Button is not a Curling Canada determination.

## 5. Accessibility and bilingual obligations

Each slice carries accessibility obligations and English and French content
dependencies as first-class planning obligations, not as later additions.
Accessibility and bilingual evidence are required for acceptance and are planned
as verification obligations in V10-18.

## 6. Boundary

No experience slice authorizes implementation. All slice records carry the
not-implemented, documentary-plan-only, and not-committed posture and are bound to
a future authorization gate that has not been dispositioned as passed for
implementation.
