## 1. Purpose

This chapter consolidates the migration and data-readiness roadmap for the
affiliation vertical. It is a plan. No data is extracted, mapped, migrated,
reconciled, cut over, rolled back, accepted, or retired by this package.

## 2. Migration scope

The roadmap covers source inventories, source authority, extraction dependencies,
migration mappings, identity candidates, duplicate and unresolved candidates,
**quarantine**, evidence-reference migration, **coexistence**, **reconciliation**,
rehearsal, cutover, **rollback**, business acceptance, and source retirement.

## 3. Per-stage attributes

For every migration stage the roadmap records source, authority status, target
capability, mapping status, uncertainty, required rehearsal, data-quality
threshold dependency, reconciliation method, rollback dependency, acceptance
authority, operational dependency, evidence required, and future gate.

## 4. Required distinctions

- **Mapping complete is not identity resolved.**
- Rehearsal complete is not migration authorized.
- Cutover complete is not business acceptance.
- Business acceptance is not source retirement.

Migration work planned is not migration executed is not business acceptance is not
source retirement.
